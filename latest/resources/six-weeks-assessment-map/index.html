    Six-Weeks Assessment Map

    Status: Approved curriculum placement; Canvas assignment-group setup and SIS verification still required.

    This is the coursewide source of truth for graded work. A polished weekly product is not automatically a grade. Only the assessments listed below receive points. Everything else remains formative, practice, enrichment, or a teacher-approved recovery/replacement artifact.

    Gradebook Structure

    • Minor Assessments: 40% — three equally weighted entries per six weeks.
    • Major Assessments: 60% — two equally weighted entries per six weeks.
    • Set every listed Canvas Assignment to 100 gradebook points after converting its student-visible rubric score to a percentage. This keeps a 12-point rubric from counting less than a 16-point rubric simply because the raw scales differ.
    • Keep practice Quizzes, daily tickets, discussions, platform clicks, peer feedback, and optional enrichment at 0 points / ungraded.
    • Do not publish a graded Assignment until its rubric, answer guidance, absence route, and correct Minor or Major assignment group are attached.

    1st Six Weeks

    Category Placement Gradebook entry Scoring tool Boundary
    Minor 1 Week 0 My Career Journey Reflection 12-point Career Journey rubric Score self-evidence and reasoning, not assessment result or platform access.
    Minor 2 Week 2 IT Salary Comparison and Career-Fit Reflection 20-point salary/career-fit rubric Use labeled sources and measures; Code.org completion is not scored.
    Minor 3 Week 4 Help Desk Program Evidence and Career Connection 16-point Help Desk Evidence rubric Live demo and hardware success are practice, not the only evidence.
    Major 1 Week 3 App Design and Emerging-Career Evidence Packet 16-point App Design rubric Score the individual packet; the pitch is communication practice.
    Major 2 Week 5 Cybersecurity Capstone Evidence Portfolio 16-point Capstone rubric Paper/digital fabrication routes are equal; Xello and H&L clicks are not scored.

    Formative only: Week 1 Robots for Crayons team presentation and daily tickets. Team participation may feed feedback, but it is not an individual grade.

    2nd Six Weeks

    Category Placement Gradebook entry Scoring tool Boundary
    Minor 1 Week 3 Nursing Route and Handoff 16-point route/handoff rubric Physical measurement and public speaking are not required for full credit.
    Minor 2 Week 4 Health Career Evidence Check 16-point career-evidence rubric Fixed evidence route is equal to live-platform browsing.
    Minor 3 Week 5 Communication and Goal Synthesis 16-point communication/goal rubric Private goals and assessment results remain private.
    Major 1 Week 1 Legal Policy Position Evidence 16-point legal-evidence rubric Assigned-side speaking and platform access are not scored.
    Major 2 Week 2 Patient Care Report and Complication Plan 16-point patient-care evidence rubric Score fictional evidence and reasoning, not touch, physical technique, or performance.

    Formative/recovery only: Week 6 Biomedical synthesis and conferences. It does not create a fourth minor or third major.

    3rd Six Weeks

    Category Placement Gradebook entry Scoring tool Boundary
    Minor 1 Week 1 Veterinary Pathway Evidence Packet 16-point veterinary evidence rubric Xello clicks and triage performance are not separate grades.
    Minor 2 Week 4 Hospitality Career and Business Recommendation 16-point recommendation rubric Score the individual recommendation, not platform choice or art polish.
    Minor 3 Week 5 Cosmetology Career and Business Recommendation 16-point recommendation rubric Fabrication method and public performance are not scored.
    Major 1 Week 2 Farm-to-Table and Emerging Plant-Tech Evidence 16-point Plant Science rubric Paper, Canva, and Adobe Express routes are equal.
    Major 2 Week 3 Sustainable Engineering Design and Trends Evidence 16-point Sustainable Engineering rubric Score evidence, revision, and tradeoff reasoning—not drawing skill.

    Formative/recovery only: Week 6 Entrepreneurship Portfolio. It may replace a missing major only through a teacher-approved reassessment decision; it is not a third automatic major.

    4th Six Weeks

    Category Placement Gradebook entry Scoring tool Boundary
    Minor 1 Week 3 Aviation Route and Action Plan 16-point aviation rubric LEGO, acting, and platform clicks are not scored.
    Minor 2 Week 4 Drone Systems Evidence Brief 16-point drone evidence rubric No grade depends on piloting, flight success, or hardware availability.
    Minor 3 Week 5 Automotive Evidence Brief 16-point automotive rubric Score source use and reasoning, not vehicle access or public speaking.
    Major 1 Week 1 Mid-Year Career Blueprint 16-point blueprint rubric Missing old profile evidence uses the documented recovery strip.
    Major 2 Week 2 Individual High School and Career Plan 16-point plan rubric Unverified pathway details become counselor questions, not invented facts.

    Formative/recovery only: Week 6 Trades Capstone reflection. It may replace missing evidence after teacher review but does not create another grade by default.

    5th Six Weeks

    Category Placement Gradebook entry Scoring tool Boundary
    Minor 1 Week 1 Three-Career Architecture Comparison 16-point Three-Career Architecture Comparison rubric The design portfolio remains formative; salary measures stay labeled.
    Minor 2 Week 2 Assessment and Emerging-Specialty Evidence 16-point Assessment and Emerging-Specialty Evidence rubric Bridge fabrication and team ranking are not scored.
    Minor 3 Week 3 Construction Labor-Evidence Classification 16-point Construction Labor-Evidence Classification rubric Use the fixed dated dataset; H&L state is not evidence.
    Major 1 Week 4 Skilled-Trades Classification and Individual Response 16-point Skilled Trades Evidence rubric Group plan may support the work, but every criterion needs individual evidence.
    Major 2 Week 5 Personal Budget Evidence Portfolio 16-point Budget Portfolio rubric Use fictional/planning scenarios; never score family income or lifestyle choice.

    Formative/recovery only: Week 6 Real Estate Evidence Brief and the ungraded design/build work from Weeks 1–3. Week 6 may replace missing major evidence after teacher review, not add a third major.

    6th Six Weeks

    Category Placement Gradebook entry Scoring tool Boundary
    Minor 1 Week 1 Education Evidence Portfolio 16-point Education Portfolio rubric Service disclosure and artistic polish are not scored.
    Minor 2 Week 2 Resume, Revision, and Job-Search Evidence 16-point Resume and Design rubric Use fictional/redacted information; design polish and personal work history are not scored.
    Minor 3 Week 3 Ethical Marketing Evidence Brief 16-point Marketing Evidence rubric No real post, sale, testimonial, contact form, or data collection.
    Major 1 Week 5 Job Skills, Application, and Mock Interview Portfolio 24-point Job Skills rubric Private live, recorded, conference, AAC, and documented alternative routes are supported.
    Major 2 Week 6 Individual Career Plan and Communicated Capstone 24-point Capstone rubric Missing old artifacts use the recovery inventory; public presentation is not required.

    Formative only: Week 4 Career Oral Evidence is the structured rehearsal for the Week 5 and Week 6 majors. It receives feedback and revision time without becoming a fourth minor or third major.

    Performance Bands and Entry Rule

    Convert each rubric total to the published percentage table on that scoring tool, then enter the percentage as the score out of 100 in Canvas:

    • Masters: 90–100
    • Meets: 80–89
    • Approaches: 70–79
    • Needs Improvement: 60–69
    • Below 60: follow campus policy for insufficient evidence, reassessment, or recovery.

    Do not average rubric levels and silently treat a level 3 as 75 unless the scoring tool explicitly publishes that conversion. Students must see the rubric and conversion before the graded task.

    Canvas Setup Gate

    Before any mapped assessment is published:

    1. Create or verify Minor Assessments at 40% and Major Assessments at 60%.
    2. Put only the five mapped assessments for that six weeks into those groups.
    3. Set each mapped Assignment to 100 points and attach the correct rubric.
    4. Keep all other interactions ungraded unless a teacher intentionally uses the documented recovery/replacement route.
    5. Test one student-view submission route and one absence/platform fallback.
    6. Confirm that hidden teacher keys and licensed files remain unavailable to students unless intentionally released.
