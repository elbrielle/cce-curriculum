## Target and Evidence

**TEKS:** d(1)(B), d(1)(C)
**Evidence:** five-page Community Classroom Learning-Space Plan

Students describe how Education and Training workers help people learn, then design a third-grade science learning space whose choices support specific learning goals.

## Before Class

- Open the Canvas student guide and licensed FYF pp. 213-215.
- Post the Community Classroom packet and private annotation Assignment.
- Decide whether students draw digitally or on paper. No poster board is required.

## 50-Minute Flow

1. **Warm-up and cluster description — 5 min.** What did a teacher, coach, tutor, trainer, librarian, or museum educator help you learn?
2. **Licensed scenario and model — 10 min.** Read FYF pp. 213-214. Model one design choice that connects to a science goal; decoration alone is not evidence.
3. **Plan and draw — 22 min.** Students complete the goal, career map, sequence, access/safety response, and full-page labeled drawing.
4. **Booking card and feedback — 8 min.** Students write for a teacher deciding whether to reserve the space. Peer feedback is optional; teacher conference or self-check is equal.
5. **Exit — 5 min.** Name one Education career, its contribution, one design choice, and the learning goal it supports.

## Monitor and Key

Accept many designs. Full evidence includes a clear third-grade science goal, a sequence children actually perform, two Education careers with distinct contributions, a labeled space, an access or safety response, and a persuasive explanation tied to learning. Trim polish before reasoning.

## Access, Absence, and Platform Route

The packet contains the full task. Students may type, draw, dictate, or describe the layout orally to a scribe. H&L is optional exploration only. If images load slowly, use the visible text summary and packet; the student is not blocked.
