## Target and Evidence

**TEKS:** d(1)(C), d(4)(E)
**Evidence:** FYF activity plus a two-page individual revision and service analysis

Students design and revise a classroom-safe activity that practices gross and fine motor skills, then explain how service benefits a community and develops transferable skills.

## Before Class

- Open licensed FYF pp. 216-217 and post the two-page individual-evidence companion and private annotation Assignment.
- Default: one device and one FYF workbook per student, one projector, zero prints. Paper route: one two-page companion per student and one collection tray.
- Optional physical test, per pair: one pair of teacher-approved scissors and two scrap sheets. A tabletop walk-through, teacher conference, or individual simulation is equal.
- Do not require disclosure of personal volunteer history.

## 50-Minute Flow

1. **Warm-up and model — 5 min.** Demonstrate gross versus fine motor without grading physical ability.
2. **Read the brief — 6 min.** Establish safety, access alternatives, and child-friendly language.
3. **Create once in FYF — 17 min.** Teams write the steps and map the activity once in the workbook; the companion does not repeat the plan.
4. **Test and revise — 10 min.** Partner, tabletop, teacher-conference, or individual simulation route.
5. **Service analysis — 7 min.** Real, planned, or supplied tutoring scenario; connect one skill across two careers.
6. **Submit and cleanup — 5 min.** Students submit the two-page individual evidence and return scissors, scrap, and devices.

## Monitor and Key

- **Minute 11:** the FYF activity includes a gross-motor target and a fine-motor or access-equivalent target. If one-third treats access as an afterthought, project the supplied picture/texture-cue model.
- **Minute 27:** every team has one testable FYF activity; students can name the action, materials, and one safety/access support without copying the full plan.
- **Minute 38:** each student's two revisions name test evidence and explain why the change helps.
- **Minute 45:** service analysis names a community benefit and one skill used in two careers. Redirect personal disclosure to the supplied library-tutoring scenario.
- Trim sharing first. Protect the two revisions, service transfer, submission, and material cleanup. Do not score physical performance, cutting skill, disability, artistry, disclosure, or partner availability.

## Access, Absence, and Platform Route

The locked FYF images provide the brief. If the workbook or team is unavailable, students use the complete teacher-supplied activity scenario, individual simulation, and supplied library-tutoring scenario to answer the same two-page evidence questions. Speech-to-text, enlarged print, pre-cut materials, adapted scissors, a seated/supported route, and teacher scribing are valid supports.
