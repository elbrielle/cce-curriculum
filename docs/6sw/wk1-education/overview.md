**6th Six Weeks** · **5 class periods (50 minutes each)**

## Current Evidence Boundaries

- Current TEA guidance describes five common requirements for most Texas classroom-teacher certificates: accredited bachelor's degree, approved educator preparation program, appropriate exams, state application, and fingerprinting/background review. Specific certificates and preparation routes vary.
- “Traditional” and “alternative” are not complete price, time, paid-status, or quality labels. Students compare verified statewide requirements and identify what must be checked with an approved provider.
- Educational Aide I has specific age, course, grade, credit, district-verification, application, and background-review conditions. A pathway name does not guarantee a credential.
- Current Irving public evidence names **Education and Training** at Irving High, MacArthur, and Nimitz, and **Early Childhood Education** at Cardwell. The public page does not by itself guarantee schedule, travel, admission, placement, or certification.
- Supplied job-posting cards are fictional practice records based on common employer fields. They teach how to read evidence without depending on a changing job board or exposing an assessment to search variance.
- H&L browsing, Xello **Discover learning pathways**, and eDynamic 7.2 may be used as supplemental extensions. No new required Grade 8 Xello completion task belongs here, and no platform click is graded.

## Assessment and Grading

Days 1-4 create visible formative evidence. Day 5 is **Minor 1** in the [6SW assessment map](../../resources/six-weeks-assessment-map.md). Keep it unpublished until the Canvas review gate passes. The rubric uses Masters, Meets, Approaches, Needs Improvement, and Insufficient Evidence. Preference, artistic polish, handwriting, English mechanics, platform access, and willingness to disclose personal service are not scored.

## Access and Absence Route

Every required direction and source is visible in Canvas. Students may annotate, type labeled responses, upload a document, dictate, use enlarged print, or complete paper. Partner work has an individual or teacher-conference route. Required multi-sentence responses have full-width writing space; tables hold only labels or short comparisons. Licensed imagery remains in locked Canvas and is not required to understand the directions.
