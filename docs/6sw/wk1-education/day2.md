## Target and Evidence

**TEKS:** d(2)(A), d(2)(B)
**Evidence:** three-page Texas Education Career Routes comparison and evidence-based recommendation

Students separate statewide certification requirements from provider-specific details and compare a university-based route with a post-baccalaureate or alternative route.

## Before Class

- Open the current TEA teacher and Educational Aide pages.
- Default: one device per student, one projector, zero prints. Post the three-page route guide and private annotation Assignment.
- Paper route: one three-page guide per student and one collection tray. Students use either paper or Canvas, not both.
- Do not present a named preparation provider's cost, length, paid status, or quality without current provider evidence.

## 50-Minute Flow

1. **Warm-up — 5 min.** Which facts would you need before choosing a preparation program?
2. **Five common requirements — 10 min.** Model degree, approved EPP, exams, application, and fingerprinting/background review. Note CTE exceptions.
3. **Two route records — 14 min.** Students distinguish what is verified statewide from what varies by provider.
4. **Educational Aide I + Irving boundary — 8 min.** Students record two conditions and one counselor/CTE question.
5. **Fictional Jordan decision — 8 min.** Recommend what Jordan should compare; do not score route preference.
6. **Exit — 5 min.** One statewide requirement, one provider-variable fact, and one question before enrollment.

## Monitor and Key

- **Minute 13:** students can name two common requirements and mark one provider-variable field. If one-third treats a route label as proof, sort four statements together.
- **Minute 29:** Educational Aide evidence includes age 18+, course/credit/grade conditions, superintendent verification, and the application/background-review boundary without promising certification.
- **Minute 42:** Jordan's response names three exact provider facts. Accept “cannot decide yet” when the missing evidence is named.
- Trim sharing first. Protect the statewide/provider distinction, Educational Aide boundary, recommendation, submission, and cleanup.

## Access, Absence, and Platform Route

All required evidence is in the packet. Browser research, H&L, Xello, eDynamic, applications, provider contact forms, personal data, and payment are not required.
