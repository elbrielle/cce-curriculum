# Day 3: Xello Discover Learning Pathways
<!-- CCE DAILY CONTRACT START -->
## Daily Learning Contract

- **Topic:** Education Pathways
- **Objective:** Students will compare learning pathways to three careers and explain which pathway they would investigate first.
- **TEKS:** d(2)(A), d(2)(B)
- **Demonstration of Learning:** completed Xello Discover learning pathways lesson plus one evidence-based pathway comparison.
<!-- CCE DAILY CONTRACT END -->
## Target and Evidence

**TEKS:** d(2)(A), d(2)(B)
**Evidence:** Xello lesson completion plus one pathway comparison

Students investigate university, community college, technical school, apprenticeship, straight-to-work, military, and other pathways without treating one route as correct for every career.

## Before Class

- Provide one device per student and test **ClassLink > Xello > Home > Lessons > Discover learning pathways** in a demo account.
- Open the Completion Standards report to the Grade 7 **Discover learning pathways** task.
- Check the actual prerequisite: the lesson requires at least three saved careers. Students missing the prerequisite save three careers during the first work block; do not assign or report a separate Save careers completion task.
- Post the two-page support sheet. Page 1 supports the lesson; page 2 is the fixed no-device learning route and does not count as Xello completion.
- Prepare a private status list: ready, prerequisite missing, access issue, absent, or complete.

## 50-Minute Flow

1. **Warm-up and pathway preview — 5 min.** Ask, “Can two people reach the same career through different learning pathways? Give one possible example.”
2. **Prerequisite check — 10 min.** Open the lesson. Students with fewer than three saved careers save three careers they are willing to compare. A peer may point but does not control another account.
3. **Discover learning pathways — 30 min.** Students complete the assigned lesson and compare pathways connected to their saved careers. Keep the point-of-use word bank visible.
4. **Report check and exit — 5 min.** Verify lesson completion or record supervised recovery. Students state one career, one possible pathway, one benefit, and one question.

## Monitor and Key

- **Minute 8:** every student is inside the assigned lesson or has a named prerequisite/access barrier.
- **Minute 15:** each student can access three saved careers. If several students are missing the prerequisite, pause and model one career save without ranking or sharing choices.
- **Minute 30:** students are comparing pathways, not only naming a preferred career.
- **Minute 45:** lesson completion is visible in Xello or a supervised recovery need is recorded.
- A complete comparison names the career, pathway, reason, and one question or limitation. Do not treat university as automatically better than apprenticeship, community college, technical school, straight-to-work, or another appropriate route.

## Access, Absence, and Platform Route

If Xello is unavailable, students complete the fixed pathway comparison on page 2. That work supports the TEKS and feeds the Day 5 portfolio, but it does not create Xello completion. Schedule the required lesson in a supervised recovery window. Do not require profile screenshots or public disclosure of saved careers.
