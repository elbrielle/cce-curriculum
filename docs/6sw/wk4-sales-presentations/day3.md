# Day 3: BrainBoost Rescue and Career Oral Outline
<!-- CCE DAILY CONTRACT START -->
## Daily Learning Contract

- **Topic:** Problem Solving
- **Objective:** Students will identify how a sales, marketing, or design career uses evidence to define a campaign problem, screen solutions, and organize a bounded career brief.
- **TEKS:** d(1)(C), d(4)(B)
- **Demonstration of Learning:** Completed FYF BrainBoost work plus a two-page individual cause statement, evidence-linked decision, fixed-source career outline, and cross-career problem-solving connection.
<!-- CCE DAILY CONTRACT END -->
**TEKS:** d(1)(C), d(4)(B)
**Evidence:** FYF BrainBoost work plus individual cause/evidence, career outline, and transfer evidence

## Before Class

Have students open FYF pp. 244-247 and post the two-page companion as Canvas-first with print fallback. Sticky notes are optional, and the companion keeps each student's reasoning visible. The three fixed career cards remove any prior-research or login dependency.

## 50-Minute Flow

1. **Problem-versus-result warm-up - 5 min.** Low sales are a result; unclear value is a possible cause supported by supplied feedback.
2. **Analyze evidence - 8 min.** Review FYF pp. 244-245: message, visuals, audience, and three customer comments.
3. **Generate/screen solutions - 10 min.** Use FYF p. 246; reject invented health, nutrition, discount, scarcity, popularity, or data claims.
4. **Build the rescue - 10 min.** Complete FYF p. 247 and record the individual cause/evidence decision in the companion.
5. **Select career evidence - 7 min.** Prior verified evidence or fixed BLS fallback; keep measure/date/geography visible.
6. **Outline oral brief - 7 min.** Opening, duty, preparation, labor evidence, bounded conclusion.
7. **Exit - 3 min.** Name one transfer from campaign problem solving to the selected career.

## Key and Trim Point

BrainBoost reached its stated audience; the problem is weak differentiation/need/message, not audience targeting. Multiple solutions work when grounded and bounded. Fixed fallback BLS cards are U.S. medians/projections, not DFW starting pay. Trim the campaign sketch first; preserve individual career evidence.

## Absence and Access

The locked FYF images plus the two-page companion are the complete no-workbook route. Students may work independently; no partner, H&L, prior portfolio, or open-web search is required.
