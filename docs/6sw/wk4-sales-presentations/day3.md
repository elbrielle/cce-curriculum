# Day 3: BrainBoost Rescue and Career Oral Outline

**TEKS:** d(1)(C), d(4)(B), d(4)(C)
**Evidence:** seven-page individual rescue plan and career-brief outline

## Before Class

Open locked FYF pp. 244-247 and post the private annotation Assignment. Sticky notes and team-only evidence are not required. Prepare the three fixed fallback career cards for students missing prior research.

## 50-Minute Flow

1. **Problem-versus-result warm-up - 5 min.** Low sales are a result; unclear value is a possible cause supported by supplied feedback.
2. **Analyze evidence - 8 min.** Review message, visuals, audience, and three customer comments.
3. **Generate/screen solutions - 10 min.** At least six ideas; reject invented health, nutrition, discount, scarcity, popularity, or data claims.
4. **Build the rescue - 10 min.** Three evidence-linked solutions and one revised concept.
5. **Select career evidence - 7 min.** Prior verified evidence or fixed BLS fallback; keep measure/date/geography visible.
6. **Outline oral brief - 7 min.** Opening, duty, preparation, labor evidence, bounded conclusion.
7. **Exit - 3 min.** Name one transfer from campaign problem solving to the selected career.

## Key and Trim Point

BrainBoost reached its stated audience; the problem is weak differentiation/need/message, not audience targeting. Multiple solutions work when grounded and bounded. Fixed fallback BLS cards are U.S. medians/projections, not DFW starting pay. Trim the campaign sketch first; preserve individual career evidence.

## Absence and Access

The packet contains the campaign facts and fallback cards. Students may work independently; no partner, H&L, prior portfolio, or open-web search is required.
