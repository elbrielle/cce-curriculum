# Day 2: Deliver, Test, and Revise a Sales Pitch

## Daily Learning Contract

- **Topic:** Oral Revision
- **Objective:** Students will communicate an audience-specific pitch twice, use feedback to make one visible revision, and explain how the communication skill transfers to a second career.
- **TEKS:** d(4)(B)
- **Demonstration of Learning:** Two timed oral/AAC pitch attempts, one specific feedback point, a visible before/after revision, and a two-career transfer response.

**Evidence:** two timed oral/AAC attempts, specific feedback, visible revision, and two-career transfer

## Before Class

Per student, have the Day 1 pitch, a pencil, and one two-page delivery record by private Canvas annotation or print. One timer serves the class. Set live partner, small-group, teacher-conference, recorded audio/video, and AAC routes before class. Project this before-feedback-after model: **Before:** “Sign up now.” **Feedback:** the next step is vague. **After:** “Compare the two fictional session options and circle the one that fits your schedule.” **Effect:** the audience knows the safe next action. Recorded students upload the written record and private audio/video together. Live, conference, and AAC students submit the written record while the teacher records student, date, route, both attempt times, feedback/revision, and oral/AAC completion. Do not make partner attendance or camera use a requirement.

## 50-Minute Flow

1. **Delivery model - 5 min.** Demonstrate understandable pace, organization, and a safe call to action.
2. **Silent accuracy check - 5 min.** Students remove unsupported urgency, guarantees, health, popularity, or income claims.
3. **First delivery - 10 min.** Record route, time, and evidence.
4. **Feedback - 8 min.** Partner, teacher, self-recording, transcript, or checklist identifies one strength and one specific revision.
5. **Revise - 10 min.** Preserve before/after language and reason.
6. **Second delivery - 7 min.** Apply the revision and record time/effect.
7. **Exit - 5 min.** Name transferable communication evidence and next goal.

## Key and Trim Point

At minute 15, check Attempt 1 time and one exact strength. If one-third give trait feedback, return to the before-feedback-after model. At minute 31, require visible before/after language and a reason tied to audience need. At minute 44, check Attempt 2 time, effect, and transfer. Do not score accent, eye contact, camera use, confidence, or memorization. Trim class share-outs first; preserve the second attempt and visible revision. Collect one record.

## Absence and Access

Students may complete a teacher conference, private recording, or AAC attempt later. A written draft alone is not mislabeled oral evidence unless an accommodation changes the task.
