## Before Class

Open locked FYF pp. 241-242, post the private annotation Assignment, and choose a fictional teacher model. H&L, a real product, a public pitch, and printing are not required.

## 50-Minute Flow

1. **Persuasion warm-up - 5 min.** Identify a hook, reason/benefit, and request.
2. **Read SparkClean - 8 min.** Label hook, offer, benefit, and call to action; flag any real-world claim that would need verification.
3. **Define offer and audience - 10 min.** Use a fictional product/service and distinguish audience evidence from assumption.
4. **Plan four parts - 12 min.** Draft concise accurate language.
5. **Write the full pitch - 10 min.** Four to six short sentences; accuracy over hype.
6. **Exit - 5 min.** Name one transferable skill and one sales/marketing career work product.

## Key and Trim Point

Require all four pitch parts, an audience situation, benefit rather than feature list, one assumption limit, ethical boundary, and a Day 2 oral route. Trim optional design or partner sharing first.

## Absence and Access

The packet is complete. Students may type, dictate, annotate, enlarge, or use paper. The Day 2 route may be live, conference, recording, or AAC.
