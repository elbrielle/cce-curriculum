# Day 2: Write a First Resume

**TEKS:** d(7)(A)
**Evidence:** six-page First Resume Draft and private Canvas submission

## Before Class

Post the packet, a fictional middle-school model, and the private upload/annotation Assignment. Optional Xello access must not replace the Canvas/paper route.

## 50-Minute Flow

1. **Purpose and privacy — 7 min.** A résumé matches true evidence to a target; it is not a personal-data form.
2. **Model standard sections — 10 min.** Header/target, Education, Skills, and Experience/Projects/Activities/Service. References and photos stay off.
3. **Draft evidence — 23 min.** Students use real projects, classes, responsibilities, activities, or the supplied scenario. Paid work is not required.
4. **Assembly check — 7 min.** Standard headings, action words, specific evidence, consistency, readable formatting, no sensitive data.
5. **Exit — 3 min.** Strongest bullet and one needed revision.

## Monitor and Key

Do not reward invented titles, dates, awards, hours, results, or tools. “No paid experience” is not a deficit when projects and responsibilities provide evidence. CareerOneStop recommends standard headings and relevant true evidence; graphic decoration cannot rescue weak content.

## Privacy and Absence Route

No home address, personal phone/email, date of birth, student ID, SSN, photo, family information, or reference contact. Students never exchange accounts or devices.
