# Day 1: Arts/AV Cluster + Behind the Microphone

**TEKS:** d(1)(C)
**Evidence:** five-page individual Podcast Production Plan

## Before Class

Open licensed FYF pp. 255, 270-271 and post the private annotation Assignment. Recording, public posting, sticky notes, chart paper, H&L, and a guest are not required.

## 50-Minute Flow

1. **Cluster opener — 7 min.** Describe how Arts/AV workers communicate through sound, image, text, movement, and interaction.
2. **Read the podcast brief — 8 min.** Establish audience, copyright, privacy, and accessibility boundaries.
3. **Plan the episode — 22 min.** Students complete purpose, structure, three interview questions, audio/transcript plan, promotion concept, and closing.
4. **Production-role feedback — 8 min.** Optional partner/teacher/self-review; every student records an individual role and revision.
5. **Exit — 5 min.** Name one production career, its work product, and one planning decision.

## Key and Trim Point

Full evidence includes audience, purpose, structured outline, three questions, original/licensed audio boundary, transcript/caption plan, two careers, individual contribution, revision, and one income/popularity limitation. Trim decorative promotion work first.

## Absence and Access

The packet is complete. Students may type, dictate, or use paper. No platform account or public recording is needed.
