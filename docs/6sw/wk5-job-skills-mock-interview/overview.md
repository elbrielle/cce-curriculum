# Week 5: Job Search, Applications, and Interviews

**6th Six Weeks** | 5 class periods | 50 minutes each

## Weekly Target

Students use one complete fictional job case to screen a posting, tailor business correspondence, complete a sample application, apply reference protocol, prepare for an interview, participate as interviewer and interviewee, revise from feedback, and write a thank-you note.

## TEKS Evidence

- **d(6)(A):** seven-step search and posting-screening evidence, Day 1.
- **d(7)(B):** tailored cover letter and thank-you correspondence, Days 2 and 5.
- **d(7)(C):** complete, accurate sample application, Day 3.
- **d(7)(D):** appropriate reference roles, reasons, permission-before-sharing protocol, Day 3.
- **d(6)(B):** workplace/task/safety/format/access interview-readiness decisions, Day 4.
- **d(6)(C):** assessable interviewer and interviewee evidence through an equivalent private route, Day 5.

## Required Case and Privacy Boundary

The core case is **Pecan Creek Animal Care - Student Office Assistant**, with fictional applicant **Jordan Rivera** and an `example.com` address. Fixed evidence makes the week teachable without open-web searching or access to a real applicant's records.

Students do **not** enter or submit a real address, phone or email, birth date, student/driver ID, Social Security number, banking/tax data, signature, immigration/health/disability/religion information, family data, availability constraints, or another person's contact information. They do not send an application, permission request, cover letter, or thank-you note and do not contact an employer. A current posting or FYF Rung 5 may be a teacher-approved extension after screening; it is not required evidence.

## Teacher Readiness

Post the coordinated Teacher and Student Guides, the six student-visible packets, and the day interaction. Digital annotation, typed labeled responses, upload, dictation, enlarged print, paper, recorded role simulation, teacher conference, and AAC are valid routes as appropriate. Pair work is useful but never the only access route.

Xello Job Interviews, H&L, FYF Rung 5, live job boards, and CareerOneStop pages may extend practice. They are not load-bearing, are not district Xello completion evidence, and do not replace the fixed case.

## Assessment Status

The six-criterion, 24-point rubric is student-visible. Day 5 remains a **Major draft** until the live sixth-six-weeks 40/60 assessment map confirms placement. Performance bands are Masters 90-100, Meets 80-89, Approaches 70-79, and Needs Improvement 60-69; below 60 follows campus policy. Content, accuracy, privacy, relevance, and communicated evidence are scored—not accent, eye contact, camera use, clothing cost/style, disability, public confidence, or English mechanics unless meaning is unclear.

## Current Teacher Sources

- CareerOneStop: job-search, application, cover-letter, references, interview, and follow-up guidance.
- Xello Job Interviews: optional licensed teacher/student extension in authenticated Canvas when available.

## Access and Absence

All required facts and prompts are inside the packets. Students who miss paired practice use a teacher conference, small group, private recording, AAC, or written interviewer-evidence route plus an assessable interviewee route. No family adult, real employer, public Discussion, camera, personal-data disclosure, or platform login is required.
