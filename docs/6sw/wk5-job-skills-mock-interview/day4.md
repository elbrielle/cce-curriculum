## Before Class

Post the packet and practice Quiz. Preassign private routes for students who should not be required to perform publicly or use a camera.

## 50-Minute Flow

1. **Warm-up - 5 min.** Name one controllable interview concern.
2. **Readiness model - 8 min.** Research, timing, materials, workplace/task/safety context, virtual backup, and accommodations.
3. **Question planning - 22 min.** Prepare concise evidence for eight common questions using situation/task, action, result/reflection where helpful.
4. **Interviewer role - 10 min.** Write relevant, lawful, privacy-safe follow-ups and practice neutral evidence notes; complete Quiz feedback.
5. **Exit - 5 min.** State the chosen Day 5 route and one final preparation action.

## Key and Trim Point

Appearance decisions are context-first and access-neutral. Do not teach expensive, gendered, body-based, cultural, religious, or disability-based rules. Eye contact and handshakes are not universal scoring requirements. Strong answer notes are evidence cues, not memorized scripts. Interviewers avoid protected/private topics and do not reward oversharing. Trim optional partner rehearsal first.

## Absence and Access

The packet has one spacious page per two questions and a complete interviewer plan. Accept typing, annotation, dictation, paper, AAC, or a private conference. Xello Job Interviews is supplemental only.
