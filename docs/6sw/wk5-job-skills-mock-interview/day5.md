# Day 5: Mock Interview and Follow-Up
<!-- CCE DAILY CONTRACT START -->
## Daily Learning Contract

- **Topic:** Mock Interview
- **Objective:** Students will describe context-appropriate interview preparation, participate in a mock interview as interviewee and interviewer, revise from feedback, and write appropriate thank-you correspondence.
- **TEKS:** d(6)(B), d(6)(C), d(7)(B)
- **Demonstration of Learning:** Four-page mock-interview and thank-you record plus the six-criterion Major 1 evidence profile.
<!-- CCE DAILY CONTRACT END -->

## Before Class

Post the four-page record, two-page rubric, and private Major 1 Assignment. Keep Days 1-3 practice evidence available where students already submitted or turned it in; students do not upload the same packet again. Confirm every student's interview route and feedback method before class.

## 50-Minute Flow

1. **Ready check - 5 min.** Open the posting, evidence bank, three answer notes, assigned route, and feedback method.
2. **Round 1 - 10 min.** Each student produces assessable interviewee evidence and interviewer evidence through the assigned route.
3. **Feedback and revision - 6 min.** Record one specific strength and one exact change.
4. **Round 2 - 10 min.** Apply the change in a second response or role exchange.
5. **Thank-you note - 9 min.** Draft a brief, specific fictional note. Audit it; do not send it.
6. **Self-score and revision - 7 min.** Use all six rubric criteria and revise the weakest available evidence.
7. **Private submit - 3 min.** Submit the Day 5 record through the authorized route.

## Monitor, Key, and Trim Point

Interviewee evidence is relevant, truthful, organized, responsive, and visibly revised. Interviewer evidence includes listening, neutral notes, and a job-related follow-up. The thank-you note is brief, specific, accurate, courteous, and unsent. Do not score accent, eye contact, handshake, camera use, clothing cost or style, disability, public confidence, paid work history, or memorization. Trim an extra live exchange, not each student's revision or thank-you evidence.

## Major 1 Scoring Route

Score the Day 1 search/posting record, Day 2 cover letter, Day 3 application and reference protocol, and Day 5 interview/thank-you record with the six-criterion rubric. Earlier Canvas submissions or labeled paper remain the evidence source; students do not compile duplicate uploads. Enter the converted percentage as the score out of 100 in `Major Assessments (60%)`.

## Supports and Access

Keep **interviewee/entrevistado**, **interviewer/entrevistador**, **follow-up/seguimiento**, **revision/revisión**, and **thank-you/agradecimiento** beside the record. Use the frame: “I changed **[specific response move]** after **[feedback]**. The change helped because **[effect]**.” Partner absence does not block completion. Use a teacher conference, small group, private recording, AAC, or scheduled make-up. Written interviewer notes may show the interviewer role; the interviewee standard still needs assessable spoken or accommodation-aligned communicated evidence.
