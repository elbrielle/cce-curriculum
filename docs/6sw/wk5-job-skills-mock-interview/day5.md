# Day 5: Mock Interview, Revision, and Thank-You Note

**TEKS:** d(6)(B), d(6)(C), d(7)(B)
**Evidence:** seven-page individual role/revision/thank-you record; private Major draft

## Before Class

Post the packet, rubric, and private media/annotation Assignment. Confirm paired, small-group, teacher-conference, private recorded, and AAC routes before class. No message is sent.

## 50-Minute Flow

1. **Ready check - 5 min.** Open posting/evidence, question plan, access/technology plan, and feedback method.
2. **Round 1 - 12 min.** Students produce assessable interviewer and interviewee evidence, or the authorized equivalent.
3. **Feedback and switch - 5 min.** Record one strength and one precise revision.
4. **Round 2 - 12 min.** Apply the revision in the second role/attempt.
5. **Thank-you and portfolio reflection - 13 min.** Plan and write a brief fictional note; audit it; identify consistency/privacy revision.
6. **Private submit - 3 min.** Submit only the authorized evidence route.

## Key and Trim Point

Interview evidence is relevant, organized, truthful, privacy-safe, responsive, and visibly revised. The interviewer listens, records neutral evidence, and asks a relevant follow-up. The thank-you note is brief, specific, accurate, courteous, and not sent. Do not score accent, eye contact, handshake, camera use, clothing cost/style, disability, public confidence, or memorization. Trim a second live exchange—not individual revision or thank-you evidence.

## Absence and Access

Partner absence does not block completion. Use a teacher conference, small group, recorded role simulation, AAC, or scheduled make-up. Written interviewer notes may demonstrate the interviewer role; the interviewee standard still needs an assessable communicated response through the student's authorized route.
