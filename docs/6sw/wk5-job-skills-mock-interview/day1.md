# Day 1: Job Search and Posting Evidence

**TEKS:** d(6)(A)
**Evidence:** six-page individual search/posting packet

## Before Class

Post the fixed fictional Pecan Creek posting and private annotation Assignment. Do not require a live job board, H&L, Rung 5, a real applicant identity, or employer contact.

## 50-Minute Flow

1. **Warm-up - 5 min.** Put a first-job search in order; surface missing steps.
2. **Seven-step model - 10 min.** Self-assess, find openings, screen, prepare, apply, interview, follow up. Screening happens before sharing data.
3. **Read the fixed posting - 10 min.** Mark duties, required/preferred qualifications, source clues, and privacy boundaries.
4. **Build evidence - 20 min.** Complete match/gap evidence, screening decision, safe next action, tracker, and optional-search plan.
5. **Exit - 5 min.** Name the step most often skipped and the consequence.

## Key and Trim Point

Full evidence includes all seven steps, posting source/role/location, at least two truthful matches, one gap not hidden or invented, scam/privacy screening, and one safe next action. A current posting is extension only. Trim optional search first.

## Absence and Access

The packet contains the complete posting. Students may type, annotate, dictate, or use paper. Never score a platform click, real application, personal data, or contact attempt.
