# Day 1: Job Search and Posting Screen
<!-- CCE DAILY CONTRACT START -->
## Daily Learning Contract

- **Topic:** Job Search
- **Objective:** Students will identify and apply the steps of an effective job search by sequencing a safe search cycle, screening a fictional posting, and selecting an authorized next action.
- **TEKS:** d(6)(A)
- **Demonstration of Learning:** Two-page job-search and posting-screen decision record.
<!-- CCE DAILY CONTRACT END -->

## Before Class

Post the Student Annotation and the two-page record. Default printing is zero. Display the seven-step strip and confirm that the Pecan Creek posting is fictional, not a vacancy.

## 50-Minute Flow

1. **Bellringer - 5 min.** Put seven job-search actions in a first-draft order. Students mark one step they are unsure about.
2. **Model the cycle - 8 min.** Target, prepare, search, screen, track, tailor/apply, follow up. Emphasize that screening occurs before personal data or contact.
3. **Notice and label - 10 min.** Read the fixed posting. Mark job duties, qualifications, evidence limits, and facts that would require verification in a real search.
4. **Posting screen - 17 min.** Students identify two truthful matches, one honest gap, two verification checks, and the authorized next action.
5. **Turn and talk - 5 min.** Partners compare one match and one risk. Students revise their own record.
6. **Exit - 5 min.** Name the search step most likely to be skipped and the consequence.

## Monitor, Key, and Trim Point

Full evidence puts the seven actions in a defensible order, distinguishes a posting from independent employer verification, uses two supplied matches, keeps one gap visible, and chooses a safe next action. A job-board result is a lead, not proof. Trim the optional partner share before reducing the individual screen.

## Supports and Access

Keep **posting/oferta de empleo**, **screen/revisar**, **verify/verificar**, **evidence/evidencia**, and **authorized/autorizado** visible beside the posting screen. Use the frame: “I would verify **[detail]** through **[official or known route]** before **[action]**.” Students may type, annotate, dictate, use enlarged print, or use paper. A live job board, H&amp;L, FYF Rung 5, employer contact, account, or personal data is not required.
