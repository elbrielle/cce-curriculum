**Evidence:** eight-page individual career plan

## 50-Minute Flow

1. **Model - 5 min.** A plan is current and revisable, not a forever promise.
2. **Self/career - 10 min.** Interests/values/strengths, direction, alternative, task/work product.
3. **Preparation/labor - 10 min.** Measure, amount, geography, date, source, limitation.
4. **High school/training - 10 min.** Current verified Irving label, question, enrollment/action, postsecondary boundary.
5. **Goals/support/conclusion - 12 min.** Three actions, obstacle, backup, support, flexible conclusion.
6. **Exit - 3 min.** Strongest evidence and uncertainty.

## Key and Access

No pathway guarantee. Current source and unresolved question stay visible. Accept typing, dictation, annotation, enlarged print, or paper; each section has a dedicated page.
