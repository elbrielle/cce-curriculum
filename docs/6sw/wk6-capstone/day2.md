# Day 2: Individual Career Plan
<!-- CCE DAILY CONTRACT START -->
## Daily Learning Contract

- **Topic:** Career Plan
- **Objective:** Students will select a career pathway or direction, document high-school and postsecondary or training requirements, and write an individual plan for starting the career after high school and any postsecondary education.
- **TEKS:** d(8)(A), d(8)(B), d(8)(C)
- **Demonstration of Learning:** Four-page individual career plan with current evidence, source limits, route questions, three actions, support, and backup.
<!-- CCE DAILY CONTRACT END -->

## 50-Minute Flow

1. **Model - 5 min.** Show how a current plan can be specific without becoming a forever promise.
2. **Current direction - 8 min.** Students connect self evidence to a direction, alternative, and career task.
3. **Preparation and labor evidence - 10 min.** Students label amount or trend, measure, geography, date, source, and limitation.
4. **High school and training route - 10 min.** Students separate a current verified label from a course, application, cost, time, or transportation question that still needs confirmation.
5. **Action plan - 12 min.** Students write three actions, dates, an obstacle, a backup, and a support request.
6. **Exit - 5 min.** State the strongest evidence and the most important uncertainty.

## Key and Access

Check the evidence basis rather than the student's career preference. A national median is not local starting pay, a pathway label is not an admission guarantee, and a projected trend is not a personal outcome. Students may type, dictate, annotate, use enlarged print, or use paper. Keep the four-page plan in its original Canvas or labeled-paper location for Major 2 scoring; students will not upload it again on Day 4.
