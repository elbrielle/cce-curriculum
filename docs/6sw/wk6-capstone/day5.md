# Day 5: Final Reflection and Transfer Forward

**TEKS:** d(8)(A), d(8)(C); reflection/revision evidence
**Evidence:** seven-page private final course reflection

## 50-Minute Flow

1. **Then/now - 5 min.** Career planning at course start versus now.
2. **Explore the year - 12 min.** One meaningful example per six weeks and the example that changed thinking.
3. **Self and transfer - 13 min.** Self-knowledge plus five transferable skills/evidence contexts.
4. **Next action - 10 min.** Action, date, support role, realism, flexibility.
5. **Advice/closing - 7 min.** Advice, trusted-adult/counselor message, open question.
6. **Private submit - 3 min.** Celebration/share is optional and consent-based.

## Key and Access

Specific evidence matters more than positivity. Do not require a class photo, public share, family detail, top career declaration, or H&L login. Accept private typed, dictated, annotated, enlarged-print, or paper reflection.
