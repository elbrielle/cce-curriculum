## 50-Minute Flow

1. **Then/now warm-up - 5 min.** Current direction and one change.
2. **Model evidence jobs - 8 min.** Self, career, labor, pathway, training, action, support.
3. **Inventory - 17 min.** Mark ready/revise/recover and identify where each claim lives.
4. **Recover - 15 min.** Use fixed source, staff conference, role card, or supervised catch-up; record what it can/cannot prove.
5. **Exit - 5 min.** Six strongest pieces, one conflict, Day 2 priority.

## Key and Access

Do not lower mastery because an old workbook page, family interview, partner, or platform is missing. Require current evidence, source limits, and an honest recovery path. No private profile screenshot or contact data.
