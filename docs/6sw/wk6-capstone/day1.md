# Day 1: Evidence Audit and Recovery
<!-- CCE DAILY CONTRACT START -->
## Daily Learning Contract

- **Topic:** Evidence Audit
- **Objective:** Students will select a current career pathway or direction using self and career evidence, identify one missing or uncertain evidence job, and document an honest recovery action.
- **TEKS:** d(8)(A)
- **Demonstration of Learning:** Two-page career-evidence inventory and recovery plan.
<!-- CCE DAILY CONTRACT END -->

## 50-Minute Flow

1. **Then/now Stop and Jot - 5 min.** Name the current direction and one change or stable result.
2. **Model the evidence jobs - 8 min.** Show one ready item, one item that needs revision, and one item that needs recovery.
3. **Inventory and active monitoring - 17 min.** Students mark eight evidence jobs and name where each item lives or can be rebuilt.
4. **Recovery block - 15 min.** Students use one supplied source, prior Canvas artifact, staff conference, or supervised platform catch-up. They write what the evidence supports and what it cannot prove.
5. **Exit - 5 min.** Record four pieces to carry forward, one conflict, and the Day 2 priority.

## Key and Access

The recovery action must produce usable current evidence, not a promise to find it later. Do not lower mastery because an old page, family interview, partner, or platform is missing. Preserve the evidence inventory and recovery; trim partner discussion first. No private profile screenshot or contact data.
