# Day 1: Mid-Year Profile Audit

## Lesson Overview

| | |
|---|---|
| **Time** | 50 minutes |
| **Objective** | Compare an earlier career-assessment or profile result with current evidence |
| **TEKS** | d(1)(A) |
| **Deliverable** | Mid-Year Profile Audit |
| **Materials** | Audit sheet, FYF Rung 1 excerpt, earlier portfolio/profile evidence when available |

## Before Class

- Open the Day 1 Student Guide and model.
- Give students access to earlier evidence without requiring a private screenshot.
- Prepare the no-prior-evidence route: students use today's self-evidence inventory as a baseline.

## Warm-Up (5 min)

**Name one interest, skill, or career idea that has changed since the start of the year. What caused the change?**

Students may write “not sure yet.” The lesson is designed to help them find evidence.

## Activity 1: What Counts as Evidence? (10 min)

Use FYF Rung 1 to review six evidence types: interests, strengths, skills, work values, personality results, and Building Blocks. Model the difference between a label and evidence:

- Label: “I am a Helper.”
- Evidence: “I chose patient-care and teaching tasks repeatedly, and I liked explaining directions to a partner.”

An assessment result is a starting point, not a verdict. Students do not retake an assessment just to make the lesson work.

## Activity 2: Build the Audit (20 min)

Students complete the Mid-Year Profile Audit:

1. record one earlier result or idea;
2. record three current pieces of evidence from different sources;
3. decide whether the evidence supports, complicates, or changes the earlier result; and
4. explain one important pattern.

**Monitoring pass 1:** Every student has an earlier result or a clearly labeled current baseline.

**Monitoring pass 2:** Evidence is specific. “I like technology” is too broad; “I chose troubleshooting in three activities and liked the network simulation” is usable.

## Activity 3: Discuss the Pattern (10 min)

Students choose a private written response or a structured partner exchange:

- “My earlier result said ____.”
- “Current evidence shows ____.”
- “The best explanation is ____.”

Partners respond with one clarifying question, not a judgment about whether the career is right.

## Exit Ticket (5 min)

**What is one conclusion you can defend about yourself now, and what evidence supports it?**

Submit the completed audit. The teacher checks for a result, current evidence, and a defensible conclusion.

## Supports and Fallbacks

- **Absent:** Complete the same audit from the Student Guide. Partner talk becomes a written clarifying question.
- **Platform unavailable:** Use notebook, workbook, and remembered classroom evidence. Platform history is optional.
- **Support:** Offer the evidence bank and sentence frames. Allow speech-to-text.
- **Extension:** Identify evidence that does not fit the current conclusion and explain why it matters.
