# Week 4: Culinary Arts and Hospitality

**3rd Six Weeks** | Hospitality and Tourism | 5 class periods, 50 minutes each

## Lesson Objective

Students explore three hospitality careers, use the *Find Your Future* Culinary Twist, Motivation, Hotel Rescue, and Cater and Create activities, compare three careers with fixed current evidence, and recommend a career and small-business opportunity for a defined student scenario.

**Learning Target:** I can use career evidence and client needs to make and explain hospitality decisions.

**Success Criteria:**

- I can name three hospitality careers and describe a task from each.
- I can compare the May 2024 U.S. median pay and common preparation for all three careers.
- I can distinguish intrinsic and extrinsic motivation and transfer the idea across careers.
- I can solve a hotel crisis without ignoring safety, accessibility, or an unverified promise.
- I can describe what a hospitality entrepreneur sells, coordinates, and risks.

## Demonstration of Learning

Students submit an individual Hospitality Career and Business Recommendation. The response uses one correctly labeled career-data fact, one preparation or schedule tradeoff, one career task, one small-business opportunity, and a current Irving ISD connection when supported.

The guide preserves preparation, outlook, annual openings, schedule differences, and the national-median boundary. Xello may add localized evidence only when geography, date, and measure are visible.

## Vocabulary

- **Hospitality:** Work that supports guest food, lodging, events, travel, or experiences.
- **Median pay:** The middle pay among workers in an occupation.
- **Intrinsic motivation:** Motivation connected to learning, achievement, attitude, or personal pride.
- **Extrinsic motivation:** Motivation connected to an outside reward, recognition, influence, or avoided consequence.
- **Entrepreneur:** A person who starts and operates a business and takes responsibility for its decisions and risks.
- **Tradeoff:** A choice that gains one benefit while accepting a cost, limit, or risk.

## Assessment and Grading

Days 1-4 are formative practice. Do not grade art polish, platform choice, public speaking confidence, or whether a classmate was available.

**Mapped minor grade:** Hospitality Career and Business Recommendation, 16 raw rubric points converted to the existing 100-point Canvas Minor 2 assignment.

- Career evidence: 4 points
- Preparation and tradeoff: 4 points
- Entrepreneurship: 4 points
- Recommendation and verified local connection: 4 points

This is Minor 2 in 3SW. The importer must find exactly one unpublished 100-point assignment in **Minor Assessments (40%)** before it writes any Wk4 content. The rubric conversion is `(raw score / 16) × 100`, rounded to the nearest whole number.
