# Day 5: Veterinary Pathway Brief

> **Canvas is authoritative.** This Markdown page is a source backup for the unpublished Canvas Teacher Facilitator Guide and Student Guide.

## 50-minute plan

- **Warm-up — 5 minutes:** Sort statements into current fact, opportunity, or guarantee.
- **Current local evidence — 10 minutes:** Compare the current Nimitz Veterinary Science page with the workbook's district-context pages.
- **Build the pathway brief — 20 minutes:** Career, preparation route, daily task, dated labor-market fact, Nimitz connection, and one realistic next action.
- **Rubric check and revision — 10 minutes:** Use the student-visible four-criterion rubric.
- **Exit — 5 minutes:** Name one action an eighth grader can take now without claiming guaranteed admission or certification.

Current Nimitz public wording includes **Small Animal Management, Large Animal Veterinary Science, and Advanced Animal Science**, multiple certification opportunities, and a Certified Veterinary Assistant route that describes 300 clinical internship hours by the end of senior year. Present these as opportunities with requirements, not guarantees. The workbook pages remain useful historical curriculum context.

Hats & Ladders App Exploration is optional enrichment. No H&L favorite count, profile screenshot, or live login is required for the pathway brief.

## Evidence and grading

The recommended durable artifact is a 16-point **minor** evidence packet. Score career/preparation accuracy, labor-market evidence, current local pathway connection, and a realistic next action. Fabrication, platform access, paragraph length, and design polish do not affect the score.
