# 3SW Week 1: Veterinary Science

> **Canvas is authoritative.** This page records the build logic and is not a teacher delivery surface.

## Week target

Students compare veterinary careers with dated evidence, apply observation and prioritization in a fictional triage simulation without diagnosing, complete the required Xello Skills lesson, and build a pathway recommendation using the district workbook and career evidence.

## Canvas sequence

| Day | Focus | Durable evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Meet the Veterinary Team | One career choice supported by daily-work and preparation evidence |
| 2 | Compare Veterinary Career Paths | Three-role comparison and evidence-based recommendation |
| 3 | Veterinary Triage | FYF p. 99 notes for Leo and Barnaby, a two-observation defense, and an ungraded retryable Canvas quiz |
| 4 | Xello Skills | Completion Standards report plus private two-career transfer reflection |
| 5 | Veterinary Pathway Recommendation | Career route, FYF pathway evidence, high-school/postsecondary transition, and student-visible 16-point rubric check |

Every day has a paired unpublished Teacher Facilitator Guide and Student Guide. Licensed workbook and Xello visuals remain in locked Canvas folders. Day subheaders keep the module chronological and easy to scan.

## Source and safety boundaries

- Salary evidence uses one basis: May 2024 U.S. medians from BLS. It is not starting pay or a guarantee.
- FYF pp. 100-101 are the primary local pathway source for students. The public Nimitz page is teacher background for current-detail questions and does not replace a workbook answer.
- H&L is optional enrichment, not required evidence.
- Xello Skills is the required task for this placement: 35 minutes with at least three saved careers as the prerequisite.
- Triage is fictional. Students observe, compare, prioritize, and report; a veterinarian diagnoses and treats.
- Student profile screenshots and public assessment-result sharing are not required.

## Grading recommendation

Use the Day 2 comparison, Day 3 triage reasoning, and Day 5 pathway recommendation as one 16-point **minor** evidence packet. Day 1 and Day 4 checks are formative. Canvas quiz attempts and Xello clicks are not separate grades.

## Accessibility and worksheet-space rule

Required directions remain visible. Optional vocabulary and fallback information may use disclosures. Typed, written, dictated, and teacher-scribed evidence are equal when documented. Every printable is rendered and checked so the writing space matches the requested response. FYF p. 99 is the default triage writing surface; the custom triage record is an optional structured scaffold or absence route.
