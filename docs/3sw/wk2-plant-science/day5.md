## Required-versus-extended boundary

The licensed Xello facilitator package is an **80-minute sequence**:

1. Career Trailblazers - 30 minutes
2. Xello Biases and career choices - 30 minutes
3. Non-traditional Career Matches - 20 minutes

The district-assigned Grade 8 task is **Activity 2 only**. It has no prerequisite. Activity 3 recommends Matchmaker and asks students to revisit a discounted career; it is an optional extension, not today's completion minimum. Work experiences was completed in the prior week and is not repeated.

## Privacy and facilitation

Students may use an assumption from media, school, or general culture. They are not required to disclose identity, family income, disability, or a personal experience with discrimination. Public discussion is optional; the evidence is private. Do not require a profile screenshot.

The reflection asks students to:

1. Name one career assumption.
2. Use one Plant-Tech Guide or Xello career-profile fact to challenge, complicate, or confirm it.
3. Explain one fair investigation strategy before ruling a career in or out.

## Supports and fallback

Use the official slide deck, read prompts aloud, provide bilingual labels, and allow a private teacher conference. If Xello fails, students complete the reflection with the fixed Plant-Tech Guide and move the required lesson to supervised catch-up. Paper does not count as Xello completion.
