# Day 5: Test a Plant-Career Assumption with Evidence
<!-- CCE DAILY CONTRACT START -->
## Daily Learning Contract

- **Topic:** Fair Career Investigation
- **Objective:** Students will revisit an assumption about emerging or nontraditional career work and evaluate it with one career fact.
- **TEKS:** d(1)(D)
- **Demonstration of Learning:** Revised plant-tech evidence plus a private assumption-evidence-action reflection.

| | |
|---|---|

## Before class

- Give each student the Emerging Plant-Tech Evidence Guide and their Day 4 evaluation.
- Project the corrected warm-up and one complete assumption-to-evidence model from the Canvas Student Guide.
- Post the one-page private reflection and private Canvas assignment. Default print count is 0; print one copy per student only for the paper route.
- Keep all work individual/private. Public sharing is optional.

## 50-minute flow

| Time | Work |
|---:|---|
| 5 | Private warm-up: Where do career assumptions come from? |
| 20 | Audit the Day 4 plant-tech evaluation and strengthen one weak evidence link |
| 15 | Private reflection: assumption, one fixed career fact, one fair next move |
| 7 | Revise and submit the evidence packet |
| 3 | Close: name one question that still needs a source |

## Privacy and facilitation

Students may use an assumption from media, school, or general culture. They are not required to disclose identity, family income, disability, or a personal experience with discrimination. Public discussion is optional; the evidence is private. Do not require a profile screenshot.

The reflection asks students to:

1. Name one career assumption.
2. Use one fixed Plant-Tech Guide fact to challenge, complicate, or confirm it.
3. Explain one fair investigation strategy before ruling a career in or out.

The private evidence check reinforces d(1)(D) and gives students a final revision opportunity before the week closes.

## Monitor, pivot, and trim

- Use a private Stop and Jot, then Active Monitor the evidence labels without reading personal opinions aloud.
- By minute 15, every student should have identified one evidence link to strengthen.
- By minute 35, every student should have one assumption, one fixed career fact, and one fair investigation move.
- If time is short, cut public sharing. Protect the evidence revision, private reflection, and submission.

## Supports and fallback

Read prompts aloud, provide bilingual labels, and allow a private teacher conference. The fixed Plant-Tech Guide and paper or Canvas reflection are the complete routes; no platform catch-up is required.
