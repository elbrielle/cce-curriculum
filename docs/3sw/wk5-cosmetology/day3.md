# Day 3: Quality Check and Texas Cosmetology Pathways

## Lesson Overview

| | |
|---|---|
| **Time** | 50 minutes |
| **Objectives** | Evaluate and revise an SFX design, identify current Texas Cosmetology Operator requirements, compare high-school and postsecondary training settings, and report questions needed before enrollment |
| **TEKS** | d(2)(A), d(3)(G) |
| **Deliverable** | SFX Quality Check and Revision Plan plus Cosmetology Pathway Decision |
| **Materials** | FYF p. 131 and pp. 136-137, two student PDFs, projector |

## Before Class

- Post the dated Texas Cosmetology Operator Evidence Guide and Pathway Decision.
- Do not make live TDLR navigation load-bearing. The fixed evidence page protects the lesson from site changes and reading variance.
- Note the current district wording: Cosmetology is listed at Cardwell, Irving, MacArthur, and Nimitz. Do not present Ratteree as the current location.
- Do not teach a Texas cosmetology apprenticeship route. TDLR's current operator page requires 1,000 hours at a licensed school.

## Warm-Up (5 min)

**WARM-UP:** What is one part of your model that still works today, and what is one part you would rebuild first?

## Activity 1: Quality Check and Revision (10 min)

Use FYF p. 131 and the expanded quality sheet. Students rate:

- how clearly the texture reads;
- whether layers or pieces stay aligned or attached; and
- whether the final build follows the concept map.

Students cite one visible success, identify the cause of the biggest problem, and draw a revision with at least three labels. The revised drawing has a full usable page area.

## Activity 2: Read the Current Evidence (15 min)

Students annotate the fixed evidence guide:

- box the 1,000-hour requirement;
- underline the written and practical exams;
- star the age and application fee;
- bracket the current Irving ISD campus list; and
- circle one item that still requires a counselor, coursebook, CTE office, or school answer.

Clarify that the TDLR license requirements stay the same whether the approved training happens during high school or after graduation. Cost, schedule, transportation, admission, and local hours cannot be guessed.

## Activity 3: Compare and Recommend (15 min)

Students complete the two-route table for an Irving ISD high-school setting and a licensed postsecondary school. Then they recommend a route for Alex using one verified fact and one unanswered question.

Students write exact enrollment questions for three sources and put the five license steps in order. This is stronger evidence than ranking invented costs or completion dates.

## Exit Check (5 min)

What state requirement stays the same in both routes, and what local question could change the decision?

**DELIVERABLE:** Quality/revision evidence and complete pathway decision.

## Teacher Key

- Current common requirement: a 1,000-hour Cosmetology Operator course at a licensed school.
- Written exam eligibility begins after the school reports 900 hours; practical exam eligibility follows completion of 1,000 hours and the written exam.
- Current application facts: age 17, $50 nonrefundable fee, two-year license.
- Valid local questions include admission, campus, transportation, schedule, high-school hours, tuition, supply cost, program approval, and completion time.
- Either training setting can be recommended if the evidence and unanswered question fit Alex.

## Support and Absence Route

- Read one evidence section at a time and use the mark-up key.
- Provide bilingual labels: license/licencia, training/capacitación, exam/examen, fee/tarifa, question/pregunta.
- Allow oral rehearsal before the 3-4 sentence recommendation.
- The fixed guide and worksheet are the full absence route; no live website is required.
