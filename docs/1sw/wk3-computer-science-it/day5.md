## Activity 1: Grade 7 Xello Learning Style Quiz (20 min)

**Source:** Bowie Grade 7 Xello Completion Standard, **Learning Style quiz**. Students saved three researched careers in Week 2 so the paired Grade 7 lesson can open today.

Confirm the roster and Completion Standards report before class. Students use the district SSO and do not create another account.

Students first turn in the completed Major 1 packet, then open **About Me > Learning Style**, complete the quiz, and review the result. Before the quiz, show three unlabeled method groups: watching/reading, listening/speaking, and hands-on/movement. Students predict which methods help them, then compare the prediction with the result.

Do not present the result as a fixed label or a limit on what the student can do. The useful evidence is the learning method the student can try in a real class task.

---

## Activity 2: Grade 7 Xello Learning Styles Lesson (30 min)

Students open **Dashboard > View all lessons > Learning styles**. They investigate visual, auditory, and tactile methods and how those methods can help in school and at work. During the lesson, students complete the Method-to-Task Connection sheet. They name one method, one school or IT task where it could help, one obstacle, and one method they will test next.

Close with the reminder that a learning-style result does not measure ability or predict grades. Students can use methods from every group and can change strategies as the task changes.

## Evidence Collection and Close

Collect or verify the Day 3 app packet and Day 4 research sheet as one Major 1 submission. Use the 16-point rubric. Check the Xello Completion Standards report separately; the quiz, lesson, and three saved-career dependency are not part of the major grade.

## Differentiation

- **Support:** Read the three method groups aloud, keep the visual examples visible, and let students rehearse the connection before writing. If Xello is blocked, use the method chart printed on the connection sheet as the complete learning-evidence route for the day; schedule platform completion separately without recording false completion.
- **Extension:** Students choose one method outside their result and explain a task where that different method could work better.
- **ELL:** Use the word bank visual, auditory, tactile, method, obstacle, and strategy. Complete frame: "I will try _____ when I _____ because _____." Students may rehearse in their strongest language before writing in English.
