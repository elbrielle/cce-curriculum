## Next-Day Operations

- **Default:** one device and retained Day 4 report per student, one projector, zero new prints. Students reopen the same Days 4–5 Assignment.
- **Paper:** return the retained five-page report; print one replacement only for an absent or missing-copy student. Use one collection tray per class. Print the two-page feedback guide only when a student needs paper.
- Private recording requires one device with a working microphone and headphones per recording student. Live and teacher-conference briefings use the visible report/evidence card. An authorized AAC route uses the student's speech-generating device or approved assistive technology.
- **Checkpoint:** by minute 30, five findings, the thermal boundary, and the overall limitation are present. Students who are behind switch to labeled bullets rather than dropping a criterion. At minute 38, each student highlights the five briefing jobs in the strongest complete finding.
- **Trim/recovery:** cut public sharing and decorative formatting. Protect the report, individual briefing, submission, and material return. Incomplete evidence is saved or collected for the same private recovery route, not a second report or homework packet.

## Ready-to-Project Model

**Technology choice:** visible report/evidence card during a teacher conference.

“For Image 3, I observed irregular brown discoloration where the ceiling meets the wall. It could indicate current or past moisture, but the image cannot prove the source or whether the area is wet now. A Texas-licensed Professional Real Estate Inspector should evaluate it next and document the observation in an inspection report. If the inspector confirms a roof or plumbing concern, the report can direct the client to the appropriate qualified trade professional.”

**Non-example:** “The roof leaks, so replace it.” This diagnoses a cause and gives repair advice without enough evidence.

## Warm-Up (5 min)

Rewrite “Something is wrong with this house” as one precise observation and one cautious possibility.

## Activity 1: Report Model and Boundary (5 min)

Model one finding. Use a fictional student analyst ID or fictional inspector name—not a student legal name. Each finding contains:

- image and area;
- visible observation;
- what it could indicate;
- evidence limit or next check;
- qualified professional who should evaluate it.

The report does not diagnose, price repairs, rank purchase decisions, or promise safety.

## Activity 2: Complete the Evidence Report (25 min)

Students revise the shared five-page evidence report begun on Day 4 rather than recopying the findings into a second packet. They complete five image blocks, the thermal boundary, and the overall summary. A neutral image still requires the area checked, visible evidence, and the limit of the photograph.

Teacher monitoring sequence:

1. every row begins with a visible observation;
2. possibilities use “could” or “may”;
3. no row claims mold, a leak source, code violation, or safe condition as proven;
4. each row names an appropriate qualified follow-up role.

## Activity 3: Individual Professional Briefing (12 min)

Every student prepares and delivers one 30-45 second briefing using one appropriate technology choice: the visible report/evidence card during a live or teacher-conference briefing, a private Canvas audio/video recording, or an accommodation-aligned AAC/speech-generating device. A written report or transcript may scaffold preparation but does not by itself demonstrate the oral standard.

Briefing jobs:

1. name the image/area;
2. state the visible evidence;
3. explain one cautious possibility and limit;
4. recommend the qualified role for further evaluation;
5. name the work product that worker would create or use next;
6. use the selected evidence or assistive technology during the briefing.

Public presentation is optional practice and is not required. There is no cold team jigsaw.

## Exit Check and Private Submission (3 min)

Submit the report and oral evidence. Then answer: Which word or phrase kept your report from overstating the evidence?

## Teacher Key and Scoring

Use the Day 4 interpretation guide. Score four criteria, 0–4 each:

1. visible evidence accuracy;
2. cautious inference and evidence limit;
3. correct career/qualified-role connection;
4. organized professional briefing using an appropriate evidence or assistive technology.

Art, accent, grammar, H&L state, recording polish, camera use, eye contact, and visual polish do not determine the score unless meaning is unclear. Keep the Assignment unpublished and ungraded until the six-weeks Minor/Major map and 40/60 weighting are verified.

## Supports and Fallback

Provide sentence frames, bilingual labels, a model/non-example, speech-to-text for the report, and a private teacher-conference briefing. Absent students use the same locked images and complete an individual route. H&L favorites, a partner, and public speaking are not required.
