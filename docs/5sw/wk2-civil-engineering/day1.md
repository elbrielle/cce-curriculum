## Before Class

Ask students to use FYF p. 103 and pp. 174–175 in their workbooks. Post the locked page images for projection, absence, or enlarged viewing. Add the fixed Civil Engineer evidence card and a short companion only for evidence the workbook does not collect. H&L is optional.

## 50-Minute Flow

### 1. Infrastructure is a system — 5 minutes

Students name an Irving road, drainage, transit, water, or public-space system and one group that depends on it. Do not promise a universal 50-year service life.

### 2. Engineering cluster and local pathway — 10 minutes

Use FYF p. 103 and the dated district card. Clarify that the public MacArthur page says Engineering while the 2026–27 coursebook names Civil Engineering. Students describe what problem the cluster solves and how engineers, technicians, surveyors, planners, and construction workers exchange evidence.

### 3. Civil Engineer evidence — 15 minutes

Students record duties, typical entry education, May 2024 U.S. median, outlook, and licensure boundary. Entry-level work does not universally require a PE. Any local Xello figure stays in a separate field with geography/year/measure.

### 4. FYF kitchen systems decision — 15 minutes

Students draw on the FYF p. 175 grid, using the existing p. 174 appliance plan. They document one placement that forced a second change. The analogy is limited: the activity practices dependency reasoning, not civil-engineering approval.

### 5. Exit — 5 minutes

In three short jobs: describe the cluster, name one civil-engineering career and its work, and state one accurate preparation requirement.

## Teacher Look-Fors

- Students preserve U.S./median/May 2024/BLS labels.
- They distinguish entry education from PE licensure.
- The systems explanation names a first choice, affected element, and reason.

## Absence Route

Use the fixed career card and the kitchen overlay. No live app, partner, or open research is required.
