# Day 3: Location Changes the Cost Target

**Time:** 50 minutes

**TEKS:** d(5)(D) supporting application

**Evidence:** same-household location comparison and relocation judgment

## Fixed February 2026 evidence

MIT Living Wage Calculator, one adult/no children, 2,080 work hours per year:

| Location | Living-wage hourly rate |
|---|---:|
| Tulsa County, Oklahoma | $21.23 |
| Dallas County, Texas | $23.31 |
| Los Angeles County, California | $28.92 |
| New York County, New York | $38.21 |

Students compare Dallas with two of the other locations. The household scenario and source date stay the same. CareerOneStop may be demonstrated as a current extension, but it is not load-bearing.

## 50-minute flow

1. **Prediction — 5 min.** Rank the four locations before seeing the rates.
2. **Read the fixed evidence — 8 min.** Identify household scenario, date, hourly unit, and source limitation.
3. **Three-location comparison — 20 min.** Convert hourly requirement to an annual before-tax target using 2,080 hours and compare each target with the selected career's correctly labeled salary.
4. **Relocation decision — 12 min.** Choose a location for fictional Jordan using cost target, career salary, and one missing factor.
5. **Exit — 5 min.** Rank three locations and cite one number for the first and last ranks.

## Monitoring key

Hourly living-wage rate × 2,080 gives an approximate annual earnings target. This does not provide a local salary for the student's occupation, predict rent, prove affordability for every person, or include a student's desired household. A higher salary in another place must be compared with that place's higher or lower cost target.

## Access and absence route

All required numbers appear in the packet. Calculator, multiplication table, precomputed annual targets, typed response, annotation, or paper are acceptable. If the live tool is blocked, instruction continues unchanged.
