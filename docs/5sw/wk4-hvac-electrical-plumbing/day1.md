# Day 1: Four Skilled-Trades Careers and Preparation

**Time:** 50 minutes

**TEKS:** d(1)(C), d(2)(A)

**Evidence:** four-career preparation record

## Before class

Provide the fixed evidence packet. Do not depend on H&L, open search, or remembered apprenticeship lengths. Keep the current MacArthur Welding/Construction labels visible without promising admission, credentials, placement, or employment.

## 50-minute flow

1. **System warm-up — 5 min.** Name one building system and the workers whose evidence must connect before it is safe to use.
2. **Current pathway boundary — 8 min.** Separate high-school program, occupation, training route, state registration/license, and employer credential.
3. **Four fixed career cards — 22 min.** Students record work, typical preparation, state boundary, May 2024 U.S. median, 2024–34 growth, and annual openings for Electrician, Plumber/Pipefitter/Steamfitter, HVAC Mechanic/Installer, and Welder.
4. **Compare preparation — 10 min.** Students explain two important differences and write one question that must be checked with an official state or provider source.
5. **Exit — 5 min.** Name one career, one accurate preparation fact, and one boundary the evidence does not prove.

## Teacher key

- Electricians: high school typical; most learn through a four- or five-year apprenticeship; Texas license type and eligibility must be checked with TDLR.
- Plumbers, pipefitters, and steamfitters: high school typical; most learn through apprenticeship; Texas plumber licenses and apprentice registration are governed by TSBPE. Journeyman eligibility includes 8,000 hours plus the approved training/program condition and exam.
- HVAC mechanics/installers: postsecondary nondegree award typical plus long-term on-the-job training. Texas non-exempt technician work requires the relevant TDLR registration or certification under a licensed contractor; contractor licensure is a separate route.
- Welders: high school typical plus technical and on-the-job training. Do not invent one universal Texas welding license or require one named industry certification for every job.

## Access route

The packet is the complete independent route. Accept annotation, typed labeled responses, enlarged print, dictation, or paper. H&L browsing may be an early-finisher extension but no favorite or screenshot is required.
