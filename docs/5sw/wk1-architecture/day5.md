# Day 5: Unexpected Architecture and Weekly Synthesis

## Lesson Overview

| | |
|---|---|
| **Time** | 50 minutes |
| **Target** | Apply city-goal evidence to a novelty-building concept and explain how multiple cluster roles work together. |
| **TEKS** | d(1)(B), d(1)(C); portfolio reassesses d(2)(A), d(5)(E) |
| **Evidence** | Individual landmark evidence + completed weekly portfolio |

## Before Class

- Upload the licensed “Unexpected Architecture” city-goals slide and FYF pp. 182–184 only to locked Canvas.
- Prepare groups of three or four and an individual evidence form for every student.
- Keep the final portfolio Assignment private. It accepts the Day 2 comparison, design image/paper equivalent, revision record, and individual synthesis by upload, text, or media.
- H&L favorites and eDynamic 3.1 are optional extensions only after live verification.

## 50-Minute Flow

### 1. Read the city brief — 5 minutes

Students study the licensed examples and city goals. Clarify that a memorable shape still must serve users, communicate purpose, and acknowledge cost/material limits.

### 2. Firm concept — 25 minutes

Each firm:

1. names its fictional firm;
2. selects two city goals;
3. brainstorms multiple shapes;
4. chooses one concept using evidence;
5. draws labeled front and side views;
6. names materials as design ideas, not approved engineering specifications;
7. prepares a one-minute explanation.

Each student completes an individual record naming the chosen goals, one contribution, one design decision, and one A&C career responsible for related real-world work.

### 3. Paired/gallery pitches — 15 minutes

Firms exchange one-minute pitches. Listeners give one evidence-based strength and one question using the 1–4 feedback scale. Public speaking is formative; a written or recorded private explanation is equal.

### 4. Cluster synthesis — 5 minutes

Individually describe how at least three roles—such as architect, drafter, engineer, construction manager, or skilled-trades worker—would contribute at different points. Name one preparation or salary fact from Day 2 and preserve its source label.

## Weekly Portfolio

Students submit:

- Day 2 three-career comparison;
- Day 4 design or paper equivalent;
- one documented test and revision;
- Day 5 individual cluster synthesis.

The 16-point rubric scores career/source accuracy, design and revision evidence, career/cluster connection, and supported explanation. Grammar, art, accent, tool choice, group attendance, and platform access do not determine the score.

## Teacher Look-Fors and Key

- The synthesis includes at least three distinct roles and describes work, not just titles.
- One salary claim retains occupation, May 2024, U.S., median, and BLS.
- One preparation claim distinguishes education, experience, examination, or other training.
- The landmark connects to stated city goals rather than visual novelty alone.

## Absence Route

Students use the same locked city-goal visual and create a solo front/side concept or analyze a supplied model. They complete the same individual evidence and portfolio; no group or live pitch is required.
