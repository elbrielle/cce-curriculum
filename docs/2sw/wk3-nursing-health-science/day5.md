# Day 5: Build a Nursing Career Evidence Map

## Warm-Up (5 min)

**WARM-UP:** Name one career you want to keep exploring and one fact you still need before you could make a decision.

Students may name a career outside Health Science as a future comparison, but today's evidence comes from the fixed nursing route guide.

## Activity 1: Audit Three Nursing Careers (20 min)

Students reopen the fixed Nursing Career Route Guide and the comparison they began earlier in the week. They select at least three careers and check four evidence categories for each one: responsibility, preparation, work condition, and source-labeled pay.

Default setup is individual work. A pair may rehearse one comparison, but each student completes a separate map.

1. Circle or list three careers from the route guide.
2. Record one accurate responsibility for each career.
3. Record the common preparation route and one work condition for each career.
4. Copy one pay figure with its year, geography, and measure still attached.
5. Star the evidence category that matters most to the student's current decision.

Use the route guide as the source of record. A live profile or screenshot is not required.

**Safe trim:** Use the completed nursing assistant model as one row and require students to finish two additional rows. Protect the supported current-fit statement and next fact to verify.

## Activity 2: Compare and Decide (20 min)

Students complete the [Nursing Career Evidence Map](../../resources/worksheets/2sw-wk3-xello-save-careers-reflection.pdf). They identify:

- one career that currently fits,
- one Week 0 result or building block that supports the choice,
- one career that needs more research, and
- the exact fact they will check next.

Students may use a current counselor- or teacher-approved source to answer the next question after the fixed comparison is complete. H&L and eDynamic are not required evidence.

## Exit Ticket (5 min)

**EXIT TICKET** (Concept Map / Connection Diagram) · [Printable PDF](../../resources/exit-tickets/2sw-wk3-day5-build-a-nursing-career-evidence-map.pdf):

Place one nursing career in the center. Connect it to:

1. one daily responsibility,
2. one preparation step,
3. one work condition or salary-source label, and
4. one reason to keep exploring or rule it out.

Star the connection that comes from a verified source. *(d(1)(C))*

## Differentiation

- **Support:** Begin with the completed nursing assistant model and highlight the same four evidence fields on two additional career rows.
- **Extension:** Compare one Health Science career with one career from another cluster using the same evidence categories and source labels.
- **ELL:** Pre-teach compare = comparar, responsibility = responsabilidad, work condition = condición de trabajo, and preparation = preparación. Allow oral rehearsal before the final current-fit statement.
