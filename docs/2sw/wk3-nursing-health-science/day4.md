## Warm-Up (5 min)

**WARM-UP:** Which statement belongs in an observation section: "heart rate was 132" or "the patient has a heart condition"? Explain the difference.

The first statement records supplied data. The second claims a diagnosis that the evidence and student role do not support.

## Activity 1: Use the Five-Part Routine (8 min)

Model the routine with one line from Card A:

1. **Situation:** Where is the person, and what needs attention?
2. **Observations:** What values and exact reported symptoms are supplied?
3. **Comparison:** What changed, stayed the same, or appears unreliable?
4. **Handoff:** Which supervised role receives the report?
5. **Reasoning:** Which case facts support that action?

Required privacy boundary: all names and data come from the fictional cards. Students do not share personal medical information.

## Activity 2: Analyze Four Case Patterns (17 min)

Use the [Fictional Patient Handoff Cards](../../resources/worksheets/2sw-wk3-fictional-patient-cards.pdf). Assign one card to each pair, then rotate or display all four.

- Card A asks students to compare after-activity and after-rest values.
- Card B requires a prompt supervised handoff based on the full picture, including reported symptoms.
- Card C shows why normal-looking numbers do not erase what a person says.
- Card D requires a device or process recheck because the readings conflict.

Students may discuss, but each student records their own observations.

## Activity 3: Write the Individual Handoff (12 min)

Students complete the [Observation and Clinical Handoff Record](../../resources/worksheets/2sw-wk3-clinical-handoff-record.pdf). Use the [Nursing Route and Handoff Rubric](../../resources/worksheets/2sw-wk3-handoff-rubric.pdf) to revise one sentence before submission.

The evidence is the distinction between observation and inference, the use of two case facts, and the organized supervised handoff. Speaking and physical technique are not scored.

## Activity 4: Practice Quiz and Review (5 min)

Students complete the unpublished Canvas **Vital Signs and Handoff Check**. It is an ungraded practice quiz with immediate feedback. A paper question set using the same five ideas is the equal fallback.

Review only the most-missed item before the exit ticket.

## Exit Ticket (3 min)

**EXIT TICKET** (Mini-Case / Scenario Application) · [Printable PDF](../../resources/exit-tickets/2sw-wk3-day4-document-a-fictional-patient-handoff.pdf):

Scenario: A fictional patient has three device readings that change sharply within 20 seconds, but the person is sitting still and reports no symptoms.

1. What data-quality problem should the worker notice?
2. What should be rechecked?
3. Write one sentence for the supervised handoff that separates the reading from the person's report. *(d(1)(C))*

## Differentiation

- **Support:** Color-code values, quoted symptoms, and inference. Provide the frames "The device displayed _____. The person reported _____. I would hand this information to _____."
- **Extension:** Compare Card B and Card D. Explain why both require attention for different reasons.
- **ELL:** Pre-teach symptom = síntoma, observation = observación, inference = inferencia, recheck = revisar de nuevo, handoff = entrega de información. Score the content, not accent or presentation speed.
