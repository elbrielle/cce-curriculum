## Before Class

Open the dated route guide and the current Irving ISD Singley Academy page. If offering Xello or H&L, preflight it on the student network. Do not make a vendor login the only research route.

## Warm-Up (5 min)

**If someone calls 911, which workers may be involved before, during, and after responders reach the scene?** Students list roles, then sort them into call-taking, response, care, investigation, and documentation.

## Activity 1: Read the Numbers Correctly (8 min)

Model the difference between a national median, a local estimate, and a starting salary. Students annotate the route guide:

1. circle the data year;
2. underline the geography;
3. box the word **median**; and
4. write “not a starting-pay promise” beside the table.

## Activity 2: Compare Three Careers (17 min)

Students choose three careers and complete the table. Pause after the first row to check that each student has one daily task, one preparation step, and an accurate explanation of what the median does and does not show.

**Active monitoring:** reject fixed promises such as “every academy lasts six months” or “all EMT programs take twelve weeks.” Accept routes that preserve the source language: requirements vary by agency, state, or locality.

## Activity 3: Compare Route Systems and Connect to Irving (15 min)

Students use the fixed guide to compare civilian and military law-enforcement route systems. Require one similarity, one difference, and one civilian requirement or credential that must be verified before a student assumes transfer. Military training may build related skills and credentials; it does not automatically replace every civilian academy, agency requirement, state license, or certification.

Students then use the current district page and FYF district spread to connect **Law Enforcement** and **Emergency Medical - EMT** to one career each. Make clear that a program is an opportunity to investigate, not a guaranteed job or credential.

Optional Xello extension: add one current local figure with career, geography, date checked, and whether the display is a range, median, or another measure.

## Exit Ticket (5 min)

**EXIT TICKET** (Ranked Justification) · [Printable PDF](../../resources/exit-tickets/2sw-wk2-day1-compare-first-responder-routes.pdf):

Rank the three careers from most to least preparation after high school. Defend the top ranking with one route-guide detail, then describe one responsibility shared across the cluster.

## Supports and Fallbacks

- Pre-teach route, academy, license, certification, median, and locality.
- Use the frame: “This career commonly requires ___; the source says requirements vary by ___.”
- For the route comparison, use: “Both systems ___, but only the ___ route ___. Before transfer, I would verify ___.”
- Students may compare two careers first, then add the third.
- If all sites are unavailable, the route guide contains the complete required evidence.
