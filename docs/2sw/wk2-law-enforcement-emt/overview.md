# Week 2: First Responders — Evidence, Response, and Handoff

**2nd Six Weeks** | Law, Public Safety, Corrections & Security | 5 class periods (50 minutes each)

## Week at a Glance

Students compare five first-responder careers with a dated route guide, investigate the fictional Clinton Lake case, run a controlled trail-response simulation, and write a fictional Patient Care Report with a safety-first complication plan. The week ends with an individual career and integrity reflection.

Hats & Ladders and Xello may extend career exploration. Neither platform is required to complete the core evidence.

**Learning target:** I can compare first-responder routes, separate evidence from claims, document a fictional response accurately, and explain why integrity matters when other people rely on a professional's information.

**Success criteria:**

- compare at least three careers using correctly labeled preparation and pay evidence;
- use three Clinton Lake files and name an important uncertainty;
- follow the trail simulation boundary or complete the equal observer/documenter route;
- write all five sections of a fictional Patient Care Report; and
- use week evidence to explain one career decision and one integrity risk.

## Source-Control Notes

- Pay figures in the route guide are May 2024 **U.S. medians**, not starting salaries or DFW guarantees. Students may add a current Xello local figure only when they record geography, date, and salary type.
- The current Irving ISD Singley Academy page lists **Law Enforcement** and **Emergency Medical - EMT**. Requirements and certifications must be rechecked before promising a specific credential.
- The trail activity is a classroom simulation, not medical training or certification. Students never practice on an injury. A model, mannequin, or observer/documenter route earns the same credit.
- The Clinton Lake packet does not prove one cause. Strong work distinguishes pollution evidence, possible-source evidence, source limits, and what investigators still need.

## Grading

The Patient Care Report and complication plan form the recommended second **major** of the 2SW block. Score the 16-point durable evidence set with the district bands in the rubric. Do not grade platform access, willingness to be touched, physical technique, artistic presentation, or public speaking. The other artifacts are formative only.

## Access and Participation

- Keep all required directions visible; use Canvas accordions only for optional help.
- Read case files aloud or provide their Canvas text descriptions. Students may annotate digitally or on paper.
- Permit speech-to-text, oral rehearsal, labeled sketches, and teacher-scribed responses when documented.
- Provide the observer/documenter role before students are asked to choose a simulation route.
- An absent student uses the same embedded files and completes written evidence independently. No partner or vendor login is required.
