# Week 4: Dental Science and Health Data

**2nd Six Weeks** | Health Science | 5 class periods (50 minutes each)

## What students will do

Students read dental evidence without diagnosing, design a toothbrush for a specific user, complete two required Xello profile tasks, and try a controlled ICD-10-CM coding simulation using fictional records. Across the week, they build one three-career evidence comparison and use it to make a supported recommendation.

## Learning target

I can compare health careers using preparation, responsibilities, pay, and outlook, then explain which route fits a student's needs.

## Success criteria

- I separate an observation from a diagnosis.
- I label salary figures with the year, geography, and measure.
- I classify careers with the same evidence rule.
- I complete the required Xello tasks or join the supervised catch-up list.
- I support my recommendation with at least two accurate facts.

## Standards

- **d(1)(C):** identify career opportunities in Health Science (Days 1-2).
- **d(2)(A):** describe preparation and training requirements (Days 1, 4-5).
- **d(3)(B):** explore and list current opportunities to earn college credit in high school (Day 3).
- **d(5)(B):** classify high-skill, high-wage, and high-demand evidence using a stated comparison rule (Days 2, 4-5).

## Source and accuracy notes

The core pay figures are May 2024 **U.S. medians** from the Bureau of Labor Statistics. They are not starting pay and are not DFW-localized. Xello may be used for a local figure only when students record the geography, date, and pay measure shown.

The current Irving ISD public page lists **Dental** at Singley Academy. The workbook also introduces Medical Billing and Coding, but the current public page does not verify it as a Singley program. This week therefore teaches medical records and coding as career exploration, not as a district-program promise.

The Day 4 practice set uses the FY 2027 ICD-10-CM diagnosis-code descriptions effective October 1, 2026. It is a small classroom simulation, not medical-coding training or health advice. Every chart is fictional.

## Assessment and grading

Days 1-4 are formative. Day 5 is the recommended **16-point minor checkpoint** once the Canvas Minor assignment group is confirmed. The physical or digital format, handwriting, platform access, and English mechanics are not separate grading criteria. The week does not add another major grade.

## Platform roles

- **Xello:** required Grade 8 completion tasks and localized career data when clearly labeled.
- **Hats & Ladders:** optional career exploration; never the only evidence source.
- **Canvas:** official directions, licensed images, practice quiz, catch-up path, and submission point.
- **Paper:** equal route for observation, design, comparison, and coding work.
