## Learning target

I can record real experiences and identify two current ways a high-school student may earn college credit.

## Before class

Provide **one internet-connected device per student**, **one teacher device with the Xello Completion Standards report open**, and **one teacher display** for the two fixed course cards. Keep **one printed one-page college-credit fallback per student only if Canvas is unavailable**; do not print it by default. This is individual profile work, not a shared-device or team task.

## Lesson flow

### 1. Privacy and honesty check (4 minutes)

Record only an experience that actually happened. Do not invent volunteer hours. Do not include private contact information, client names, medical details, or a public link to a student profile.

### 2. Education experiences (10 minutes)

Open Xello → About Me → Experiences → Education. Add at least one actual education experience and save it. Students who already meet the completion standard verify it and improve a description if useful.

### 3. Volunteer hours (15 minutes)

Open Volunteer experiences and add at least one completed hour. A student with no completed volunteer hour does not fabricate one; the teacher records that student for supervised catch-up after a real experience is available.

### 4. College-credit opportunity response (14 minutes)

Students use two fixed current coursebook cards in Canvas: English III Dual Credit (H) and AP Biology. In the Canvas constructed response, they **list both opportunities**, identify each type, state one requirement or limitation for each, and name one fact to verify with a counselor. The current coursebook says English III Dual Credit (H) is open in grades 10-12 after English II and awards high-school and college credit. It lists AP Biology for grades 11-12 after Biology with Chemistry completed or taken at the same time. An AP course does not guarantee college credit; each receiving institution sets its policy. Industry certification is a valuable credential but is not itself college credit.

Point-of-use frame: **“The source lists ____ as a ____ opportunity. One requirement or limitation is ____.”**

### 5. Verify, submit, and reset (7 minutes)

The Canvas constructed response is the college-credit evidence; do not assign a second exit sheet. Students submit it, confirm Education experiences and Volunteer hours status privately with the teacher, and close Xello. The [printable response](../../resources/exit-tickets/2sw-wk4-day3-add-real-experiences-in-xello.pdf) is an outage/access fallback only.

## Active monitoring move

- **Minute 14 CFU:** verify Education experiences is saved or the student is on the documented recovery route.
- **Minute 29 CFU:** verify Volunteer hours is saved or catch-up is recorded; never direct a student to invent an hour.
- **Lap 3:** check that each constructed response names **both** opportunities and one requirement/limitation for each. If more than one in four students treats AP enrollment as guaranteed college credit, pause and reteach receiving-institution policy before submission.

## Teacher verification and fallback

Use the Xello Completion Standards report, not a public screenshot, to verify completion. A platform outage moves the required task to supervised catch-up. The one-page print check is only a fallback for the college-credit response; it does not replace Xello. Students may use the licensed *My Experiences* guide in authenticated Canvas.
