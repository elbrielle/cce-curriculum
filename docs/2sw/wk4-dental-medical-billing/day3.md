## Learning target

## Before class

Provide **one internet-connected device per student**, **one teacher device with the Xello Completion Standards report open**, and **one teacher display** for the two fixed course cards. Keep **one printed one-page college-credit fallback per student only if Canvas is unavailable**; do not print it by default. This is individual profile work, not a shared-device or team task.

## Lesson flow

### 1. Privacy, honesty, and source check (5 minutes)

Record only an education experience that actually happened. Do not include private contact information, names, medical details, or a public link to a student profile. Preview the two fixed college-credit source cards before students open Xello.

### 2. Education experiences (10 minutes)

Open Xello → About Me → Experiences → Education. Add at least one actual education experience and save it. Students who already meet the completion standard verify it and improve a description if useful.

### 3. School Subjects at Work (30 minutes)

Open **Home → Lessons → School subjects at work**. Complete the lesson's school-subject and career connections. Students may use a current class they take now; they do not need to disclose grades or a private schedule.

### 4. College-credit opportunity response and reset (5 minutes)

Students use two fixed current coursebook cards in Canvas: English III Dual Credit (H) and AP Biology. In the short Canvas response, they **list both opportunities** and give one requirement or limitation for each. The current coursebook says English III Dual Credit (H) is open in grades 10-12 after English II and awards high-school and college credit. It lists AP Biology for grades 11-12 after Biology with Chemistry completed or taken at the same time. An AP course does not guarantee college credit; each receiving institution sets its policy. Industry certification is a valuable credential but is not itself college credit.

Point-of-use frame: **“The source lists ____ as a ____ opportunity. One requirement or limitation is ____.”**

The Canvas constructed response is the college-credit evidence; do not assign a second exit sheet. Students submit it, confirm Education experiences and School subjects at work status privately with the teacher, and close Xello. The [one-page printable response](../../resources/worksheets/2sw-wk4-xello-experiences-checkpoint.pdf) is an outage/access fallback only.
