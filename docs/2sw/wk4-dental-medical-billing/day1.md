## Before class

Open the seven licensed slides in Canvas and the projected career evidence guide. Students write in their assigned FYF workbook on pp. 69-71. The optional observation PDF is an access or no-workbook fallback, not a default class set. Do not call the five images a student's diagnostic record. They are a training image set for noticing patterns. Students describe what they see; they do not diagnose disease.

## Learning target

I can use visible evidence carefully and compare two dental careers without turning an observation into a diagnosis.

## Lesson flow

### 1. Observation or conclusion? (5 minutes)

Show two statements: “I see a bright white area” and “The patient needs a filling.” Students identify which is an observation and explain why the other requires a qualified professional.

### 2. Smile Squad evidence review (18 minutes)

Students use FYF pp. 69-71 and the embedded reference chart. Project X-rays 1-5 one at a time. For each image, students record one visible pattern and the chart category it most resembles. Accept careful uncertainty; an image with no obvious flag is not proof that a person is healthy.

Teacher look-fors:

- X-ray 1: developing teeth; no obvious bright restoration.
- X-ray 2: developing teeth and a darker area that deserves careful review; no student diagnosis.
- X-ray 3: bright restoration/crown pattern and a bright line through one root.
- X-rays 4-5: multiple bright restorations or crown-like areas.

### 3. Make a prevention recommendation (7 minutes)

Students choose one low-risk prevention step supported by the workbook clues: brush with fluoride toothpaste, reduce frequent sugary drinks/snacks, or follow a dental professional's plan. They cite two pieces of evidence.

### 4. Compare two dental careers (15 minutes)

Using the projected evidence guide or the Canvas check, students compare Dental Assistant and Dental Hygienist. They must keep “May 2024,” “U.S.,” and “median” attached to the pay figure. The current 2026-27 district coursebook lists **Health Science: Dental** at Singley and names Registered Dental Assistant as the program certification. Present that as program context, not a promise that every student earns the credential.

### 5. Exit ticket (5 minutes)

**EXIT TICKET** (Mini-Case) · [Printable PDF](../../resources/exit-tickets/2sw-wk4-day1-read-dental-evidence-carefully.pdf):

An image shows a bright white area. A student writes, “This proves the patient has a cavity.” Rewrite the sentence as one accurate observation, then name one dental career that would need training to interpret or act on the evidence.

*(d(1)(C), d(2)(A))*

## Supports and recovery

Provide the adjacent written descriptions for students who cannot access or visually interpret the images. Students may dictate observations. An absent student completes the same Canvas image sequence and evidence guide. H&L is optional.
