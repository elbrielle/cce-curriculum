## Evidence packet: 16 points

Use this rubric to check the Day 2 comparison, Day 3 triage reasoning, and Day 5 pathway recommendation. Xello completion is checked separately and is not scored as a click.

| Criterion | Masters - 4 | Meets - 3 | Approaches - 2 | Needs Improvement - 1 | No evidence - 0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Career and route evidence | All three careers use accurate duties, routes, boundaries, and dated labels | Accurate evidence; one label or detail is thin | Two careers usable; one measure or route confused | Career named with unsupported or mislabeled facts | Blank or off topic |
| Route evaluation | Uses two facts and explains an education/responsibility trade-off | Uses two relevant facts and a clear reason | One fact plus a general preference | Preference with little evidence | Blank or off topic |
| Triage reasoning | Uses two case/reference details, separates appearance from evidence, and states a limit | Uses two relevant details and avoids diagnosis | Uses one relevant detail or weak comparison | Relies on appearance or invents a conclusion | Blank or off topic |
| Pathway and skill connection | Accurate FYF fact, transferable skill, middle-school action, and postsecondary requirement; no outcome promise | Accurate FYF connection and transition; one detail is thin | General pathway or skill connection; one transition step is missing | Program or skill word without an evidence link | Blank or off topic |

## Before you submit

- Keep May 2024 U.S. medians separate from starting or local pay.
- Use two case or reference details in the triage response. Do not diagnose either animal.
- Use FYF pp. 100-101 for the local pathway fact. A pathway builds preparation; it does not guarantee an outcome.
- Use specific evidence. Your pet ownership, speaking confidence, English mechanics, and enthusiasm for a career are not evidence criteria.
