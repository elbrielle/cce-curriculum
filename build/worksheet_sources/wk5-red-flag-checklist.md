You are the cybersecurity analyst. Use this sheet while you work the inbox. Tick a flag every time you spot it.

## The Five Red Flags

- [ ] **Flag 1. Strange sender address.** Letters are swapped, or extra words are added. security@bankofarnerica.com is not bankofamerica.com.
- [ ] **Flag 2. Urgent language.** "Act in 24 hours or your account will be deleted."
- [ ] **Flag 3. Asks for private information.** Your ID number, your card number, your birth date, your password.
- [ ] **Flag 4. Suspicious link or attachment.** The words say Netflix. Hover the link and the real address says streaming-update-alert-44.com.
- [ ] **Flag 5. Spelling or grammar mistakes.** "Dear Valued costumer, your account have been locked."

## Mark Each Email

Write an X under every flag the email triggers. Then circle Safe or Spoofed. An email with no flags is safe. Not every email in this inbox is a trap.
