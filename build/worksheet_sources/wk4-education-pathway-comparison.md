**Compare three ways into an IT career.** Use the dated Route Data Guide. Career 1 is a support role where a high school diploma plus relevant certification may qualify some candidates. Career 2 typically needs an associate degree. Career 3 typically needs a bachelor's degree.

Do not guess costs or promise that one credential guarantees a job. Requirements vary by employer. Use the BLS wording and record the year beside every pay number.
