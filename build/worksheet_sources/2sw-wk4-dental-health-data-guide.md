## Read the label before you compare

The pay figures are **May 2024 U.S. medians from BLS**. A median is the middle wage. These are not starting pay and are not DFW-localized. Outlook is the projected U.S. employment change from 2024-34.

| Career | Common preparation | U.S. median | Outlook | One common responsibility |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| Dental Assistant | Postsecondary nondegree award is typical; requirements vary by state | $47,300 | 6% | Prepare patients, assist with procedures, records, or X-rays when qualified |
| Dental Hygienist | Associate degree is typical; programs often take about 3 years; state license | $94,260 | 7% | Clean teeth, assess oral health, and teach prevention |
| Medical Records Specialist | Postsecondary nondegree award is typical | $50,250 | 7% | Review, classify, and enter health information; some assign clinical codes |

**Do not mix measures.** If Xello shows a local figure, record the geography, date, and type of pay before comparing it.

## Course evidence rule

This is a classroom comparison rule, not an official government label.

- **High-wage evidence:** May 2024 U.S. median is above the same-source all-occupation median of **$49,500**.
- **High-demand evidence:** projected growth is above the same-source all-occupation rate of **3%**.
- **High-skill evidence:** the source shows specialized preparation, licensure/credential, or specialized duties.

[[pagebreak]]

## Use evidence without overclaiming

- “The median is $94,260” does not mean every worker earns that amount.
- “Postsecondary nondegree award” does not guarantee the same program length everywhere.
- Growth above 3% is evidence for this class comparison; it does not prove every local employer is hiring.
- A career page describes a common route. Students should verify current Texas and program requirements before enrolling.
