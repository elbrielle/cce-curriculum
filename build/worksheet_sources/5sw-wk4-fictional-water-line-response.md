## Safety Boundary and Team Jobs

This is a paper/digital communication simulation. Students do not locate utilities, enter a street, direct traffic, shut a valve, excavate, select repair materials, use tools, or write real water-main repair instructions.

Actual response belongs to authorized city/utility personnel and qualified tradespeople following current local procedures.

Team jobs:

- **Safety and communication lead:** records who must be alerted and how people stay outside the supplied hazard area.
- **System-evidence lead:** records the supplied evidence and what the team cannot conclude.
- **Plan and record lead:** sequences the classroom response and documents changes.
