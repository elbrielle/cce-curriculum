## Recommended major evidence packet: 16 points

Score the final Pest Patrol design, revision record, and Trends Evaluation together. Paper, Canva, Adobe Express, or another approved design route is equal. Xello completion, drawing skill, platform access, and public speaking are not separate grades.

| Criterion | Masters - 4 | Meets - 3 | Approaches - 2 | Needs Improvement - 1 | No evidence - 0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Constraint and evidence reasoning | Uses three clear constraints and connects at least three design features to specific field-report evidence | Three constraints and evidence links are present; one link is thin | Two usable constraints or mostly general evidence links | Partial design with little source evidence | Blank or off topic |
| Functional communication | Six or more readable labels explain sensing, movement, reporting, and safety/crop protection | Six labels are present and most functions are clear | Four or five usable labels; one required function is unclear | A drawing is present but the reader must guess how it works | Blank or unusable |
| Revision and tradeoff | Makes a visible evidence-based revision and explains a specific benefit plus cost, risk, or limit | Revision is visible and a reasonable tradeoff is named | Revision or tradeoff is present but weakly explained | Feedback is copied or acknowledged without a useful change | No revision evidence |
| Trend and career evaluation | Compares two trends, uses two accurate sourced facts, explains changed work, and states an evidence limit | Uses two facts and gives a defensible career recommendation | Uses one accurate fact and a general trend connection | Preference is stated with little or inaccurate evidence | Blank or off topic |

## Scoring boundaries

- Score the evidence chain and function, not artistic polish or access to a premium design tool.
- One visible revision is enough when the student can explain why it improves the work.
- Peer feedback is formative. Do not reduce a score because a reviewer missed a problem.
- BLS figures are national medians and projections. Do not reward claims that present them as DFW starting pay or guaranteed outcomes.
- English mechanics do not lower the score unless the meaning cannot be understood.
- Students may revise according to the teacher's normal major-assignment practice.
