## Part 1: Plan from evidence

Design a drone concept that responds to the three field reports in *Find Your Future*. You are designing the **idea and evidence chain**, not a working aircraft.

### Required design evidence

- [ ] At least six labeled features
- [ ] A sensor or camera and what it detects
- [ ] A movement or field-coverage plan
- [ ] A way to report information to the farmer
- [ ] A safety or crop-protection feature
- [ ] Three labels that point back to field-report evidence
- [ ] One tradeoff the design cannot completely avoid
