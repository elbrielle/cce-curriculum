## Fixed Career Evidence

| Field | Current evidence |
|---|---|
| Occupation | Civil Engineer |
| Work | Plans, designs, and supervises construction/maintenance of infrastructure such as roads, bridges, water, and sewage systems |
| Typical entry education | Bachelor's degree in civil engineering or related field |
| May 2024 U.S. median | $99,590 — BLS |
| 2024–34 outlook | 5% growth; about 23,600 openings per year — BLS |
| Licensure boundary | Not universally required for entry-level work; generally required when providing services directly to the public or signing regulated engineering work; state routes vary |
