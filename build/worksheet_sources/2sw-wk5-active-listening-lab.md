## Part 2: Safe workplace listening practice

Choose one fictional card. One person speaks, one listens, and an optional observer records evidence. A written response is equal to acting.

**Card A: Supply handoff.** The morning team says the first-aid cabinet is low on gloves, but the inventory sheet says two boxes remain. The next shift needs a clear count and a safe next step.

**Card B: Appointment mix-up.** A fictional customer says the reminder lists Tuesday, while the office calendar lists Thursday. No real names, phone numbers, or appointment details may be used.

**Card C: Equipment problem.** A coworker says a cart wheel sticks only when the cart is full. The repair request says only “cart broken.” The team needs a better description before routing it.
