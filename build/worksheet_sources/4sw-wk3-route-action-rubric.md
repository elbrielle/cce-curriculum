## 16-point scoring tool

| Criterion | 4 - Masters | 3 - Meets | 2 - Approaches | 1 - Needs Improvement | 0 - Insufficient evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Source accuracy | Facts retain source, access date, geography, and measure; one limitation or uncertainty is named accurately. | Facts retain source/date and use the correct geography or measure label. | Evidence is relevant but one source or label is incomplete. | Uses a vague or unsupported fact as if verified. | Blank, off-topic, or copied without usable evidence. |
| Route reasoning | Direction cites two relevant facts, explains a tradeoff, and names evidence that could change the decision. | Direction cites evidence and names one tradeoff or eligibility question. | Direction has one evidence connection but limited tradeoff reasoning. | Direction is named with little relevant evidence. | No usable direction or reasoning. |
| Timed action plan | Three stages include verbs, completion evidence, realistic time windows, and an honest current/conditional/unverified label. | Three timed stages include actions and completion evidence. | Two usable stages or timing/evidence is incomplete. | One broad action without usable timing. | No actionable plan. |
| Support and revision | Names authorized support, obstacle, equal backup, revision condition, and makes a visible revision after self-scoring. | Names support, obstacle, backup, and revision condition. | Includes some support or backup evidence, but it is broad or incomplete. | Names one vague support or backup. | No support or revision evidence. |

## Scoring boundary

Score private evidence and reasoning. Do not score team model quality, public speaking, H&L ratings, family military background, English mechanics unless meaning is unclear, handwriting, art, or platform access.

Students may choose aviation, another Transportation career, or a current decision not to pursue the cluster. The direction itself is not graded.
