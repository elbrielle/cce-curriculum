This lab has real equipment and real tools. These rules keep you safe and keep the equipment working for every class. Read each rule. Then sign at the bottom and take this page home for a parent or guardian signature.

1. I will use the Chromebook cart the way my teacher shows me. I will put my Chromebook back in the right slot and plug it in.
2. I will open only the websites my teacher assigns. I will close other tabs when my teacher asks.
3. I will take care of all lab equipment. I will not take lab equipment home.
4. I will tell my teacher right away if something is broken, missing, or not working. I will not try to fix it myself.
5. I will keep food and drinks away from all technology.
6. I will keep the walkways clear. My backpack goes where my teacher shows me.
7. I will use the voice level my teacher posts. Voice 0 is silent work. Voice 1 is quiet partner talk. Voice 2 is group work. Voice 3 is presenting.
8. I will wear the safety gear my teacher gives me for hands-on projects, such as safety glasses or gloves.
9. I will use a tool only after my teacher shows me how. I will stay at my own station and keep my hands off other people's tools and projects.
10. I will help clean up my station before the bell.

**If I break a rule:** I may lose lab privileges, and my teacher will contact my family.

**Extra rules for our lab.** Your teacher will tell you these. Write them here.
