## Boundary and Scenario

This is a fictional hazard-identification exercise. It is not commercial-diving, emergency, engineering, or construction guidance. Qualified professionals would create and approve a real plan.

The fictional team must coordinate trained divers, surface support, heavy equipment, power tools, changing visibility, cold conditions, and an evacuation route.

## Evidence Categories

| Evidence category | What the fictional plan should notice |
|---|---|
| People and communication | Trained roles, supervisor, check-ins, clear stop-work signal |
| Breathing and environment | Qualified systems, water conditions, visibility, temperature |
| Tools and equipment | Approved equipment, inspection, controlled use, trained operator |
| Movement and work area | Marked route, entanglement/struck-by risks, controlled zone |
| Emergency planning | Surface support, evacuation route, emergency-equipment location |

Do not invent technical settings or procedures. Mark anything that requires a qualified professional.
