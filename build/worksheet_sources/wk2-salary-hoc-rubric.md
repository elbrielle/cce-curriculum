Score the three Day 2 app records, the Day 3 comparison on page 4, and the Day 5 career-fit reflection together. Four criteria, 5 points each, 20 points total. Circle the descriptor that fits what the student actually submitted. Students analyze the supplied BLS table; they do not need to find three BLS pages.

Student: _______________   Period: ______   Total: ______ / 20   Teacher initials: __________   Date: __________

| Criterion | 1 - Needs Improvement (2 pts or fewer) | 2 - Approaches (3 pts) | 3 - Meets (4 pts) | 4 - Masters (5 pts) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Career Records** (three app careers) | Fewer than two careers have usable evidence. | Three careers are named, but pay labels, tasks, or preparation are mostly missing. | Three records include a task, preparation, and sourced pay; one label may be unclear. | All three records name the app, place, pay measure, date viewed, task, and preparation clearly. |
| **Source Accuracy** (app evidence and supplied BLS table) | Pay or growth claims have no source or confuse national median with local starting pay. | Sources appear, but at least two claims mix the place, measure, or years. | Claims use the correct source and measure; one label or limitation needs repair. | Each claim keeps its source, place, measure, and years; the student names a real limit of the data. |
| **Comparison Reasoning** (page 4) | No supported comparison is present. | Names a career but gives no accurate number or comparison. | Correctly identifies the highest median and fastest growth with numbers. | Correctly compares all three same-source careers, supports a choice with two facts, and explains a data limit. |
| **Career Fit Reflection** (page 5) | No career decision or supporting example is present. | A career is named, but the fit reason or evidence is missing. | All four prompts are answered with a specific example or fact. | The reflection uses a real trait or example, a career task or data fact, and an honest yes/no/not-sure decision. |

**Scoring notes.** Record this as Minor 2 in the 1SW assessment map. When a source label is wrong, point the student back to the app record or the printed BLS source line and allow a correction before entering the score. Code.org completion is supplemental and is not scored.

**Reteach triggers**

- More than a third of students score 1 or 2 on **Source Accuracy**: model the difference between the app's local pay figure and the BLS national median before the next comparison.
- More than a third score 1 or 2 on **Comparison Reasoning**: model one comparison with two numbers from the same table before the next salary activity.
