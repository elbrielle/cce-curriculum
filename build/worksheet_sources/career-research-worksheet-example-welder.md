**Read the example first.** This is how one student filled in the six parts for a Manufacturing career. Then fill in the same six parts for your own career at the bottom.

### Example: Welder

| Part | What the student wrote |
|---|---|
| **1. Name of Career** | Welder |
| **2. What Interests You?** | I like fixing and building things with my hands. I want a job where I can point at what I made at the end of the day. |
| **3. Brief Job Description** | A welder joins pieces of metal using heat. Welders read a drawing, cut and line up the metal, melt the joint, then check the weld for cracks. |
| **4. Education / Training Needed** | High school diploma or GED, plus a welding program at a technical school or community college. Many welders also earn a welding certification. |
| **5. Salary Evidence** | **$51,000 median annual wage** for welders, cutters, solderers, and brazers · United States · U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook · May 2024. This is a national median, not local or starting pay. |
| **6. What Tools, Equipment, or Skills are Needed for This Career?** | Welding machine and torch, welding helmet with a dark shield, fire resistant gloves and jacket, clamps, grinder, and a tape measure. Skills: steady hands, reading a blueprint, careful measuring, and always thinking about safety. |
