## Published Course Rule and Fixed Evidence

These are classroom comparison labels, not official government designations.

- **High-skill:** documented apprenticeship, moderate/long-term OJT, postsecondary award, or regulated license/credential boundary.
- **High-wage:** May 2024 U.S. median above $49,500.
- **High-demand for this comparison:** 2024–34 growth above 3.1%.

| Occupation | U.S. median | Growth | Annual openings | Preparation summary |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Electricians | $62,350 | 9% | 81,000 | HS typical; most learn through apprenticeship; Texas license boundary |
| Plumbers, Pipefitters, and Steamfitters | $62,970 | 4% | 44,000 | HS typical; most learn through apprenticeship; Texas registration/license boundary |
| HVAC Mechanics and Installers | $59,810 | 8% | 40,100 | Postsecondary nondegree award typical; long-term OJT; Texas technician/contractor boundary |
| Welders, Cutters, Solderers, and Brazers | $51,000 | 2% | 45,600 | HS typical; technical and OJT routes; employer/process credentials vary |

Source basis: May 2024 U.S. medians and 2024–34 national projections, BLS. Openings include replacement needs and do not prove a DFW shortage.
