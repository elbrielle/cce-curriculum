## Use the current district pages

Pathway names, campuses, courses, applications, and student organizations can change. Use the current Irving ISD High School CTE pages and the current 2026-2027 coursebook. Do not use an old poster as the only source.

### Where to look

1. Open Irving ISD **High School CTE**.
2. Open the campus page for Irving High, MacArthur, Nimitz, Singley Academy, or Cardwell.
3. Use the **2026-2027 High School Course Work** page to check one possible course or prerequisite.
4. Record a question for a counselor when a campus, course, transportation route, or application step is unclear.

Singley Academy is application-based. Other travel, transfer, or center arrangements must be confirmed with a counselor. A pathway page does not guarantee admission, transportation, a credential, or a job.

## 3. Career and Technical Student Organizations

The current TEA page lists state-recognized CTSOs as part of CTE programs. The examples below are the organizations most directly connected to the career areas in this guide; the state list also includes additional organizations. CTSOs can provide leadership practice, projects, competitions, service, and contact with people who share a career interest. Local chapter availability varies.
