Use this card when a CCE task lasts more than one class, when you need to recover missed work, or when a source has facts you will use later. Keep the work in your engineering notebook, workbook, or teacher-designated digital folder. This card is a guide, not another assignment.

## Plan > Do > Recover > Reflect

| Move | Ask yourself | Record |
|---|---|---|
| **Plan** | What is the evidence job? What do I already have? | One next action and when I will do it |
| **Do** | What part can I finish now? | The work, source, or checkpoint I completed |
| **Recover** | Was I absent, offline, blocked, or unfinished? | The exact page, person, or catch-up route I will use |
| **Reflect** | What helped? What got in the way? | One change I will make next time |

## Locate and Use Check

- [ ] I can locate the current CCE page, file, notebook entry, or workbook page.
- [ ] I can open it and point to the evidence I need.
- [ ] I can use that evidence in today's task.
- [ ] I know the recovery route if something is missing.

The check is about finding and using your work. Binder style, color coding, handwriting, and purchased supplies are not the score.

## Career Evidence Notes

1. **Capture:** Write two or three useful facts in short phrases.
2. **Label:** Keep the source, date or data year, geography, and measure with the fact when they matter.
3. **Question:** Name one point of confusion, missing fact, or limit.
4. **Use:** Apply one fact to a comparison, design decision, explanation, or recommendation.

**Example:** Capture: Median pay is $48,000. Label: BLS, U.S., May 2024, median annual wage. Question: Is the Dallas-area figure different? Use: I can compare the pay with the training time before recommending this route.

**Remember:** Notes earn their place when you use them.
