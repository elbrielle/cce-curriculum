## Student-visible 16-point rubric

| Criterion | Masters: 4 | Meets: 3 | Approaches: 2 | Needs Improvement: 1 | Insufficient evidence: 0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Visible evidence and boundary | Exact visible condition; unsupported conclusion clearly rejected; authorized next evidence named and explained | Visible condition and boundary are accurate; next evidence named | Mostly accurate, but boundary or next evidence is general | Observation and conclusion are blurred or important boundary is missing | Blank, off-topic, or unsafe unsupported claim remains |
| Three-career comparison | All 3 medians correctly labeled/ranked; direction uses 2 relevant facts; limitation explained | All 3 medians compared with labels, 2 relevant facts, and one limitation | All 3 appear, but label, evidence connection, or limitation is incomplete | Fewer than 3 accurate careers or mixed salary measures | Blank, off-topic, or figures are invented |
| Training-route evaluation | Route recommendation uses 2 source facts, advantage, tradeoff, missing fact, and authorized verification source | Uses 2 facts plus tradeoff and verification need | Uses some evidence, but tradeoff or verification is weak | Preference with little accurate route evidence | Blank, off-topic, or guarantees unsupported outcome |
| Recommendation and next action | Career direction connects 2 facts to a priority; limitation and specific seven-day action with evidence; visible revision | Recommendation, limitation, next action, and revision are present | Direction is plausible, but connection or action evidence is general | Mostly preference; action, limit, or revision missing | Blank, off-topic, or no usable evidence |

## Scoring notes

- Score evidence and reasoning, not the student's career preference.
- Do not score public speaking, drawing, vehicle experience, platform access, handwriting, or English mechanics unless meaning is unclear.
- Text, speech-to-text, private media, Canvas annotation, upload, and paper are equal evidence routes.
- Recommended conversion only after local approval: 15-16 Masters; 13-14 Meets; 12 Approaches; 10-11 Needs Improvement; below 10 follows campus policy.
