## One current planning scenario

**Source checked August 8, 2026:** MIT Living Wage Calculator, Dallas County, Texas. The source page says its data were updated February 15, 2026.

This classroom scenario describes **one adult with no children**. MIT estimates that this household needs about:

- **$48,489 per year before taxes**;
- **$41,399 per year after taxes**; and
- **$23.31 per hour** as a living wage for a full-time worker.

For classroom math, round the after-tax amount to **$3,450 per month**. This is not a promise, a starting salary, or personal tax advice. Costs change with family size, housing choice, health needs, transportation, debt, and location.

## Rounded monthly cost categories

| Category | MIT annual estimate | Classroom monthly amount |
|---|---:|---:|
| Food | $4,019 | $335 |
| Medical | $3,032 | $253 |
| Housing | $17,805 | $1,484 |
| Transportation | $8,440 | $703 |
| Civic | $2,583 | $215 |
| Internet and mobile | $1,454 | $121 |
| Other | $4,067 | $339 |
| **Total** | **about $41,400** | **$3,450** |

**Civic costs** include ordinary participation in community life. **Other** includes necessary personal and household expenses that do not fit the categories above. This table does not create a separate savings target or debt payment.

## Read the number correctly

Attach all four labels whenever you use a figure:

1. **Place:** Dallas County, Texas
2. **Household:** one adult, no children
3. **Date:** MIT page updated February 15, 2026
4. **Measure:** living-cost estimate, not guaranteed pay

## Use a local career figure carefully

If Xello shows a salary, keep the geography, date, occupation, and measure visible. Do not change a median or salary range into “starting pay.” H&L may support career exploration, but it is not the required source for this budget.

## Entrepreneur connection

A business owner cannot assume every dollar of sales is personal income.

**Revenue** = all money the business receives.

**Business expenses** may include supplies, equipment, rent, insurance, fees, marketing, payroll, and taxes.

**Profit** = revenue minus business expenses.

An owner builds a personal budget from money actually available after business costs, not from total sales.
