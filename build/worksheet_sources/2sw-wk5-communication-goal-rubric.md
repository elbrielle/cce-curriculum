## Recommended minor checkpoint: 16 points

| Criterion | Masters - 4 | Meets - 3 | Approaches - 2 | Needs Improvement - 1 | No evidence - 0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SMART goal and time plan | Complete, feasible goal; protected time and useful backup | Goal and time plan complete; one detail thin | Goal partly measurable or time plan incomplete | Vague wish or unrealistic plan | Blank/off topic |
| Practiced communication evidence | Specific action and evidence from two week activities | Specific action and evidence from one activity | Skill named with general evidence | Skill word only | Blank/off topic |
| Transfer across careers | Accurately compares how one skill works in two careers and what changes | Two careers and actions are clear | Two careers named; comparison thin | One career only | Blank/off topic |
| Reflection and next action | Uses a result pattern plus a specific, controllable next action | Clear reflection and next action | General reflection or action | Preference without evidence | Blank/off topic |

## Calibration notes

- Score the evidence, not accent, performance confidence, handwriting, hashtags, or English mechanics unless meaning is unclear.
- A student may use the private or written route and earn full credit.
- Do not require students to disclose personal assessment results, personal health information, or a public SMART goal.
- Xello platform completion is checked in the Completion Standards report. A paper reflection supports learning but does not replace the required save.
- If CareerOneStop is unavailable, score the fixed sample-skill fallback with the same transfer criterion.
