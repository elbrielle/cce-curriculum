## 1. Rate the evidence, not the effort

| Criterion | Masters | Meets | Approaches | Needs Improvement | My rating |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Texture reads clearly | clear from 3 feet; details support it | main texture is clear | texture is partly clear | texture is hard to identify | |
| Layers or pieces hold | all remain aligned or attached | most remain aligned or attached | some shift or flatten | most shift, flatten, or detach | |
| Plan matches build | build follows the map and labels | most choices match | several choices changed without notes | plan and build do not yet connect | |
