## 16-point scoring tool

| Criterion | 4 - Masters | 3 - Meets | 2 - Approaches | 1 - Needs Improvement | 0 - Insufficient evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Design reasoning | Connects a specific user need, component job, environmental constraint, tradeoff, and evidence-based redesign. | Connects user need, component, and constraint with a defensible explanation. | Design response is relevant but one evidence connection is incomplete. | Names a feature with little connection to the mission. | Blank, off-topic, or copied without reasoning. |
| Occupation and classification evidence | Uses exact occupation, source/date/geography/measure, preparation/duty evidence, all three rule decisions, annual openings, and one limitation. | Uses accurate occupation and source labels with evidence for all three classifications. | Relevant occupation evidence is present but labels or one classification are incomplete. | Uses vague “drone career” claims or unsupported salary/growth. | No usable occupation evidence. |
| Rule and safety accuracy | Correctly distinguishes indoor, outdoor educational, and Part 107 boundaries; names current source/authorized person and campus/model check. | Correctly applies or verifies the selected scenario rule and names a safety check. | General rule direction is plausible but one boundary or verification step is unclear. | Treats one rule as universal or confuses a classroom checklist with flight authorization. | No usable rule/safety reasoning. |
| Test-based revision and transfer | Uses a specific result to justify one-variable revision, names next evidence, and transfers one skill across two occupations. | Uses test evidence to justify a revision and connects one skill to two occupations. | Revision or transfer is relevant but weakly tied to evidence. | Names a change or skill with little evidence. | No test/revision/transfer evidence. |

## Scoring boundary

Score private evidence and reasoning. Do not score live flight, hardware access, speed, artistic quality, public speaking, H&L activity, English mechanics unless meaning is unclear, or submission mode.
