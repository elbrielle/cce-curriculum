The Hats & Ladders app sorts Manufacturing careers into six pathways, with one example career printed for each. As you explore the app, add one more career you find to the last column.

| Pathway | What people do in it | Example career | A career I found |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Robotics and Automation Technology** | Build, fix, and run robots and machines. | Automation Specialist | |
| **2. Industrial Maintenance** | Fix and take care of machines in factories and buildings, using welding, plumbing, and electrical skills. | Mechatronics Technician | |
| **3. Manufacturing Technology** | Use machines and plastic to make parts, and fix the equipment that makes them. | Assembler and Fabricator | |
| **4. Electronics Technology** | Learn how electricity works and fix electrical systems, circuits, and wiring. | Electrical and Electronics Repairer | |
| **5. Welding** | Join metal and plastic parts together using heat. | Welder | |
| **6. Advanced Manufacturing and Industrial Technology** | Work with high-tech equipment like CNC machines, robots, and computers to shape metal. | CNC Machinist | |
