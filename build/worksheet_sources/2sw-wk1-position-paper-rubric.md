Score the student's final policy position and Legal Entrepreneur Card as one 16-point evidence set. Spoken debate performance and technology access are not graded.

| Criterion | 4 - Masters | 3 - Meets | 2 - Approaches | 1 - Needs Improvement | 0 - No evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Position and reasoning | Clear decision with a thoughtful explanation of why | Clear decision with a reasonable explanation | Decision is present; explanation is general or incomplete | Position is difficult to identify or mostly unsupported | Blank/off-topic |
| Evidence and tradeoff | Uses two accurate details and addresses a meaningful benefit, risk, or opposing idea | Uses two accurate details to support the position | Uses one accurate detail or lists evidence without explaining it | Uses little or inaccurate evidence | Blank/off-topic |
| Career and professional connection | Accurately explains how one legal career and one safeguard/concern connect to the policy | Names a fitting legal career and explains the connection | Names a career but connection is general | Career is named without a relevant connection | Blank/off-topic |
| Entrepreneur and association card | Complete, plausible business/service, startup need, risk/reward, and relevant association with source/date | Most fields complete and connections are reasonable | Several accurate fields; one important connection is missing | Minimal or largely unsupported entries | Blank/off-topic |

## Administration Notes

- Students may argue either side. Score the evidence and reasoning, not agreement with the teacher.
- A written or recorded response may demonstrate the same target when an accommodation requires it.
- Do not reduce the score for English mechanics unless meaning is unclear.
- Use the same controlled evidence bank for absent students in a supervised catch-up block.
