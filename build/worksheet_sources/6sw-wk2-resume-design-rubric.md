## Four-Criterion Rubric

| Criterion | 4 — Masters | 3 — Meets | 2 — Approaches | 1 — Needs Improvement | 0 — Insufficient Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Truthful, privacy-safe résumé | Standard headings; specific true evidence; consistent details; no sensitive data | Complete and accurate with a minor clarity gap | Several sections usable but one major evidence gap | Vague, inconsistent, or unsafe information needs repair | Blank, off-topic, or no usable résumé |
| Xello completion and visible revision | Required Xello Resume is complete and clear before/after evidence shows a meaningful accuracy or specificity repair | Xello Resume is complete with a useful visible revision | Platform completion or revision evidence has one major gap | Completion is unresolved and revision is vague or unsupported | No usable evidence |
| Safe seven-step job search | Target, credible sources, screening, tracking, tailoring, authorized application route, and follow-up are complete and privacy safe | All seven steps are present with one minor clarity gap | One or two steps or safety checks need correction | Several steps are missing or depend on unsafe/unverified action | Blank, off-topic, or no usable plan |
| Tailored evidence and next action | A true résumé bullet is tailored to the fictional opportunity, supported by evidence, and paired with a safe specific next action | Tailored evidence and next action are mostly specific | Bullet or next action is general or partly supported | Generic trait/platform click with little evidence | Blank or off-topic |

Total: ____ / 16

This is Minor 2 in the approved sixth-six-weeks assessment map. Merch Mode design is formative and does not add a scored criterion. Canvas records the grade out of 100: add the four ratings, divide by 16, multiply by 100, and round to the nearest whole point.

## Submission Check

- [ ] private résumé PDF/document or completed résumé packet;
- [ ] Xello Resume completion verified or supervised recovery recorded;
- [ ] résumé before-and-after revision record;
- [ ] seven-step fictional job-search plan and tracker;
- [ ] tailored truthful résumé bullet and authorized next action;
- [ ] no personal address, phone, personal email, identifiers, references, or unauthorized contact data;
- [ ] no Xello contact details or profile screenshot copied into the submission.

Submission route: one combined Canvas upload / typed response under the exact rubric labels / one labeled paper bundle / authorized accommodation
