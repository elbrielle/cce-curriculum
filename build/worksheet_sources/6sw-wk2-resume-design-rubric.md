## Four-Criterion Rubric

| Criterion | 4 — Masters | 3 — Meets | 2 — Approaches | 1 — Needs Improvement | 0 — Insufficient Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Truthful, privacy-safe résumé | Standard headings; specific true evidence; consistent details; no sensitive data | Complete and accurate with a minor clarity gap | Several sections usable but one major evidence gap | Vague, inconsistent, or unsafe information needs repair | Blank, off-topic, or no usable résumé |
| Attention to detail and revision | Clear before/after evidence shows a meaningful accuracy or specificity repair | Visible useful revision with explanation | Minor revision or weak explanation | Claims revision without visible evidence | No revision evidence |
| Design reasoning and originality | Original solution uses all five principles, audience evidence, test result, and revision | Coherent original design with four or more principles and revision | Understandable design with partial principle evidence | Decoration or copied convention with little reasoning | Blank, copied, or no usable evidence |
| Career and job-search connection | Accurate career duty/source limit, tailored résumé bullet, and seven-step search reasoning | Complete connection with one minor gap | General connection or one major job missing | Preference or platform clicks without evidence | Blank or off-topic |
