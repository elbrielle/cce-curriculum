## Fixed Career and Association Bank

Use these as starting points. Membership, certification, and a license are different things. Joining an association does not replace the education, examination, or state requirements for a career.

### Career options

| Career | Possible independent work | Boundary to remember |
|---|---|---|
| Lawyer | Solo practice or a small law firm serving clients in an area of law | A lawyer must meet the requirements to practice law in the jurisdiction. |
| Paralegal or legal assistant | Contract document, research, or case-support services for attorneys or firms | A paralegal supports legal work and does not independently provide legal advice as an attorney. Employer requirements vary. |
| Court reporter or simultaneous captioner | Freelance reporting, deposition, captioning, or transcription services | State requirements and the type of work vary. Equipment, accuracy, and confidentiality matter. |

### Professional association options

| Association | Career connection | Support it provides |
|---|---|---|
| **State Bar of Texas** | Lawyers practicing in Texas | Regulates the profession in Texas and provides ethics, education, and practice resources. |
| **NALA — The Paralegal Association** | Paralegals and legal assistants | Certification, professional development, advocacy, networking, and career resources. |
| **National Court Reporters Association (NCRA)** | Court reporters, captioners, and legal videographers | Education, certification, professional development, networking, and industry resources. |

### Source record

- State Bar of Texas, official website, checked August 2026: **texasbar.com**
- NALA, official website, checked August 2026: **nala.org**
- NCRA, official website, checked August 2026: **ncra.org**
