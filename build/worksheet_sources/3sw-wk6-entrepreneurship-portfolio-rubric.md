Use this rubric before submitting the individual Entrepreneurship Portfolio. Group pitch participation supports the evidence, but a student's score does not depend on another student's attendance or speaking performance. Score reasoning and evidence, not art polish, accent, handwriting, or English mechanics unless meaning is unclear.

| Criterion | 4 - Masters | 3 - Meets | 2 - Approaches | 1 - Needs Improvement | 0 - No evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Problem and opportunity | Defines entrepreneurship and explains a specific problem, customer, offer, and owner responsibility in a field of interest | Explains the problem, customer, offer, and responsibility | Names an idea but one part is broad or missing | Idea and problem connection is unclear | Blank or off topic |
| Test and decision | Compares two ideas with all three tests and makes a well-supported call using a strength, risk, and specific evidence | Tests two ideas and supports a call with strength and risk | Tests are incomplete or the call uses general reasoning | Decision has little connection to the evidence | Blank or off topic |
| Venture and work ethic | Uses the venture brief and an individual action to explain how a professional quality matters for owners and employees | Completes the venture evidence and explains one professional action | Names a quality but evidence or comparison is thin | Group product is present but individual evidence is unclear | Blank or off topic |
| Personal budget reasoning | Produces a balanced revised budget, keeps source labels attached, shows one calculation, and correctly separates revenue from personal income | Produces a balanced budget and explains one choice with correct labels | Budget is mostly complete but has a calculation or reasoning gap | Budget is incomplete or uses unlabeled figures | Blank or off topic |
