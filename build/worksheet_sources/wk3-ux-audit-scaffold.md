Two of your five problems are started for you. Finish those two on the site you are auditing, then find three more on your own.

A problem is not "it is ugly." A problem is something that stopped you from getting a task done.

**Model.** Here is one problem written the short way, then the same problem written the way a UX Designer writes it. This model is about a movie theater website, not the site you are auditing.

- Short way: "The menu is bad."
- UX Designer way: "The menu says Info and More. I wanted showtimes, and I had to click both before I found them."
