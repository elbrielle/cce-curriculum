**How to use.** Before class, write the week's three Active Monitoring checkpoints on the three lines below, then carry this page on a clipboard and mark each student as you walk your monitoring route. Print double-sided so the whole roster stays on one sheet. Example, 1SW Week 1: (1) all team members participating, (2) a Manufacturing career named in the team's talk, (3) Engineering Design Process steps visible in how the team iterates.

**How to mark.** A check for met, a dash for not yet, a circle for a student you will come back to. Use Notes for the one thing you will say to that student next.
