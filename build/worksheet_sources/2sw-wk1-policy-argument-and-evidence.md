## Hypothetical Policy for Review

A court may use an AI tool to recommend bail amounts and flag which cases a judge should review first. A judge may follow the recommendation or set it aside. The tool's scoring method is not released to the public.

This is a classroom scenario. Do not connect the discussion to a real person, case, or family.

## Evidence Bank

| Evidence that may support approval | Evidence that may support opposition or revision |
|---|---|
| The tool could sort information consistently and bring urgent files to a judge's attention. | The public cannot inspect the scoring method, so an error may be hard to identify or challenge. |
| A judge keeps final authority and may reject the recommendation. | A person may give too much weight to a computer recommendation even when a judge has final authority. |
| A tool could help staff organize a large number of cases. | A recommendation may repeat patterns found in the information used to build the tool. |
