## Minor 2 Rubric - 16 Points

This rubric scores only the Day 2 assessment-impact and emerging-specialty evidence. Bridge building, fabrication success, team ranking, and the Mars design are formative and do not affect this grade.

| Criterion | 4 - Masters | 3 - Meets | 2 - Approaches | 1 - Needs Improvement | 0 - Insufficient Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Assessment impact and boundary | Accurately explains one possible pathway impact and what the assessment result does not decide by itself | Accurate impact and boundary with one minor gap | Impact or boundary is partial or too general | Treats a score as a guarantee or gives little usable explanation | Blank, off-topic, or no assessment evidence |
| Verification question and source | Writes a specific current-policy question and names the authorized source that should answer it | Useful question and source with one minor specificity gap | Question or source is present but not both usable | Vague question or an unreliable/unrelated source | Blank or no verification route |
| Emerging-specialty comparison | Accurately compares Transportation and Water/Wastewater Engineers using work, driver/change, and preparation context | Both specialties are compared with one minor evidence gap | Comparison is mostly descriptive or one specialty is incomplete | One title is named with little supported comparison | Blank, invented occupation, or no usable comparison |
| Judgment and evidence limitation | Makes a defensible fictional community recommendation using a source detail and explains a meaningful limitation or reason the evidence may change | Supported judgment and limitation with one minor gap | Judgment uses limited evidence or gives a general limitation | Preference is stated without evidence or changing conditions are treated as certain | Blank, off-topic, or no supported judgment |

**Total:** ____ / 16

Text, speech-to-text, Canvas annotation, upload, and paper are equal evidence routes. Score current-source reasoning, not test score, bridge fabrication, team performance, platform access, drawing, or English mechanics unless meaning is unclear.
