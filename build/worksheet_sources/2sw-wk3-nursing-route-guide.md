## Read the label before you compare

The pay figures below are **May 2024 U.S. medians from the Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS)**. A median is the middle wage. These figures are not starting pay and are not DFW-localized. In Xello, record a local figure only when the screen shows the geography, date, and type of pay.

## Four nursing-related routes

| Career | Common preparation route | May 2024 U.S. median | One common responsibility |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Nursing assistant | State-approved education program; state competency or certification requirements vary | $39,530 | Measure vital signs and report patient concerns to a nurse |
| LVN | State-approved vocational nursing program, typically about 1 year, then licensure | $62,340 | Provide basic medical care and document patient status |
| RN | Approved nursing program through an associate, bachelor's, or diploma route, then RN licensure | $93,600 | Assess patients, coordinate care, and monitor equipment |
| Nurse practitioner | RN preparation plus graduate education and advanced-practice licensure | $129,210 | Assess, diagnose, and manage patient care within state scope |

**Important:** These jobs do not have identical scopes of practice. More education changes responsibility, not only pay.

## Texas route check

Texas students should verify that a vocational or registered nursing program is approved by the Texas Board of Nursing before enrolling. Graduates of approved programs apply for the appropriate licensure examination. LVN graduates use the NCLEX-PN route; RN graduates use the NCLEX-RN route.
