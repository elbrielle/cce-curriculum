## Part 1: Plan the dish

Use *Find Your Future* pp. 112-113. Choose four or five base ingredients. A partner chooses one special ingredient. If no partner is available, use the teacher's ingredient card.

Base ingredients:

[[lines: 2]]

How will the special ingredient change the flavor, texture, color, or presentation?

[[lines: 3]]

How will the dish be prepared? Name at least two steps.

[[lines: 4]]

### Food-safety boundary

This is a design activity. Do not prepare or taste food in class. Avoid allergy claims. A menu description may name ingredients but should not promise that a dish is safe for every customer.
