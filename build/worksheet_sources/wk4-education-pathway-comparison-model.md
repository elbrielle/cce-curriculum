**Column 1 is filled in for you as a model.** Read it first. Then fill in Column 2 with an associate degree career and Column 3 with a bachelor's degree career.

Use the Hats & Ladders app for the career, the CompTIA Career Roadmap for the certification, and the BLS website for the salary. Rough numbers are fine.

| Field | Career 1: certification only | Career 2: associate degree + cert | Career 3: bachelor's degree |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Career Name** | Help Desk Technician | | |
| **Required Education** | High school diploma plus the CompTIA A+ certification | | |
| **Time to Complete** | 2 to 6 months of study after high school | | |
| **Estimated Cost** | About $250 to $500 in exam fees | | |
| **Median Salary (BLS)** | About $60,000 a year. DFW starting pay is closer to $40,000 to $50,000. | | |

**Where to find each number**

- **Required Education:** the education indicator on the Hats & Ladders Hat profile.
- **Time to Complete:** months for a certification, 2 years for an associate degree, 4 years for a bachelor's degree.
- **Estimated Cost:** certifications run about $200 to $1,000. An associate degree at a community college runs about $5,000 to $15,000. A bachelor's degree runs $40,000 and up.
- **Median Salary:** the BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook page for that job.
