## Page 1 - Four-Criterion Feedback Profile

| Criterion | 4 - Masters | 3 - Meets | 2 - Approaches | 1 - Needs Improvement | 0 - Insufficient Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Career content | Accurate career, duty/work product, preparation, and bounded conclusion | Accurate content with one minor gap | Two accurate elements; important gap | Mostly vague or unsupported | Blank/off-topic |
| Source and labor evidence | Correct measure, amount, geography, date/source, and limitation | Correct label with one minor gap | Figure present but label/limit incomplete | Figure relabeled or unsupported | No evidence |
| Organization and audience | Clear opening, logical sequence, concise explanation, and effective close | Organized with one minor lapse | Understandable but uneven or over/under time | Hard to follow or largely read without audience awareness | No assessable brief |
| Oral/AAC communication, technology, and revision | Understandable pace/volume or effective AAC, appropriate technology supports the career brief, visible practiced revision, 60-90 seconds | Clear communication and technology choice with one minor gap | Meaning is understandable with support; technology or revision evidence is partial | Communication frequently obscures meaning or technology/revision evidence is missing | No oral/AAC evidence unless accommodation changes task |

Total: ____ / 16

This is formative Week 4 feedback for the Week 5 interview and Week 6 capstone. It is not another grade.

Score evidence and meaning. Do not score accent, speech difference, disability, camera use, eye contact, public-speaking confidence, clothing cost/style, or English mechanics unless meaning is unclear.

**Submission check:** Recorded route uploads the two-page brief, this rubric/self-score, and private audio/video together. Live, conference, or AAC route submits the brief/rubric while the teacher records student, career, date, route, seconds, technology used, oral/AAC complete, and written record complete on the class checkoff. Text alone is not oral evidence. Earlier FYF work and companions stay in place.
