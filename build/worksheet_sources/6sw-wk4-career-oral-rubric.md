## Four-Criterion Rubric

| Criterion | 4 - Masters | 3 - Meets | 2 - Approaches | 1 - Needs Improvement | 0 - Insufficient Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Career content | Accurate career, duty/work product, preparation, and bounded conclusion | Accurate content with one minor gap | Two accurate elements; important gap | Mostly vague or unsupported | Blank/off-topic |
| Source and labor evidence | Correct measure, amount, geography, date/source plus limitation | Correct label with one minor gap | Figure present but label/limit incomplete | Figure relabeled or unsupported | No evidence |
| Organization and audience | Clear opening, logical sequence, concise explanation, effective close | Organized with one minor lapse | Understandable but uneven or over/under time | Hard to follow or largely read without audience awareness | No assessable brief |
| Oral/AAC communication and revision | Understandable pace/volume or effective AAC, practiced revision is visible, 60-90 seconds | Clear communication with one minor gap | Meaning is understandable with support; revision partial | Communication frequently obscures meaning or no revision evidence | No oral/AAC evidence unless accommodation changes task |

This is formative Week 4 rehearsal for the Week 5 and Week 6 majors. Use it for feedback and revision, not as another grade.

## Access and Scoring Boundary

Equivalent oral routes: whole group, small group, teacher conference, recorded audio/video, or AAC/speech-generating device. Accent, speech difference, disability, camera use, eye contact, public-speaking confidence, clothing cost/style, and English mechanics are not scored unless meaning is unclear. Note cards and a transcript may support access.
