Pick one career. The BLS evidence below is dated and uses one national measure: **May 2024 median annual pay** and **projected employment growth from 2024 to 2034**. A median is not starting pay, and a national number is not a DFW salary.

An **exact match** means the BLS occupation closely matches the title. A **proxy** is a broader occupation used because BLS does not report the selected title separately. A proxy can add context, but its numbers do not describe the exact title. A missing BLS title does not prove that a career is new.

## Evidence Cards

### AI or Machine Learning Engineer — PROXY

<p><strong>BLS occupation:</strong> Computer and Information Research Scientists<br><strong>Typical preparation:</strong> At least a master's degree is typical; some federal roles may accept a bachelor's degree.<br><strong>May 2024 U.S. median:</strong> $140,910<br><strong>Projected growth, 2024-34:</strong> 20%<br><strong>Why the work is changing:</strong> BLS connects demand to research that turns ideas into new computing technology. The source does not report AI Engineer separately.<br><strong>Source:</strong> bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/computer-and-information-research-scientists.htm</p>

### Cloud Architect — PROXY

<p><strong>BLS occupation:</strong> Computer Network Architects<br><strong>Typical preparation:</strong> Bachelor's degree plus related work experience is typical.<br><strong>May 2024 U.S. median:</strong> $130,390<br><strong>Projected growth, 2024-34:</strong> 12%<br><strong>Why the work is changing:</strong> BLS identifies cloud-computing expansion and AI-related infrastructure upgrades as demand drivers. The source covers network architects broadly.<br><strong>Source:</strong> bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/computer-network-architects.htm</p>

## Source Rules

- Record the BLS occupation, not only the career title you selected.
- Copy the year with every pay number and the year range with every growth rate.
- Label exact match, closest occupation, or proxy.
- Explain the changing technology, demand, or work task. Do not use a missing title as the only reason.
- Xello may be used as an optional DFW salary cross-check when the exact career, geography, pay measure, and date are visible. Keep it in a separate local-evidence note; do not replace or relabel the national BLS number.

**Source check:** U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Outlook Handbook pages above, accessed August 9, 2026.
