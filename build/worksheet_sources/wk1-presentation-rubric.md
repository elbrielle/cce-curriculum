Score the Day 5 team presentation on four criteria, 5 points each, 20 points total. Circle the descriptor that fits what the team actually presented.

Team: _______________   Period: ______   Total: ______ / 20   Teacher initials: __________   Date: __________

| Criterion | 1 Beginning (2 pts or fewer) | 2 Developing (3 pts) | 3 Proficient (4 pts) | 4 Mastery (5 pts) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Problem Diagnosis** (the two problems, named and explained) | One fault named, or the two faults are blended into a single problem. | Both faults named. Explanations stay general, such as "the robot was broken." | Both faults named and explained, but at least one explanation rests on a guess instead of a clue. | Both faults named as Color Confusion and Slowpoke Robot, each explained with a specific clue from the shift notes or the machine reference page. |
| **Solution Choice and Reasoning** (the solution chosen for each and why) | A solution for only one fault, or a solution that does not match the fault. | A solution named for both faults with little or no reasoning. | A solution named per fault with a reason, but no other idea is compared against it. | A solution named per fault, plus which idea it beat and why, using a reason a factory would accept: cost, downtime, or safety. |
| **Repair Plan Detail** (steps, tools or adjustments, time estimate, effect on production) | One part present, or steps listed with no tools, no time, and no production effect. | Two or three parts present. The plan could not be handed to a technician and followed. | All four parts present, but one is thin or is given for only one of the two faults. | All four parts present for both faults: ordered steps, named tools or adjustments, a time estimate, and a stated effect on production. |
| **Career Connection and Delivery** (one Manufacturing career the team would pursue, presented in 2 minutes) | No Manufacturing career named, or the presentation does not reach the audience. | A career named but not connected to the plan, or one or two students carry the whole presentation. | A specific Hat named and tied to the work. One member does not speak, or the team runs long. | A specific Manufacturing Hat named and tied to work the team just did. Every member speaks, the team stays inside 2 minutes, and the audience can follow the plan. |

**Scoring notes.** Record the total as the week's summative score. A team that scores 1 or 2 on Repair Plan Detail rewrites the plan table on FYF p. 203 before the score is entered, because that table is the artifact the criterion measures.


**Reteach triggers**

- More than a third of teams score 1 or 2 on Problem Diagnosis: reteach reading the shift notes for evidence before the next case activity.
- More than a third score 1 or 2 on Career Connection and Delivery: build 60 seconds of rehearsal time into the next presentation day.
