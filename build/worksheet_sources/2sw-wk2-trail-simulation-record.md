## Simulation Boundary

This is career role-play, **not first-aid certification or real treatment**. Never practice on a real injury. Do not straighten, pull, reduce, or force any body part. Use a mannequin, dowel/paper model, or a relaxed uninjured partner only with consent. The observer/documenter route earns the same credit.

Circle your route: **model/mannequin** · **consenting uninjured partner** · **observer/documenter**

Stop immediately for pain, numbness, tingling, color change, distress, or a request to stop. A real injury goes to the nurse/911 process—not this lesson.

## Supply Purpose

| Item | Purpose shown in the Climber Notes deck | Safety check during the simulation |
|---|---|---|
| Triangular bandage / sling | Supports an arm and limits movement | Loose, comfortable, consent maintained |
| Cohesive wrap | Holds a bandage or model splint | Never tight; stop for discomfort or color change |
| Finger-splint model | Shows how an injured part may be supported | No real injury; no force or straightening |
