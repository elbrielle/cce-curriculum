Score each row 1 to 4. Circle the descriptor that fits the evidence in front of you. Total is out of 16.

| Criterion | 1 Beginning | 2 Developing | 3 Proficient | 4 Mastery |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Career accuracy | Names a career but no accurate detail. | One accurate detail, source unclear. | Two accurate details tied to a named source. | Three or more accurate details, source named for each. |
| Reasoning | States a preference with no reason. | Gives one reason, not tied to evidence. | Gives a reason tied to one piece of evidence. | Gives reasons tied to two or more pieces of evidence. |
| Vocabulary use | No unit vocabulary. | Uses one term, meaning unclear. | Uses two terms correctly. | Uses three or more terms correctly and in context. |
| Completion | Half the sheet or less is filled in. | Most items attempted, several blanks. | All items attempted. | All items attempted with full sentences where asked. |

### Scoring notes

Record the total in the gradebook as a formative score. A student who scores 1 on Career accuracy repeats the research step with a partner before moving on.

### Reteach triggers

- More than a third of the class scores 1 or 2 on Reasoning: reteach the evidence sentence stem the next day.
- More than a third scores 1 on Vocabulary use: run the word wall routine before the next research block.
