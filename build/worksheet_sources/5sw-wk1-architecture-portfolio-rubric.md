## Four-Criterion Rubric

| Criterion | 4 — Masters | 3 — Meets | 2 — Approaches | 1 — Needs Improvement | 0 — Insufficient Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Career and source accuracy | Three careers; salary labels and preparation boundaries are complete and accurate | Three careers with mostly complete, accurate labels | Comparison is present but labels or boundaries need correction | Evidence is minimal or repeatedly mislabeled | Blank, off-topic, or not submitted |
| Design and revision evidence | Requirements are visible; test evidence leads to a specific, justified revision | Requirements and a supported revision are visible | Design or revision evidence is partial | Design lacks usable requirement/revision evidence | Blank, off-topic, or not submitted |
| Career and cluster connection | Accurately explains three distinct roles and how they work together | Accurately explains roles and a clear connection | Names roles but connection/work details are partial | Mostly titles or inaccurate role claims | Blank, off-topic, or not submitted |
| Supported explanation | Uses specific design, salary, and preparation evidence with a meaningful limitation | Uses multiple relevant evidence details | Gives a claim with limited evidence | Gives preference or summary without evidence | Blank, off-topic, or not submitted |
