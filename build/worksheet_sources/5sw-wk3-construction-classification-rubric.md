## Minor 3 Rubric - 16 Points

This rubric scores only the Day 3 fixed-data classification. The visual observation report and professional briefing are formative and do not affect this grade.

| Criterion | 4 - Masters | 3 - Meets | 2 - Approaches | 1 - Needs Improvement | 0 - Insufficient Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Comparable evidence and labels | Uses all four occupations and keeps the May 2024 U.S. wage, 2024-34 growth, annual-openings context, preparation, and source basis accurately labeled | All four occupations are present with one minor label or evidence gap | Evidence is present but several labels or one occupation need correction | Fewer than four usable occupations or repeated source/measure confusion | Blank, off-topic, or no usable evidence |
| High-skill, high-wage, high-demand classifications | Applies the published classroom rule accurately to each occupation and cites the required preparation fact or number for every label | Classifications are mostly accurate with one minor evidence gap | Several labels are unsupported or one rule is applied inconsistently | Labels are mostly guesses or treated as official government designations | Blank or no supported classifications |
| Trend and openings reasoning | Clearly explains how growth and annual openings measure different things and supports the explanation with two occupations | Accurate comparison with one minor evidence gap | Identifies a difference but reasoning or evidence is partial | Treats openings, growth, and worker shortage as the same measure | Blank or no usable comparison |
| Recommendation and limitation | Recommends one route for fictional Jordan using preparation, pay, trend, and one specific national-to-local or starting-pay limitation | Recommendation uses the required evidence with one minor gap | Recommendation omits one evidence type or gives a general limitation | Preference is stated with little evidence or an unsupported local claim | Blank, off-topic, or no supported recommendation |

**Total:** ____ / 16

Text, speech-to-text, Canvas annotation, upload, and paper are equal evidence routes. Score evidence and reasoning, not H&L state, inspection skill, public speaking, group membership, handwriting, or English mechanics unless meaning is unclear.
