## Design brief

The workbook presents a **future-technology design scenario**. Your team will design a tiny medical robot that could travel through blood vessels, reach a clot, act without damaging the vessel wall, and send a signal back to a trained medical team.

This is a classroom model, not medical advice and not a claim that the design is ready for use in people.
