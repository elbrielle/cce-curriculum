## Score the reasoning, not the route choice

- Do not deduct for handwriting, platform route, artistic quality, accent, or English mechanics unless meaning is unclear.
- Any of the three careers may earn full credit when the recommendation fits the supplied evidence and names a meaningful trade-off.
- A Dental Hygienist recommendation can earn full credit even though it exceeds Jordan's stated preparation preference when the student names that trade-off and explains why other evidence outweighs it.
- Medical Billing and Coding is current FYF and district-coursebook content. Accept the workbook title.
- The fixed **$50,250 / 7%** figures are the guide's May 2024 U.S. median and 2024-34 national outlook comparison data. They are not starting or DFW pay.
- Dental Assistant is below the course high-wage line but above the course growth line. One label does not decide overall career value.

## Fast calibration checks

| Criterion | Full-credit evidence | Common repair |
|---|---|---|
| Career data accuracy | Three complete rows; pay labels keep year, geography, and measure | Restore a missing label or correct a mixed source |
| Classification | Each label cites the course rule and one number or preparation fact | Add the evidence beside the label |
| Scenario fit | Recommendation connects preparation and duties to Jordan | Replace preference-only language with one scenario need |
| Evidence and trade-off | Two accurate facts plus a meaningful trade-off or verification question | Name what Jordan gains, gives up, or must verify |

## Feedback shorthand

- **L** = repair the label
- **E** = add evidence
- **F** = connect to Jordan's fit
- **T** = name the trade-off or verification question
