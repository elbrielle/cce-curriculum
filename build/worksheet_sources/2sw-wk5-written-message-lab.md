## Part 2: Rewrite one fixed workplace message

Choose one. Use only the supplied facts.

**A. Supply update:** “Stuff is low. Someone should get more.” Facts: the Room 204 cabinet has 12 pairs of medium gloves and no large gloves; the next delivery is Friday; notify the supply lead today.

**B. Schedule update:** “Your thing moved.” Facts: the fictional orientation moved from Tuesday at 3:30 p.m. to Thursday at 3:30 p.m.; location remains Training Room B; questions go to the program office through the official portal.

**C. Repair request:** “The cart is messed up.” Facts: cart C-14's front-left wheel sticks when the cart carries more than 20 pounds; remove it from use and send the request to Facilities.
