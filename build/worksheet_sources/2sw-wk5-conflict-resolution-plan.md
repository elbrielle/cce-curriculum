## Use three moves

**Listen:** let each person explain the need. **Compromise:** combine ideas or trade fairly. **Stay respectful:** address the problem, not the person.

## Model one move

Weak: “We will vote.”

Stronger: “Each person gets 30 seconds to explain the idea. We will combine two name ideas, then use a blind vote if the combined name does not work.”

Complete frame: “Each person needs ____. We can ____ so that ____. If that does not work, we will ____.”

## If safety is involved

Listening and compromise do not replace a safety rule. Protect immediate safety, follow the workplace procedure, and notify the appropriate supervisor.
