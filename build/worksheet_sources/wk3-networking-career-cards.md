Use these cards when Hats & Ladders is unavailable or when you need a second source. The education descriptions are typical pathways, not promises or requirements for every employer. Use Xello for current local salary information when it is available.

## Network Administrator

Keeps a network running day to day. Common tasks include managing accounts and permissions, monitoring traffic, troubleshooting connections, installing updates, and documenting changes.

**Typical preparation:** Postsecondary certificate, associate degree, bachelor's degree, industry certification, or a mix of education and experience.

**Technical focus:** Networks, accounts, devices, monitoring, troubleshooting.

## Network Architect

Plans and designs data communication networks. Common tasks include choosing equipment, planning capacity and security, documenting the design, and explaining costs and tradeoffs.

**Typical preparation:** Bachelor's degree plus related work experience is common.

**Technical focus:** Network design, capacity, security, hardware, cloud connections.

## Database Administrator

Organizes, protects, backs up, and maintains data systems. Common tasks include controlling access, testing backups, monitoring performance, and helping users retrieve reliable data.

**Typical preparation:** Bachelor's degree is common; some roles accept related education, certifications, and experience.

**Technical focus:** Databases, access control, backups, performance, data reliability.

## Systems Analyst

Studies how an organization works and recommends technology changes. Common tasks include interviewing users, mapping needs, comparing solutions, testing systems, and translating between technical and nontechnical teams.

**Typical preparation:** Bachelor's degree is common, though employers may accept other combinations of technical and business experience.

**Technical focus:** Requirements, systems, testing, process improvement, communication.
