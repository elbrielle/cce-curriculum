## 2. Civilian pilot preparation example

1. Verify eligibility and medical requirements for the intended certificate or job.
2. Choose an FAA-certificated instructor or school. A Part 141 school uses an FAA-approved structured curriculum; some colleges pair a degree with flight training.
3. Earn required certificates and ratings, build required experience, and complete employer training.
4. Scheduled airline work requires ATP qualifications. A commercial certificate comes earlier and is not the same credential.

## 3. Military pilot example: U.S. Air Force

This is one branch-specific example, not a promise or a model for every military aviation job.

1. Qualify for an officer route and hold or be near completion of a bachelor's degree.
2. Meet academic, testing, selection, medical, physical, security, and age requirements.
3. Complete officer and pilot training if selected. Aircraft and location are not guaranteed by preference.
4. Current Air Force information states a 10-year active-duty service commitment after pilot training.
