## Three aviation careers

All pay figures are **May 2024 U.S. national medians from BLS**, accessed August 2026. They are not DFW starting pay, entry pay, or a guarantee.

| Career | Core work | Common preparation | National median |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Commercial pilot | Fly and navigate aircraft; communicate with crews and ATC | FAA flight training and certificates; some employers require or prefer a degree | $122,670 |
| Air traffic controller | Direct aircraft movement and maintain separation | FAA eligibility, testing, Academy training, and facility training | $144,580 |
| Aircraft mechanic / service technician | Inspect, test, repair, and document aircraft systems | Often an FAA-approved maintenance program; military or supervised-experience routes may also qualify | $78,680 |

## Civilian pilot preparation example

**Verify eligibility and medical requirements** → **Choose an FAA-certificated instructor or school** → **Earn certificates and ratings** → **Build required experience and complete employer training**

A Part 141 school uses an FAA-approved structured curriculum. A commercial pilot certificate comes before airline transport pilot qualifications and is not the same credential.

## U.S. Air Force pilot example

**Qualify for an officer route and degree requirement** → **Meet testing, selection, medical, physical, security, and age requirements** → **Complete officer and pilot training if selected** → **Serve the current commitment**

Current Air Force information states a 10-year active-duty service commitment after pilot training. Aircraft and location are not guaranteed by preference. This is one branch-specific service route, not free flight school or a promise of selection.

## Compare before recommending

| Evidence job | Civilian example | Air Force example |
|---|---|---|
| Two steps | Eligibility/medical check; FAA instructor or school; certificates/ratings; experience | Officer route/degree; screening and selection; officer/pilot training; service |
| Possible advantage | Multiple school/instructor pathways; student can compare formats and locations | Structured paid military training if selected |
| Tradeoff or uncertainty | Cost, time, medical eligibility, school quality, and experience vary | Selection is competitive; medical/security standards and service obligation apply |
| Authorized next source | FAA, certificated school/instructor, college program, counselor | Official Air Force source or authorized recruiter, counselor, current program source |

No route is automatically better. A defensible recommendation keeps the **step**, **tradeoff**, and **verification source** together.

**Use this frame:** “The ____ route may fit Sam because ____. A tradeoff is ____. Before deciding, Sam must verify ____ with ____.”

## Local boundary

Current Irving ISD public information lists **Aviation Maintenance**, **Drone Engineering**, and **Marine JROTC** at Irving High School. JROTC supports leadership and citizenship; it is not pilot training and does not guarantee military aviation selection. Course access, prerequisites, and capacity require a current coursebook or counselor check.
