A Building Block is something you already do. It does not have to be a job. Circle 3 to 5 things below that sound like you. Then copy them into your workbook.

- playing sports
- playing or making music
- video games
- cooking or baking
- babysitting a younger sibling or cousin
- fixing or building things
- drawing or making art
- taking care of pets or animals
- helping at church or in a community group
- yard work or chores at home
- dancing
- sewing, crochet, or crafts
- coding or making videos
- reading or writing stories
- a school team or club
- translating for my family
- running errands or shopping for my family
- doing hair or nails

**Not on the list?** Write your own here.
