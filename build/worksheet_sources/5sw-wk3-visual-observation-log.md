## Observation Boundary

This is a fictional image-analysis exercise. Do not inspect your home, touch wiring, climb, handle mold, use tools, price a repair, or decide whether someone should buy a property.

For each image:

1. **I observe:** visible evidence only.
2. **This could indicate:** one cautious possibility.
3. **The image cannot prove:** an evidence limit.
4. **Next qualified role:** who should evaluate it.

“No obvious concern in this image” is valid. It does not prove that the whole system is safe.
