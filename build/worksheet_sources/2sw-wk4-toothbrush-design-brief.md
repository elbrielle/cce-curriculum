## Fixed design facts

- Soft bristles are the standard starting point for most users.
- The head needs to reach the tooth surfaces the user must clean.
- The handle needs to be comfortable and controllable for that user.
- A timer, grip texture, or visual cue can support the routine, but the feature must match the need.
