### Safety First

- Thunder nearby: move toward an appropriate enclosed building or hard-topped vehicle; do not shelter under a tree or in a small open shelter.
- Fast or unknown-depth water: do not enter it. Communicate, seek an alternate route or higher ground, and wait for trained help.
- Anxious patient: communicate calmly, explain the plan, offer choices when possible, and do not make promises you cannot keep.
