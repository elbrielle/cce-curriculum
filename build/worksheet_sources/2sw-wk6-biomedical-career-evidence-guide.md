## Three careers, one evidence basis

All pay figures below are **May 2024 U.S. median annual wages**, not starting pay and not DFW-local wages. Outlook figures are national projections for **2024-2034**. Use the source and date when you cite a number.

| Career | What the worker does | Typical entry education | 2024 U.S. median | 2024-34 outlook | Openings each year |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Bioengineer or Biomedical Engineer | Combines engineering and science to design devices, equipment, computer systems, or software | Bachelor's degree; some jobs require graduate study | $106,950 | 5% growth | About 1,300 |
| Epidemiologist | Investigates patterns and causes of disease and injury in groups of people | Master's degree | $83,980 | 16% growth | About 800 |
| Medical Scientist | Conducts research aimed at improving human health | Doctoral or professional degree | $100,590 | 9% growth | About 9,600 |

### Read the evidence carefully

- **Median** means half of workers earn more and half earn less. It does not mean a new worker starts at that amount.
- **Percent growth** tells how fast the occupation is expected to change.
- **Openings each year** includes growth and replacement openings. A high growth rate can still describe a small occupation.
- A career assessment, favorite, or salary number is information to investigate. It is not a promise or a verdict.
