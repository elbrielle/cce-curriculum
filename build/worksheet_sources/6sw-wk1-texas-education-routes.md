## Keep the Routes Accurate

For most Texas classroom-teacher certificates, the current TEA route includes five requirements: an accredited bachelor's degree, an approved educator preparation program, the appropriate certification exams, a state application, and fingerprinting/background review. Some Career and Technical Education certificates have different degree and work-experience rules.

An educator preparation program may be university-based, post-baccalaureate, or an approved alternative-certification program. Program admission, clinical teaching, internships, residencies, cost, aid, and timing vary. Do not label one route automatically cheaper, faster, paid, or better without provider evidence.

Source checked: Texas Education Agency, Becoming a Classroom Teacher in Texas, current August 2026.

## Educational Aide I Boundary

TEA says the high-school Educational Aide I certificate has specific course, grade, credit, age, district-verification, application, and background-review conditions. A pathway name alone does not guarantee that a student receives the certificate.

Current Irving public evidence names **Education and Training** at Irving High, MacArthur, and Nimitz, and **Early Childhood Education** at Cardwell. The public page does not by itself promise a particular course schedule, certificate, placement, transportation, or admission.
