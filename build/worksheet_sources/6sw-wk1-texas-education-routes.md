## State Requirements and Provider Details

For most Texas classroom-teacher certificates, TEA lists five common requirements. Some CTE certificates have different degree, work-experience, or license rules.

| Common requirement | What it means |
|---|---|
| Accredited bachelor's degree | Required for most classroom-teacher certificates |
| Approved educator preparation program (EPP) | The program must be approved for the certificate route |
| Appropriate certification exams | The EPP helps identify and authorize the required exams |
| State certification application | Apply after the EPP confirms eligibility |
| Fingerprinting/background review | Required for first-time applicants |

**Source:** Texas Education Agency, *Becoming a Classroom Teacher in Texas*, checked August 2026.

Preparation may be university-based, post-baccalaureate, alternative certification, or an approved residency. These details can vary by provider: admission, clinical teaching or internship, residency, price, aid, timing, and employment rules.

## Educational Aide I and Irving Boundary

TEA lists two possible Educational Aide I eligibility patterns. One pattern is for a high-school graduate or GED holder with approved experience working with students or parents. The high-school-student pattern includes being at least 18, earning 70 or better in at least two specified Education and Training courses for three or more credits, district verification, application steps, and a criminal-history review that includes fingerprinting.

Current Irving public evidence names **Education and Training** at Irving High, MacArthur, and Nimitz and **Early Childhood Education** at Cardwell. A public program listing does not guarantee admission, a schedule, travel, certification, placement, or employment.
