## A career has visible work and hidden preparation

Above the waterline, people may notice the job title, workplace, product, or result. Under the waterline are the skills, tools, education, responsibilities, routines, and challenges that make the visible result possible.

### Short model: Architect

| What people may see | What may be hidden underneath |
|---|---|
| building drawings; finished spaces; client meetings | math and measurement; building codes; design software; revisions; deadlines; education and licensing |

The hidden items are not all negative. They show the work and preparation behind the career.
