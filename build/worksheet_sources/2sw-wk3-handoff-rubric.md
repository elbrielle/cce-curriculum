## Recommended minor checkpoint: 16 points

Score the student's Nursing Route Comparison and Observation and Clinical Handoff Record. Platform access, device access, speaking, and physical measurement are not graded.

| Criterion | Masters - 4 | Meets - 3 | Approaches - 2 | Needs Improvement - 1 | No evidence - 0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Route accuracy | 3+ correct roles, routes, and pay labels | One minor error | Partial or mixed labels | Major errors | Blank/off topic |
| Trade-off reasoning | Preparation, pay, and trade-off support the choice | Two facts support a clear choice | One fact or thin reasoning | Preference without evidence | Blank/off topic |
| Observation and handoff | Separates values, symptoms, inference; clear supervised handoff | Accurate with one omission | Inference or handoff is unclear | Diagnoses or omits key symptoms | Blank/off topic |
| Evidence and explanation | 2+ case facts and why they matter | 2 facts with basic explanation | 1 fact or list only | Inaccurate/unsupported evidence | Blank/off topic |
