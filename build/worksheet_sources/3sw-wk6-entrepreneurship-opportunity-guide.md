## Entrepreneurship is bigger than one kind of business

An **entrepreneur** finds a problem or unmet need, organizes a response, and accepts responsibility for the result. The business may sell a product, service, experience, or specialized skill.

| Field | Problem or need | Possible business | Owner responsibility |
|---|---|---|---|
| Information Technology | A small organization cannot keep its devices secure | Cybersecurity checkup service | Protect client information and explain limits honestly |
| Hospitality | Families need affordable food for events | Mobile catering or food-prep service | Price ingredients, follow food-safety rules, and deliver on time |
| Human Services | Clients need convenient personal-care appointments | Mobile or neighborhood salon service | Maintain sanitation, scheduling, records, and licenses |
| Agriculture | Apartment residents want fresh herbs in small spaces | Balcony-garden kit and coaching service | Test the product, price supplies, and give accurate care directions |
| Business and Marketing | A local shop needs help reaching customers | Social-media content and campaign service | Use client-approved information and show what the campaign actually did |

**Important:** a business idea is not automatically a good opportunity. The owner still has to check the customer, cost, competition, rules, and ability to deliver.
