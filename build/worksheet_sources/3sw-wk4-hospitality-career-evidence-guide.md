## Compare three hospitality careers

All pay figures are **May 2024 U.S. medians**, not DFW pay or starting pay. Outlook covers **2024-2034**. Annual openings include new jobs and openings created when workers leave an occupation.

| Career | What the worker does | Common preparation | Current national evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chef or Head Cook | Plans menus, prepares food, supervises kitchen staff, checks quality and sanitation, and orders supplies | High school diploma is typical; 5 or more years of related experience is common; culinary programs and apprenticeships are other routes | $60,990 median; 7% growth; about 24,400 openings each year |
| Lodging Manager | Coordinates guest service and hotel operations so the property is efficient and profitable | Three common routes: high school diploma plus lodging experience; associate degree or certificate; or bachelor's degree in hospitality/hotel management | $68,130 median; 3% growth; about 5,400 openings each year |
| Meeting, Convention, and Event Planner | Meets with clients, plans time/location/cost, coordinates venues and services, monitors events, and reviews bills | Bachelor's degree is typical; event or hospitality experience may help | $59,440 median; 5% growth; about 15,500 openings each year |

### Read the measures correctly

- **Median pay** is the middle pay among workers in the occupation. It is not beginning pay.
- **Outlook** is a national projection. It is not a promise to one student.
- **Preparation** describes a common route, not the only possible route.
- Work schedules matter. Hospitality work may include evenings, weekends, holidays, travel, or more than 40 hours near an event.

## Current Irving ISD connections

Irving ISD currently lists the **School of Culinary Arts/Hospitality Services at Singley Academy**, with Culinary Arts and Hospitality Services pathways. The district also lists **Lodging and Resort Management** at Irving High School, MacArthur High School, and Nimitz High School. Singley's current CTE page identifies **FireBird Cafe Catering** as a student-run business.

These are opportunities to investigate. The page does not guarantee admission, a certification, a job, or a salary.
