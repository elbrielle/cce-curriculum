## Registered Apprenticeship — Stable Evidence

A Registered Apprenticeship is a paid job with structured on-the-job learning, related instruction, mentoring, progressive wages, and a portable industry-recognized credential.

Sponsor-specific details that must be checked: eligibility, length, wage, cost, schedule, application, screening, license relationship, and current opening.

Future apply steps:

1. Search the Apprenticeship Job Finder by occupation and location.
2. Open a current employer or sponsor listing.
3. Record eligibility, documents, location, training, wage, credential, and deadline exactly as listed.
4. Apply directly through the employer or sponsor when eligible and ready.
5. Follow the sponsor's screening and next-step instructions.

## Dallas College Construction Technology Card

Current options include workforce training, credit certificates, and a Construction Technology A.A.S. Estimated time, tuition basis, campus, credit status, and transfer limits vary by route.

Future enroll steps:

1. Choose a current Construction Technology route.
2. Check campus, time, published tuition basis, credit/noncredit status, and transfer limit.
3. Complete the Dallas College or ApplyTexas application when ready.
4. Complete pre-advising and determine current TSI/exemption requirements.
5. Meet with an advisor/pathway specialist and register for the correct courses.
