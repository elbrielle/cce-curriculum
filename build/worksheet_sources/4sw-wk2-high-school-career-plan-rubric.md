## 16-point scoring tool

This scoring profile applies to the four-page Individual High School and Career Plan only. Days 1-4 help you build the evidence; they are not four additional Major uploads.

| Criterion | 4 - Masters | 3 - Meets | 2 - Approaches | 1 - Needs Improvement | 0 - Insufficient evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Self-evidence and direction | Direction is supported by two specific pieces of self-evidence and includes an honest revision condition. | Direction uses two relevant pieces of self-evidence. | Direction has one clear evidence connection or relies on broad claims. | Direction is named with little relevant evidence. | Blank, off-topic, or copied without a personal evidence connection. |
| Course and pathway planning | Uses current names, a four-year sequence, one prerequisite chain, a verification question, and a workable backup. | Includes a four-year sequence, pathway connection, and one verification question. | Sequence is partly complete or contains claims that still need labels. | Plan lists isolated courses without a usable sequence. | No course or pathway evidence. |
| Postsecondary and college-credit evidence | Accurately documents preparation plus one advanced or college-credit option with source/date and a limitation or question. | Documents preparation and one advanced or college-credit option with source/date. | Includes preparation or college-credit evidence, but labels or source meaning are incomplete. | Uses vague, unsupported, or incorrectly labeled claims. | No usable postsecondary evidence. |
| Action, support, and revision | Includes a seven-day action, counseling action, Grade 9 action, support, backup, and specific evidence that would trigger revision. | Includes three timed actions, support, and a backup. | Actions are present but broad, weakly timed, or missing support/backup. | One vague action is listed. | No actionable plan. |

## Scoring boundary

Score evidence and reasoning. Do not score family availability, English mechanics unless meaning is unclear, handwriting, art, accent, platform access, or whether the student's current direction matches an adult preference.
