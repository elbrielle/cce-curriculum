## Scoring Boundaries

- Paper, Canva, Adobe Express, SVG, PNG, hand-cut card stock, and teacher-selected laser examples score equally.
- Do not score Xello login access, H&L favorites, public speaking, machine operation, or gallery participation.
- A student may decide cybersecurity or IT is not a fit and still earn full credit when the evidence supports the decision.
- Peer feedback is formative. Score the student's own final evidence only.

## Reteach Triggers

- **Plan:** name one audience before choosing activities.
- **Integrity:** replace copied content or personal contact details with original/credited content and a fictional or teacher-approved sign-up route.
- **Goal:** complete the goal sentence before opening a design tool.
- **Reflection:** use the recovery box when Week 0 evidence is missing; then require one specific change and both transition steps.
