Score the final one-page flyer. FYF p. 35 is the planning space; do not require a second plan or a four-part portfolio. A teacher-approved accessible design format is scored with the same evidence criteria.

Student: _______________________ Period: ______ Total: ______ / 16 Date: __________

| Criterion | 4 - Masters | 3 - Meets | 2 - Approaches | 1 - Needs Improvement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Audience and event information** | The title, intended audience, topic, time/place, and a safe way to sign up are clear and easy to find. | All required event information is present; one detail is hard to find or too general. | Two required details are missing or hard to understand. | The audience or event purpose is unclear, or most required details are missing. |
| **Accurate safety teaching** | The flyer gives two specific, accurate actions the audience can use to stay safer online and explains the benefit without a guarantee. | Two useful safety actions are present; one needs a clearer explanation. | One action is accurate, but another is vague, incomplete, or misleading. | Advice is mostly missing, inaccurate, or makes an unsafe promise. |
| **Design and readability** | Text hierarchy, spacing, contrast, and one relevant visual help the audience understand the message quickly. | The design is readable and organized; one choice makes a key detail less clear. | The message can be found, but crowded text, weak contrast, or an unrelated visual slows reading. | Key information is difficult to read or the layout obscures the message. |
| **Integrity and privacy** | Wording and visuals are original or credited; the sign-up path avoids personal student contact details and the flyer makes no unsupported claim. | The work is privacy-safe and mostly original or credited; one credit or claim needs clarification. | A credit or privacy detail needs repair before sharing. | The flyer copies substantial work without credit, exposes personal contact information, or makes an unsafe claim. |

## Scoring Boundaries

- The flyer is the single Major 2 submission. Do not score a portfolio, goal symbol, Evidence Log, or duplicate copy of the workbook plan.
- A teacher-approved accessible design format can earn the same score. Judge the information and design, not whether the student had uninterrupted Canva access.
- The flyer is fictional classroom work. It is not posted publicly and does not collect real sign-ups.
- Peer feedback and a revision check support instruction; score the final student work.

## Reteach Triggers

- **Audience:** name one group and make the event details easy for that group to locate.
- **Safety:** replace a vague warning with two actions a participant could actually try.
- **Design:** enlarge the most important text, strengthen contrast, and remove decoration that competes with the message.
- **Integrity:** replace personal contact information with a teacher-approved sign-up path and credit reused assets.
