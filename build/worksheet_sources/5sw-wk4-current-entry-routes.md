## Registered Apprenticeship Route Card

A Registered Apprenticeship is a paid job with structured on-the-job learning, related instruction, mentoring, progressive wages, and a portable industry-recognized credential.

Sponsor-specific details that must be checked: current opening, occupation, eligibility, application, screening, length, wage progression, schedule, cost, credential, and license relationship.

Future verification sequence:

1. Search the Apprenticeship Job Finder by occupation and location.
2. Open a current employer or sponsor listing.
3. Record the listing's eligibility, documents, training, wage, credential, location, and deadline.
4. Apply directly through the listed employer or sponsor when eligible and ready.
5. Complete the sponsor's stated screening and next steps.

## Dallas College Electrical Technology Card

Current published examples accessed August 2026 include:

- Electrical Pre-Apprentice Continuing Education certificate: estimated 6 months and $5,184 in-county.
- Commercial Electrical credit certificate: estimated 8 months and $2,673 in-county.

These are published estimates, not promises. Cost, time, prerequisites, campus availability, aid, and transfer treatment must be checked for the student's cycle.

Future verification sequence:

1. Choose the current credit or continuing-education route.
2. Check campus, schedule, prerequisites, cost basis, credit status, aid, and transfer limits.
3. Complete the appropriate Dallas College application when ready.
4. Complete required advising or readiness steps.
5. Register for the correct current courses with college guidance.
