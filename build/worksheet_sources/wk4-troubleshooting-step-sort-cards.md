**Print one sheet, cut out all fifteen cards, and keep each row's three cards together in one envelope or paper clip.** Give a team the row that matches its Help Desk scenario card.

**Your job.** The three steps for your scenario are out of order. Put them in the order a help desk tech would really try them, then program that order into your micro:bit. The cheapest and fastest check goes first. The step that costs the most time or money goes last.

| Scenario | Card A | Card B | Card C |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Printer not working** | Restart printer | Check power cable | Check paper tray |
| **2. Wi-Fi disconnected** | Forget and reconnect network | Check Wi-Fi switch | Restart router |
| **3. Software crashing** | Check for updates | Close and reopen app | Restart computer |
| **4. Computer won't turn on** | Check power button | Hold power 10 sec | Check power cable |
| **5. Password reset** | Call IT to reset | Check caps lock | Use forgot password link |

**Talk it out before you commit.** Say your order out loud to your partner using this stem: "We put _____ first because it takes the least time, and we put _____ last because _____."

**Check yourself.** Ask one question about your first card: if this step fixes the problem, is the user done in under a minute? If the answer is no, that card probably belongs later.
