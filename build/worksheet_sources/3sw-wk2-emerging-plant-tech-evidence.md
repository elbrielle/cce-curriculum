## Emerging work often grows inside an established occupation

Job titles change faster than government career categories. The three specialties below show how technology is changing plant and agriculture work. The salary and outlook figures belong to the **broader BLS parent occupation**, not every exact specialty or local job.

| Emerging specialty to investigate | BLS parent occupation | How technology changes the work | Typical entry route | Parent occupation evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Precision Agriculture Systems Technician | Agricultural and Food Science Technician | Uses sensors, field samples, maps, and digital records to help monitor crops, water, pests, and production | Usually an associate degree; routes vary | $48,480 May 2024 U.S. median; 5% growth, 2024-34 |
| Controlled-Environment Plant Scientist | Soil and Plant Scientist | Studies plant stress, nutrients, light, water, pests, and growing conditions in greenhouses or indoor systems | At least a bachelor's degree; some research roles prefer graduate study | $71,410 May 2024 U.S. median; 5% growth, 2024-34 |
| Agricultural Automation Engineer | Agricultural Engineer | Designs or improves automated irrigation, spraying, harvesting, climate controls, and data-connected equipment | Bachelor's degree in an engineering field | $84,630 May 2024 U.S. median; 6% growth, 2024-34 |

### What the current evidence says

- BLS reports that agricultural engineers may integrate **artificial intelligence and geospatial systems** and work on precision or automated irrigation, spraying, and harvesting.
- USDA describes precision agriculture as using sensors and other tools to apply resources in the right amount, place, and time.
- USDA-supported specialty-crop projects use sensors, imaging, automation, and machine learning to monitor plant wetness, heat stress, and disease.

## Evaluate, do not advertise

An occupation can be important even when its percentage growth is not huge. An “emerging” specialty may mean that the **tasks and tools are changing**, not that the whole parent occupation is brand new.
