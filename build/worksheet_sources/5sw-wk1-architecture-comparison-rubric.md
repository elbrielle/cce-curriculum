## Minor 1 Rubric - 16 Points

This rubric scores only the Day 2 three-career comparison. The design portfolio, Tinkercad or paper model, and public speaking are formative and do not affect this grade.

| Criterion | 4 - Masters | 3 - Meets | 2 - Approaches | 1 - Needs Improvement | 0 - Insufficient Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Career and source accuracy | All three careers include accurate work products, occupation labels, source, date, geography, and salary measure | All three careers are present with one minor label or work-product gap | Three careers are named, but several labels or work details need correction | Fewer than three usable careers or repeated source/measure confusion | Blank, off-topic, copied without evidence, or not submitted |
| Preparation boundaries | Education, experience, examination, registration, and variable-route boundaries are accurately separated where relevant | Preparation is mostly accurate with one minor boundary gap | Preparation evidence is partial or combines two different requirements | Preparation is mostly unsupported or presented as one universal route | Blank or no usable preparation evidence |
| Three-career salary comparison | Correctly ranks all three May 2024 U.S. medians and explains one meaningful difference without relabeling the figures as local, starting, or guaranteed pay | Correct ranking and figures with one minor explanation or label gap | Partial ranking or one unsupported salary label | Comparison uses fewer than three figures or mixes incompatible measures | Blank or no comparable salary evidence |
| Recommendation and limitation | Recommends one career for fictional Jordan using a labeled salary fact and preparation difference, then names a useful limitation or next question | Recommendation uses both evidence types with one minor reasoning gap | Recommendation uses only one evidence type or gives a general limitation | Preference is stated with little evidence or no meaningful limitation | Blank, off-topic, or no supported recommendation |

**Total:** ____ / 16

Equal routes: text, speech-to-text, Canvas annotation, upload, or paper. Score evidence and reasoning, not platform access, art, or career preference.
