## Read the Labels

All pay figures below are **May 2024 U.S. median annual wages from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics**. Median is not starting pay, DFW pay, or a guarantee.

| Career | Work product | Common preparation | May 2024 U.S. median |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Architect | Plans and designs structures; coordinates project requirements | Bachelor's degree is the BLS typical entry education. Registration requirements vary; common Texas structure includes approved education, documented AXP experience, and ARE. | $96,690 |
| Drafter | Converts designs into technical drawings with CAD software | Associate's degree is the BLS typical entry education; certificate or diploma routes also exist. | $65,380 |
| Interior Designer | Plans functional, safe, and attractive interior spaces and documents selections/layouts | Bachelor's degree is the BLS typical entry education; registration rules vary by work and jurisdiction. | $63,490 |

Sources accessed August 2026: BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook profiles for Architects, Drafters, and Interior Designers; NCARB/Texas sources for architecture registration structure.
