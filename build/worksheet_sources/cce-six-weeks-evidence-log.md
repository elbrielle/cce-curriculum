Add **one entry at the end of each six weeks**. Look back at work you already completed. Do not upload or redo the old artifact. Keep this log in your CCE binder or teacher-designated digital folder. You will use it later for a first resume, interview examples, and the capstone.

**Transferable-skill bank:** Communication · Teamwork · Problem-Solving · Adaptability · Leadership · Time Management · Creativity · Work Ethic · Empathy · Confidence

Write a short phrase in most cells. In **Evidence**, name one visible action, not just a trait. In **Revision or recovery**, name what you changed, fixed, or finished after a barrier.
