**Dated source guide — BLS May 2024 pay data, checked August 6, 2026.** Median pay is national. It is not starting pay, local pay, or a promise. Employer requirements vary.

## Route 1: Computer User Support Specialist

**BLS preparation:** Some college courses are common. Candidates may qualify with a high school diploma plus relevant IT certifications.

**2024 national median pay:** **$60,340**

**Time consideration:** A four-year degree is not automatically required for every position. Certification study time varies by learner and provider.

**Tradeoff:** This route may allow earlier entry, but requirements vary and the national median is lower than the other routes on this sheet.

## Route 2: Computer Network Support Specialist

**BLS preparation:** An associate degree is typical.

**2024 national median pay:** **$73,340**

**Time consideration:** An associate degree is commonly designed as a two-year college program, though actual completion time varies.

**Tradeoff:** More formal study than the first route; broader network responsibilities and a higher national median.

## Route 3: Software Developer

**BLS preparation:** A bachelor's degree in computer and information technology or a related field is typical.

**2024 national median pay:** **$133,080**

**Time consideration:** A bachelor's degree is commonly designed as a four-year program, though actual completion time varies.

**Tradeoff:** Longer formal preparation; different work centered on designing applications or programs and a higher national median.

## About certifications and cost

CompTIA A+ is one example of a vendor-neutral IT certification. Exam versions, voucher prices, study time, and district payment options change. Use a current official CompTIA or district source if your teacher asks you to compare cost. Do not copy an old price from a search result.

**BLS sources:** Computer Support Specialists; Software Developers, Quality Assurance Analysts, and Testers.
