Teacher Facilitator Guide - 1SW Wk0 Day 5

Catch-Up and Your Choice

Flex day - 50 minutes - catch-up first, career research by default

Catch-up has priority. Students missing a core result or My Career Journey work on that first. Students who are current learn the six-field career-research format used in later weeks.

Open the student guide

Before students arrive

  • Sort students into catch-up and career-research paths.
  • Post Voice 0 for the first 15 minutes, then Voice 2 for partner comparison.
  • Prepare workbook pages 4-5 and two copies per student of the career-research worksheet.
  • Keep the worked example and bilingual labels ready.
  • Open H&L Hat Finder and BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook.
  • Prepare the Day 5 exit ticket.

Student evidence

Catch-up students submit the missing core artifact. Other students submit two career-research records plus the comparison exit ticket. The research work is a completion/evidence minor-grade option.

Default 50-minute flow

1. Launch two paths - 5 minutes

Name each student’s path and the exact artifact due. Keep the first 15 minutes quiet for H&L assessments.

2. Perks and Quirks - 10 minutes

Walk the Pest Control Technician example. Emphasize that a perk for one person can be a quirk for another.

3. Research two Hats - 20 minutes

Students use H&L plus BLS. Require BLS confirmation for pay and training. Monitor for both careers carrying all six workbook facts.

4. Teach the CCE transfer sheet - 10 minutes

Model Career 1. Name the changed fields instead of implying a direct copy. Students complete Career 2 independently.

5. Exit ticket - 5 minutes

Students compare pay, training, and one quirk, then identify the career they would research next and the missing fact they still need.

Catch-up order
  1. Discover Your Core
  2. Work Values and three Building Blocks
  3. My Career Journey major reflection

Students who finish one missing input should move directly to the next missing artifact.

Language and research supports

Use the worked example, bilingual field labels, partner reading, and oral rehearsal. Require the same evidence while reducing language-generation load.

Platform fallback

If H&L is unavailable, students choose two careers from BLS and complete the same fields. Record students who still need profile catch-up. If BLS is blocked, use teacher-provided career pages and verify figures later.
