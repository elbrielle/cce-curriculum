Catch Up, Xello, and Perks & Quirks

One verified priority · 50 minutes · TEKS d(1)(B), d(4)(A)

Projectable lesson: Download the Day 5 source-grounded slide deck. Speaker notes carry the timing, source, model, pivot, and recovery cues; this guide remains the full teacher route.
Do not run a menu. Assign each student one starting priority: core catch-up, required Xello, or FYF. Verify it before a second task begins.

Open the student guide

Before students arrive

  • Create a private list: core catch-up; Xello required; ready for FYF.
  • Verify H&L and Xello launch routes.
  • Set one FYF workbook per student to pp. 4-5.
  • Open the Day 5 Student Guide and deck.
  • Default copies: 0. FYF p. 5 is the ready-path response surface; do not distribute the separate CCE research worksheet.

Complete model

Hat: Veterinary Technician
Detail: Works with animals and owners in clinics.
Mark: Perk
Why: I value relationships and have a Building Block caring for my dog.
Source: Hats & Ladders career page.
Still verify: required education and current pay.

Non-model: “Good job. Perk. Google.” It names neither a career detail nor a usable source.

50-minute flow

1. Readiness + focus plan · 5 minutes

Three priorities, circle the first, name two private No-Zone distractions. Teacher assigns the starting path.

2. Protected focus block · 20 minutes

Core: earliest missing input/Minor. Xello: login + after-high-school goal only. FYF: study the example and begin one Hat table.

3. Second priority block · 15 minutes

Move only after verification. FYF minimum is one complete six-detail Hat table with source; second Hat is extension. No transfer worksheet or open BLS search today.

4. Verify + recover · 5 minutes

Verify one result per student and record remaining access problems without treating an outage as missing effort.

5. Close · 5 minutes

Completed result + next CCE action + exact place to find it. Students return FYF to the class-set location and close platform tabs. No new upload.

Xello boundary

Required: district SSO login and one after-high-school goal. Do not add Matchmaker, Personality Style, Learning Style, Skills Lab, or Mission Complete today.

Pivot, trim, recovery
  • Broad outage: affected students use FYF; record a named platform catch-up.
  • No workbook: one equivalent table; accept it without recopying.
  • Absent: assign the earliest missing requirement, not the entire menu.
  • Trim the second Hat and share first. Protect one verified requirement and the next-action close.
