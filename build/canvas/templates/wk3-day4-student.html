Week 3 - Day 4

Research an emerging IT career without stretching the evidence

Use BLS carefully, label a proxy when needed, and connect the career to a real local employer source.

Major-grade evidence: the completed research sheet supplies the fourth part of the 16-point rubric.

1. Choose one career

AI Engineer, Machine Learning Specialist, Cloud Architect, Cybersecurity Analyst, Data Scientist, UX Designer, or Drone Software Developer.

2. Use the source map

Open the BLS career-to-source map

Exact match
The BLS occupation closely matches the title you chose.
Closest proxy
The BLS numbers describe a broader occupation. Label it; do not pretend it is exact.

3. Complete the research sheet

Open the Emerging Tech Research Template

  • Use BLS for national pay and job outlook.
  • Record the number's year or year range.
  • Mark exact occupation or closest proxy.
  • Use the teacher-approved company page or current posting for the DFW employer.
  • Explain emerging with a technology, demand change, or changed work task—not simply a missing BLS title.
