Topic: Emerging IT Work

Show your learning: complete both sheets with the BLS occupation, source match, dated pay and growth, and one supported reason the work is changing.

Today you will

  • Choose one changing IT career.
  • Use the dated evidence guide to complete the research sheets.
  • Compare the changing career with an established one.

1. Choose one career

AI or Machine Learning Engineer, Cloud Architect, Information Security Analyst, Data Scientist, UX Designer, or Drone Software Developer.

2. Use the evidence guide

Open the dated Emerging IT Career Evidence Guide

Exact match
BLS reports the same job title you chose.
Closest occupation or proxy
BLS reports a related group of jobs. Use the facts, but state that they do not describe your exact title.

3. Complete the research sheet

Open the Emerging Tech Research Template

  • Write the BLS occupation you used.
  • Copy the year with every pay number.
  • Copy the year range with every growth rate.
  • Mark exact occupation, closest occupation, or proxy.
  • Explain what technology, demand, or work task is changing.
  • If you add a Xello DFW salary, keep it in the separate local-evidence box.

4. Compare careers

Open the Emerging/Established IT Career Comparison

You are done when

  • Your research identifies the exact BLS occupation or labels the closest proxy.
  • Pay and growth evidence includes the correct year or year range.
  • Your explanation names what is changing and cites one supplied fact.
  • The emerging/established comparison is complete.
