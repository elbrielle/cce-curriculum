1. Where to start

  1. Open the Canvas Student Guide for today.
  2. Open Hats & Ladders, Find Your Future, or Xello only when the directions name it.
  3. Write in your CCE notebook only when the directions ask for a note or reflection.
  4. Turn in only the work named in the guide.
Start in Canvas. Today’s guide will tell you what to open and what to complete.

2. Set up your notebook

Your teacher will tell you which option to use. Digital and paper both count.

Digital: OneNote Class Notebook

  1. Open the standalone Class Notebook link your teacher tested and shares today.
  2. Open your private notebook. Do not work in Collaboration Space today.
  3. Find these private sections: CCE Work, Focused Notes, and Evidence & Reflection.
  4. Open CCE Work → Notebook Setup + First-Week Goal.

Physical: notebook, binder section, or paper folder

  1. Label three parts: CCE Work, Focused Notes, and Evidence & Reflection.
  2. Start today's page in CCE Work.

Test line: The place I will use for CCE work is __________. If it does not open, I will use __________.

If OneNote does not open, use this Canvas page or the paper your teacher gives you. Keep what you finish; you will not need to redo it later.

3. First-week CCE goal

Use a real CCE goal or a fictional course goal. You do not have to share grades, health, disability, family information, or anything private.

Open the CCE First Week Goal-Setting Sheet

  1. My first-week CCE goal: ________________________________
  2. Why this goal matters to me: _____________________________
  3. One specific action I will take: ___________________________
  4. When or at which class checkpoint I will do it: ____________
  5. My confidence right now: 1   2   3   4   5
  6. What will help me follow through? Calendar/planner / teacher check-in / classmate / reminder or alarm / fallback route
Apoyo en español:
Mi meta es ____. Es importante porque ____. Voy a ____. Lo haré ____. Mi confianza es ____. Me ayudará ____.
Complete model

Goal: By Friday, I will complete Discover Your Core carefully.
Why: I want the result to be based on my real answers.
Action: I will read every question and ask for help if a word is unclear.
Checkpoint: Tuesday during the Hats & Ladders block.
Confidence: 4
Support: Teacher check-in and Canvas or paper if my account does not open.

4. Private self-check

  • Can someone see what I will do?
  • Did I name when I will do it?
  • Did I choose what will help if I get stuck?

You are done for today when

  • your notebook opens or you know what to use if it does not;
  • all six goal-plan fields are complete;
  • you can point to the page and explain your next action; and
  • you have returned your assigned device correctly.

If the class runs out of time: mark the last field you completed. Tuesday begins with protected recovery time; do not start a second page.
