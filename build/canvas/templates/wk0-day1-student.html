Week 0 - Day 1

Welcome to the CCE Lab

Learn how our lab works, get your tools ready, and begin noticing the careers behind everyday things.

Today you will

  • set up your engineering notebook;
  • practice the routines your class will use;
  • complete the path your teacher assigns; and
  • turn in the assigned Day 1 evidence.

Get ready

  • Engineering notebook
  • Chromebook
  • Find Your Future workbook, pages 2-3

1. Start your notebook

Write your name and CCE Engineering Notebook on the cover. On page 1, write or draw what you may want to do when you grow up. “I do not know yet” is a complete answer.

2. Learn the lab routines

Follow your teacher’s walkthrough. Record any room-specific rules you need to remember. Complete the safety contract if your class receives one.

Open the lab safety contract

Spanish and English safety contract

Open the bilingual safety contract.

3. Follow today’s class path

Your teacher will tell you which path to complete.
Path A: Classroom Career Hunt
  1. Read the examples on workbook page 2.
  2. Work with a partner. Look around the room.
  3. On page 3, list objects and the careers that designed, built, moved, or sold them.
  4. Be ready to share one surprising connection.

Open the career-hunt starter sheet if you need ideas.

Path B: Xello login and first goal
  1. Open Xello through district SSO.
  2. Confirm that your dashboard loads.
  3. Open About Me.
  4. Choose your current after-high-school goal. You may change it later.

If Xello does not open, tell your teacher and continue with the non-Xello path.

4. Complete the exit ticket if assigned

Open the Day 1 exit ticket

You are done when

  • Your notebook is labeled.
  • You completed the class path your teacher assigned.
  • Your safety contract, workbook page, Xello goal, or exit ticket is turned in as directed.
Were you absent?
