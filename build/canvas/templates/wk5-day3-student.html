Week 5 - Day 3

Plan a cybersecurity bootcamp

Design a short community lesson for one real audience and one online-safety need.

Major-grade evidence begins today: the plan and flyer are the first two parts. Canva, Adobe Express, and paper are equal routes.

Today's learning

Topic: Work Ethic

I can: explain how work ethic and integrity guide accurate, original, and privacy-safe cybersecurity work.

Show your learning: create a complete bootcamp plan and flyer prototype with accurate advice, a behind-the-scenes work task, and a privacy or integrity rule.

1. Read the workbook scenario

Treat the printed percentages as part of the workbook scenario, not as current live statistics.

2. Complete the plan

Open the Cybersecurity Bootcamp Plan See a completed model

Name one audience, one need, two things participants can do, one activity, a place/time, one accurate safety action, one behind-the-scenes job, and one privacy or integrity rule.

3. Make a flyer prototype

  1. Show the program name, audience, topic, place/time, and safe sign-up method.
  2. Use original words and visuals, or credit what you reuse.
  3. Never post a student's personal email, phone number, home address, or social account.
  4. Get one partner note: one clear part and one next revision.

You are done when

  • Every section of the two-page plan is complete.
  • The advice is accurate and does not promise perfect safety.
  • The flyer includes a privacy-safe sign-up route.
  • The flyer is original or clearly credits reused material.
No design tool or absent?

Use one sheet of paper. A clear paper prototype meets the same rubric. If no peer is available, use the flyer checklist for your own revision.
