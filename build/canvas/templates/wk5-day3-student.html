Plan a cybersecurity bootcamp

Design a short community lesson for one real audience and one online-safety need.

Response Home: Cybersecurity Bootcamp Plan (Major 2 Part A) & Flyer Prototype (Major 2 Part B).
Major-Grade Evidence Begins Today: Your bootcamp plan and flyer prototype form the first two components of the 16-point Major 2 Capstone rubric. (Canva, Adobe Express, and paper are equal options.)

Today you will

  • read the community cybersecurity bootcamp scenario in your workbook;
  • complete a structured plan for one specific audience and online safety need;
  • create a privacy-safe flyer prototype; and
  • collect peer feedback to refine your design.

1. Read the Workbook Scenario

Open your Find Your Future workbook to pp. 34–35.

2. Complete the Plan

Open the Cybersecurity Bootcamp Plan · See a completed model

Identify: (1) target audience, (2) safety need, (3) two actionable participant takeaways, (4) interactive activity, (5) logistics (time/place), (6) behind-the-scenes technician task, and (7) privacy/integrity standard.

3. Make a Flyer Prototype

  1. Include program title, target audience, topic, time/place, and a safe contact/sign-up method.
  2. Use original phrasing and graphics, or cite reused assets.
  3. Privacy Rule: Never post personal student phone numbers, personal email addresses, or home locations.
  4. Collect one peer critique: one strong element and one revision idea.

You are done when

  • every section of the 2-page bootcamp plan is complete;
  • security advice is accurate without claiming "100% invulnerability";
  • the flyer prototype uses a privacy-safe sign-up route; and
  • your design is original or properly credited.
No design tool or absent?

Use one sheet of paper. A clear paper prototype meets the same rubric. If no peer is available, use the flyer checklist for your own revision.
