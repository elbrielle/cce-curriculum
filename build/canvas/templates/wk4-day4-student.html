Test the tool and practice calm customer service

Finish the core logic, let another team test it, revise, and help a frustrated user one step at a time.

Response Home: Help Desk Program Evidence Sheet (Revisions) & Day 4 Customer Service Check.

Today you will

  • reopen and verify your team's core MakeCode troubleshooting program;
  • swap devices with a peer team to test and collect user feedback;
  • document and make one logic revision; and
  • practice a professional help desk call following customer service rules.

1. Reopen and Finish the Core

  • Use the same assigned Chromebook and project name (Period - Team - Help Desk).
  • Confirm all three Button A steps advance in order and Button B displays "FIXED."
  • If the project is missing, reload from your saved backup or use the paper trace.

2. Swap, Test, and Revise

  1. Another team runs or traces your sequence without your explanation.
  2. They name one clear element and one point of confusion.
  3. Write their feedback on your team evidence sheet.
  4. Make and document at least one program revision.
Feedback and revision frames

Feedback: “The order was clear because [reason].” · “I got confused at Step [#] because [reason].”

Revision: “We changed [element] to [new approach] so the user can [benefit].”

3. Run the Help Desk Call

Practice the four core customer service rules: (1) Acknowledge the problem with empathy, (2) Use clear, non-technical words, (3) Give one step at a time, and (4) Stay calm and patient.
