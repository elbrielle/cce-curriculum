Teacher Facilitator Guide - 1SW Wk0 Day 3

Work Values and Building Blocks

Core Day B - 50 minutes - TEKS d(1)(A), d(1)(B)

Protect this day. Work Values, Building Blocks, and recommended clusters feed tomorrow’s major reflection.

Open the student guide

Before students arrive

  • Open Climber Notes Exploring Your Work Values.
  • Confirm H&L opens and students can reach Profile.
  • Prepare workbook pages 9-11 and engineering notebooks.
  • Print the Building Blocks word bank and bilingual support.
  • Prepare the Day 3 exit ticket.

What students produce

  • Top two work values in the notebook
  • Workbook Building Blocks inventory with named skills
  • At least three saved H&L Building Blocks
  • Top three recommended clusters and exit ticket

Minor-grade option: check that the evidence is complete and connected. Do not grade which value or cluster appears.

50-minute flow

1. Warm-up - 5 minutes

Students choose between higher pay alone or lower pay with a strong team. Name the values inside their reasons.

2. Discover Your Work Values - 20 minutes

Use deck slides 2-3. Define the six starter values, then direct students to Profile > Discover Your Work Values. They record their top two and compare the result with their prediction.

3. Building Blocks - 15 minutes

Use workbook pages 9-10. Run the quick whole-class inventory, then require three experiences and a skill for each. Students save at least three in H&L.

4. Recommended clusters - 8 minutes

Students locate recommendations and record their top three. Frame them as starting suggestions, not a final answer.

5. Exit ticket - 2 minutes

Accept either job choice when the student names a fitting work value and explains the trade-off.

Active-monitoring targets
  1. Correct H&L activity and honest reading.
  2. Three real Building Blocks, not vague traits.
  3. A skill beside each experience.
  4. Three saved profile entries before recommendations.
Language and reading supports

Use the word banks, oral brainstorming, and the stems “One thing I do is…” and “It teaches me…”. Accept an oral idea before writing. Pair students for the skill-naming conversation.

If H&L is unavailable

Complete the notebook prediction and full workbook inventory. Record affected students for Day 5 catch-up. Do not invent cluster recommendations; add them only after H&L is restored.
