Teacher Facilitator Guide - 1SW Wk0 Day 4

My Career Journey Reflection

Core Day C - 50 minutes - Week 0 major grade

Collect and retain every handout. Students use it again at the mid-year reconciliation and final capstone. Schedule absent students for Day 5.

Open the student guide

Before students arrive

  • Print My Career Journey.
  • Prepare the sentence-stem and bilingual-support versions.
  • Open the 12-point rubric.
  • Prepare workbook page 22, H&L profiles, and the exit ticket.
  • Label a secure class folder “Week 0 - return at mid-year and capstone.”

Major-grade evidence

Score completeness, self-awareness, and connection between assessment data and career curiosity. Each dimension is 1-4 for 12 points total. Keep the raw score in Canvas or convert it consistently to the campus major-grade scale.

50-minute flow

1. Profile warm-up - 5 minutes

Students name one thing H&L knows now that it did not know on Day 2.

2. Gather evidence - 10 minutes

Students open profiles and notebooks and locate the five results before receiving the handout.

3. Complete My Career Journey - 30 minutes

Model item 6: a specific Hat plus two sentences tied to a type, value, or Building Block. Use workbook page 22 to frame item 8’s career community.

4. Exit ticket - 5 minutes

Students rank three clusters and justify each rank with their own Week 0 evidence.

Three active-monitoring laps
  1. Items 1-5 match the open profile.
  2. Item 6 names a career, not a cluster, and includes two sentences.
  3. Item 8 names three actual people.

If several students stall on item 6, pause and model one Hat Finder choice once for the room.

Language and access supports

Use the scaffolded handouts rather than translating the full lesson. Read prompts aloud, allow oral rehearsal, and point students back to the exact profile evidence each item requests.

Missing data or absence

Do not ask students to invent missing results. Mark the incomplete section, finish the missing H&L activity on Day 5, then complete and score the reflection. File the finished copy with the class set.
