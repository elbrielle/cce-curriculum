Complete model

Career: Veterinary Technician
Evidence: My Helper core type fits because I like supporting living things. Relationships is one of my work values. Caring for my dog is a Building Block that has taught me patience. I still need to learn the required education and daily work before deciding whether it fits.

Non-model: “Health Science because it fits me.” Health Science is a cluster, not a career, and the reason names no evidence.

50-minute flow

1. Profile warm-up · 5 minutes

What does the profile know now that it did not know on Day 2?

2. Gather evidence · 8 minutes

Students locate type, values, Building Blocks/skills, recommendations, and prediction. By minute 13, sources are open or missing items are marked pending.

3. Model + rubric · 5 minutes

Annotate the complete model and review three rubric ideas: complete evidence, accurate self-awareness, supported career connection.

4. Draft · 22 minutes

Three laps: items 1-5 match sources; item 6 is a career with two evidence-based sentences; item 8 contains three roles/people, with initials or fictional roles allowed for privacy.

5. Self-check + revise · 6 minutes

Students use the rubric and make one visible revision.

6. Submit once + cleanup · 4 minutes

One file, exact labeled text 1-8, or teacher-collected labeled paper. Confirm collection.

Pivot, trim, recovery
  • Missing result: mark pending, finish independent sections, protect Friday catch-up, then score.
  • Outage: use saved FYF/notebook evidence; never invent a recommendation.
  • Absent: same reflection during Friday catch-up or next clean period.
  • Trim warm-up/model talk first. Protect drafting, revision, and submission.
Access and privacy

Offer read-aloud, oral rehearsal, speech-to-text, dictation, and bilingual support. Students may name roles or initials in Career Community. Do not require public disclosure.
