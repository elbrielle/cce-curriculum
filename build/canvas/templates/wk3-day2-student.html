Today you will

  • learn core principles of user experience (UX) and interface design;
  • audit the Paws and Claws practice storefront;
  • record strengths, observable problems, and clear user-centered fixes; and
  • apply UX problem-solving to the Rosa tutoring mini-case.

1. Learn the UX Rules

Good UX helps a person quickly understand where to go and what will happen next. Bad UX makes the user hunt, guess, wait, or start over.

2. Investigate Paws and Claws

  1. Explore the site as a first-time customer trying to complete a purchase.
  2. Write three things that help the user.
  3. Write five observable problems that create friction.
  4. Choose three problems. For each one, propose a specific fix and explain the user benefit.
  5. Sketch one redesigned screen on FYF p. 29 and label your improvements.
