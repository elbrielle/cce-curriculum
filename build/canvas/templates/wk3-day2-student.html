1. Learn the UX rules

Good UX helps a person know where to go and what will happen next. Bad UX makes the person hunt, guess, wait, or start over.

2. Investigate Paws and Claws

  1. Explore the site as a first-time customer trying to complete a purchase.
  2. Write three things that help the task.
  3. Write five observable problems.
  4. Choose three problems. For each one, write a fix and explain how it helps the user.
  5. Sketch one redesigned page and label the fix it shows.
