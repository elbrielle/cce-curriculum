Week 2 - Day 2

Compare four programming careers

Use H&L and a second source to research work, training, pay, and demand.

Today you will

  • compare Software, Web, App, and Game Developer Hats;
  • record training, DFW pay, demand, and one reaction for each;
  • research one career in six fields; and
  • recommend a developer for a mobile-game project.

1. Build the four-Hat table

Open H&L > Information Technology > Programming and Software Development > Hat Finder. In your notebook, make four rows. For each Hat, record education, DFW salary, demand, and one Perk, Quirk, Job Gear item, or reaction.

2. Research one career

Open the career research worksheet

  1. Choose one of the four Hats.
  2. Use the Hat profile to complete all six fields.
  3. Use BLS Computer and IT Careers to confirm pay and training. App and Game Developers may use the broader Software Developers category.
Need an example or bilingual labels?

Open the Web Developer model or open bilingual field labels.

3. Make the hiring decision

Open the Day 2 exit ticket

You are done when

  • Your notebook has four complete Hat rows.
  • Your six-field worksheet uses H&L and BLS.
  • Your mobile-game recommendation names the best-fit developer and why.
Platform unavailable or absent?

Use the Web Developer model and BLS career pages. Complete one full career sheet and the exit ticket. Rebuild the four H&L rows during catch-up because Day 3 uses them.
