Week 2 - Day 5

Finish the required Xello task and make your IT call

Complete Personality Style, rate an IT pathway, and turn in the Week 2 major evidence.

Major evidence: turn in both sides of the IT Salary Comparison plus the four-sentence Career Fit Reflection. The rubric is worth 20 points.

1. Complete Xello Personality Style

  1. Open Xello through district SSO.
  2. Open About Me > Personality Style.
  3. Complete the quiz and review the result. Matchmaker must already be complete.
  4. Record one trait that sounds like you and one example from school, home, or an activity.

This is the required Grade 8 Xello task for Week 2. If your rostered account is missing, tell your teacher; do not make a second account.

2. Complete H&L Pathway Possibilities

  1. Open Information Technology > Pathway Possibilities.
  2. Answer the questions and rate at least one pathway.
  3. Review at least two IT Hats. Favorite the ones that fit. If neither fits, record both and explain why.

3. Finish the major reflection

On the salary worksheet, write four sentences: the IT pathway you rated; one salary-table fact; one IT career you reviewed and why it fits or does not fit; and whether you could see yourself in IT.

Open the 20-point rubric

4. Exit ticket

Open the Day 5 exit ticket

You are done when

  • Xello Personality Style is complete or your login issue is recorded.
  • You rated an IT pathway and reviewed two Hats.
  • Your salary worksheet and Career Fit Reflection are turned in.
  • Your exit ticket is complete.
