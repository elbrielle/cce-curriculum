Topic: Career Fit

I can: use one Personality Style result and career evidence to make an honest IT fit decision.

Show my learning: I will complete Xello Personality Style and submit the five-page salary packet with its four-part Career Fit Reflection.

Today you will

  • complete and review Xello Personality Style;
  • review an IT pathway and two H&L Hats;
  • finish the Career Fit Reflection; and
  • submit the five-page Minor 2 packet.
Minor 2 evidence: turn in the five-page IT Salary Comparison packet with the Career Fit Reflection on page 5. The rubric is worth 20 points.

1. Complete Xello Personality Style

  1. Open Xello through district SSO.
  2. Open About Me > Personality Style.
  3. Complete the quiz and review the result. Matchmaker must already be complete.
  4. Record one trait that sounds like you and one example from school, home, or an activity.

This is the required Grade 8 Xello task for Week 2. If your rostered account is missing, tell your teacher; do not make a second account.

2. Complete H&L Pathway Possibilities

  1. Open Information Technology > Pathway Possibilities.
  2. Answer the questions and rate at least one pathway.
  3. Review at least two IT Hats. Favorite the ones that fit. If neither fits, record both and explain why.

3. Finish the Minor 2 reflection

On page 5 of the salary packet, complete four labeled jobs: one Personality Style trait and example; the IT pathway or career you reviewed; one source-labeled salary, preparation, task, or demand fact; and your final IT fit decision.

Fit words: trait · example · evidence · preparation · task · fits · does not fit · might fit

Complete frame: My result included _____. This career fits / does not fit / might fit because the work requires _____, and my evidence is _____.

Open the 20-point rubric

4. Submit and close

  1. Check that pages 1-4 still show three complete career records and your comparison.
  2. Check that all four page 5 reflection jobs are complete.
  3. Submit all five pages through the Minor 2 route your teacher names.
Need a one-page replacement route?

If your original packet is unavailable, open the IT Pathway Decision. Your teacher will tell you which earlier salary evidence to attach.
