Response Home: Five-Page IT Salary Packet (p. 5: Career Fit Reflection — Minor 2) & Xello Personality Style.

Today you will

  • complete and review Xello Personality Style;
  • save three careers from your completed research packet;
  • finish the four-part Career Fit Reflection on page 5; and
  • submit the complete five-page Minor 2 packet.
Minor 2 Evidence: Turn in the five-page IT Salary Comparison packet with the Career Fit Reflection on page 5. The rubric is worth 20 raw points (converted to 100 in Canvas).

1. Complete Xello Personality Style

  1. Open Xello through district SSO.
  2. Go to About Me > Personality Style.
  3. Complete the quiz and review your result. If it does not open, use the sample result your teacher provides.
  4. Record one trait that sounds like you and one example from school, home, or an activity.

(Personality Style is a Grade 7 Xello task. Keep the result private and tell your teacher if it does not open.)

2. Save Three Researched Careers

  1. Mark three careers already recorded in your salary packet.
  2. Open Xello > Explore Careers.
  3. Search each exact career, confirm the title, and save it.

(Saving these three careers prepares Xello to open next week's Grade 7 Learning styles lesson. It is not another grade.)

3. Finish the Minor 2 Reflection

Make your copy: Personality Style, Three Careers, and IT Decision

On page 5 of the salary packet, complete four labeled jobs:

  1. One Personality Style trait or sample trait and a real example.
  2. The IT pathway or career you reviewed.
  3. One source-labeled salary, preparation, task, or demand fact.
  4. Your final IT career fit decision.
Need fit frames or Spanish vocabulary?

Spanish terms: Personality trait = Rasgo de personalidad · Fit = Compatibilidad o ajuste · Evidence = Evidencia · Preparation = Preparación requerida.

“My result included [trait]. This career [fits / does not fit / might fit] because the work requires [tasks/skills], and my evidence is [labeled salary/preparation fact].”

Open the 20-point rubric

4. Submit and Close

  1. Check that pages 1–4 show three complete career records and your comparison.
  2. Check that all four page 5 reflection jobs are complete.
  3. Submit all five pages through the Minor 2 route your teacher names.

You are done when

  • all five salary/reflection packet pages are turned in;
  • Personality Style and three saved careers are complete, or your teacher recorded the access need; and
  • your final IT decision uses a trait or sample trait and one labeled career fact.
