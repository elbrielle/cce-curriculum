Solve the machine breakdown

Use a technician’s five-step process, then research one Manufacturing career.

Response Home: Find Your Future workbook pp. 207–208 (Mystery Checklist) and your CCE Work OneNote (Career Research & Exit Ticket).

Today you will

  • identify the strongest breakdown clue;
  • complete all five checklist stages;
  • research one Manufacturing Hat; and
  • keep every pay and training fact connected to its source.

1. Learn the Technician Checklist

  1. Identify: Name the exact problem.
  2. Analyze: List possible causes.
  3. Implement: Carry out a testable solution.
  4. Test & Observe: Check if the fix worked.
  5. Prevent: Set up a routine so the problem doesn't happen again.

2. Solve the Label-Machine Mystery

Read all five clues on FYF pp. 207–208 before choosing a cause. Pay special attention to what changed immediately before the machine stopped.

Need the first stage modeled or Spanish vocabulary?

Spanish terms: Troubleshooting = Resolución de problemas · Identify = Identificar · Diagnose / Analyze = Diagnosticar / Analizar · Implement = Implementar · Evaluate / Test = Evaluar / Probar · Prevent = Prevenir.
