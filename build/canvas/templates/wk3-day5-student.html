Today's learning

Topic: Career Evidence

I can: evaluate a changing IT occupation by presenting accurate work, preparation, pay, growth, and exact/proxy evidence.

Show your learning: give a one-minute evidence pitch, complete the required Xello Learning Style quiz, and submit the Learning Style/IT connection with the app and career-evidence packet.

1. Give your one-minute pitch

  1. Name the selected career and the BLS occupation used.
  2. Name two or three work tasks.
  3. Name the typical preparation.
  4. State the BLS pay and growth numbers with their dates.
  5. Say whether the source is exact, closest occupation, or a proxy.
  6. Explain the technology, demand, or task change.

Your listener gives one evidence-based star and asks one question. The live pitch is communication practice; the teacher scores the written research evidence.

2. Complete Xello Learning Style

  1. Open Xello through district SSO.
  2. Open About Me > Learning Style.
  3. Complete the quiz and review the result.
  4. Write one recommended strategy or one strategy that already works for you on the connection sheet.

This is the required Grade 8 Xello task for Week 3. The live minimum is quiz completed. If your rostered account is missing, tell your teacher; do not create another account.
