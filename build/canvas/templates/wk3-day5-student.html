Week 3 - Day 5

Share your career research and learn how you learn

Give a one-minute pitch, complete the required Xello Learning Style quiz, and connect the result to an IT career.

1. Give your one-minute pitch

  1. Name the career and what the person does.
  2. Name the education needed.
  3. State the BLS pay and growth numbers.
  4. Say whether the BLS source is exact or a proxy.
  5. Explain what makes the career emerging.
  6. Name the DFW employer source.

Your group gives one star and one wish. The live pitch is communication practice; the teacher scores the research evidence you turned in.

2. Complete Xello Learning Style

  1. Open Xello through district SSO.
  2. Open About Me > Learning Style.
  3. Complete the quiz and review the result.
  4. Write your result and one useful learning strategy on the connection sheet.

This is the required Grade 8 Xello task for Week 3. If your rostered account is missing, tell your teacher; do not create another account.
