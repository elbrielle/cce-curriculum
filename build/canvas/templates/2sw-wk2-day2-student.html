Investigate Clinton Lake

Separate what each file shows from what someone claims.

Open the Clinton Lake Evidence Tracker.

1. Use four questions for every file

  1. What does it directly show?
  2. Who produced it?
  3. What can it not prove?
  4. How strongly does it answer the case question?

2. Review all six files

3. Write a careful conclusion

Choose the two strongest harm files, one source-related file, and one missing test or inspection. Your conclusion may say landfill cause, outside cause, mixed causes possible, or not enough evidence.

Optional Canvas counterevidence exchange

Post your strongest file and one limitation, then reply to a classmate with a different file that complicates the conclusion. Your teacher may use the private written route instead.

You are done when: all six rows are complete and the conclusion uses three files plus one uncertainty.
