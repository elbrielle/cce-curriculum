Explore legal careers

Look beyond “lawyer” and compare the work, preparation, and skills behind legal careers.

  • research one legal career using a dated source.

3. Research one career

Open the Career Research Worksheet. Use the source your teacher assigns. Record the source and date. If H&L is available, it is an exploration option; it is not the only way to finish.
