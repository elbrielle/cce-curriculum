Week 1 - Day 4

Solve two factory problems

Use the Robots for Crayons evidence to build a plan another worker could follow.

Today you will

  • separate case evidence from guesses;
  • choose a testable solution for each problem;
  • write ordered steps, tools, time, and production effect; and
  • name the next check if a fix fails.

1. Read the two problems

Read Color Confusion and Slowpoke Robot. Do not choose a fix yet.

2. Use the machine reference

Sort possible causes into three systems: color sensor, robotic arm, and production line. A possible cause is not proof.

3. Mark the shift evidence

Read through your assigned role: Shift Supervisor, Quality Control Specialist, Maintenance Technician, or Packaging Supervisor. Mark two clues for each problem.

4. Complete both action plans

Open the Robots for Crayons action plan

  1. Copy two clues from the source.
  2. Choose one solution you can test.
  3. Explain how it works.
  4. Write three ordered steps.
  5. Name tools or adjustments and estimate the time.
  6. Explain the production effect.
  7. Name a prevention step or next check.

5. Individual check

Choose one Manufacturing role. In two or three sentences, explain what that worker would do first and cite one clue from the case.

You are done when

  • Both evidence sections have two source clues.
  • Both plans include a test, steps, tools or adjustments, time, and production effect.
  • Both plans include prevention or a next check.
  • Your individual career-role response is complete.
If you were absent

The three images and the action-plan packet are the full route. Complete both plans independently. No robot or live presentation is required.
