Week 1 - Day 4

Program the factory floor and investigate two faults

Drive a Sphero RVR+ or simulator, then begin the Robots for Crayons case.

Today you will

  • run a three-block Sphero program;
  • take a production-team role;
  • identify the two factory problems; and
  • mark the strongest causes in the shift notes.

1. Join your robot team

Roles: Coder builds the program; Tester reports what happened; Navigator tracks distance and heading. Rotate roles after the first run.

Open SpheroEDU

No robot or Bluetooth?

Open the SpheroEDU simulator. Build and run the identical blocks on screen. Pace the taped course to estimate distance.

2. Build the mini-challenge

  1. Roll: forward for three seconds.
  2. Heading: set direction to 90°. Heading is a compass direction from the start, not “turn 90° from here.”
  3. Roll: forward for two seconds.
  4. Add Stop when the route is complete.
  5. Run, observe, and change one value.

3. Open the Robots for Crayons case

Read the two problems: Color Confusion and Slowpoke Robot.

Use the machine reference to find causes involving sensors, lighting, dust, calibration, motors, belts, and mismatched speeds.

4. Read the shift evidence

Take one role: Shift Supervisor, Quality Control Specialist, Maintenance Tech, or Packaging Supervisor. Circle the clues your role would investigate first.

5. Exit ticket

Open the Day 4 exit ticket

You are done when

  • Your team ran and revised a program.
  • Your role and case clues are recorded.
  • Your exit ticket names a first action, partner role, and clue.
