Week 1 · Day 4

Solve two factory problems

Use the Robots for Crayons evidence to build a plan another worker could follow.

Response Home: Use the one place your teacher assigns: CCE Work OneNote → Day 4: Robots for Crayons & Exit Ticket, or the printable Robots for Crayons Action Plan.

Today you will

  • separate case evidence from guesses;
  • choose a testable solution for each problem;
  • write ordered steps, tools, time, and production effect; and
  • name the next check if a fix fails.

1. Read the Two Problems

Read Color Confusion and Slowpoke Robot on FYF pp. 200–201. Look at what changed immediately before the breakdown.

2. Use the Machine Reference

Sort possible causes into three factory systems: color sensor, robotic arm, or conveyor line. A possible cause is not proof!

3. Mark the Shift Evidence

Read through your assigned role lens: Shift Supervisor, Quality Control Specialist, Maintenance Technician, or Packaging Supervisor. Mark two clues for each problem on FYF p. 202.

Need vocabulary or Spanish terms?

Spanish terms: Sensor = Sensor de color · Belt = Correa transportadora · Quality Control = Control de calidad · Production line = Línea de producción · Shift = Turno de trabajo.

4. Complete Both Action Plans

Open the response home your teacher assigned. Use CCE Work OneNote → Day 4: Robots for Crayons & Exit Ticket, or Open the Robots for Crayons action plan. Complete only one version.

  1. Copy two clues from the source notes.
  2. Choose one testable solution.
  3. Explain how it works.
  4. Write three ordered steps.
  5. Name tools or adjustments and estimate the repair time.
  6. Explain the production effect.
  7. Name a prevention step or next check.

5. Individual Check

At the bottom of the same OneNote page or printable plan, choose one Manufacturing role. In two or three sentences, explain what that worker would do first and cite one clue from the case.

You are done when

  • both plans in your assigned response home use two source clues and a testable solution;
  • both plans include ordered steps, tools or adjustments, time, production effect, and a next check; and
  • your individual career-role response is complete on the same page.
