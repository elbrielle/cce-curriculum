Manufacturing is more than an assembly line

Meet the people who design, build, program, repair, and test the products around you.

Today you will

  • compare six Manufacturing pathways;
  • explore the Manufacturing cluster in H&L;
  • record two careers and two questions; and
  • connect one career to your core personality type.

In your notebook, name one product you used this morning. Who designed it, built it, programmed the machines, and checked it?
