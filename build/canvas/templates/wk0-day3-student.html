What matters to you at work?

Add your work values and experiences to the Climber Profile. Make one connection you can use tomorrow.

Today you will

  • complete Discover Your Work Values;
  • record three Building Blocks and skills on FYF pp. 9-11;
  • save three Building Blocks in H&L; and
  • connect one value, experience, and possible cluster.
Do not copy everything everywhere. H&L stores your profile. FYF stores the Building Blocks inventory. Your notebook stores one connection.

1. Discover Your Work Values

  1. Open Hats & Ladders → Profile → Discover Your Work Values.
  2. Complete the activity honestly.
  3. In the private page Work Values + Building Blocks - Day 3, write: “My top work values are ____. I expected / was surprised by ____ because ____.”

2. Find your Building Blocks

Open FYF pp. 9-11. Record at least three real things you do and one skill each thing builds.

Model

Building Block: I help my younger cousin with homework.
Skill: Explaining clearly and patience.
Possible cluster: Education & Training, because those careers use clear explanations and patience.

Need ideas or bilingual support?

Open the Building Blocks word bank or open the bilingual word bank.

3. Save profile inputs

  1. Return to your H&L Climber Profile.
  2. Save at least three Building Blocks.
  3. Locate any recommended clusters. If none appear, write cluster pending. Do not make one up.

4. Make one connection

In the same private notebook page, finish:

One value I care about is ____. One Building Block that shows a related skill is ____. A cluster I may investigate is ____ because ____.

You are done when

  • your work values are saved or marked provisional;
  • FYF has three Building Blocks and skills;
  • three Building Blocks are saved in H&L or marked for catch-up; and
  • your one connection is complete.
