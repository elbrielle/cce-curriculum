Week 0 - Day 3

What matters to you at work?

Find your work values, name the experiences already building your skills, and check your first career-cluster recommendations.

Today you will

  • complete Discover Your Work Values;
  • list at least three Building Blocks and the skills they teach;
  • save at least three Building Blocks in Hats & Ladders; and
  • record your top three recommended clusters.

Get ready

  • Chromebook and Hats & Ladders login
  • Engineering notebook
  • Find Your Future workbook, pages 9-11

1. Make a work-value prediction

In your notebook, answer: Which matters most to me right now—achievement, independence, recognition, relationships, support, or working conditions? Why?

2. Discover Your Work Values

  1. Open Hats & Ladders.
  2. Select Profile.
  3. Open Discover Your Work Values.
  4. Complete the activity honestly.
  5. Write your top two values in a notebook entry labeled Wk0 Core Day B.

3. Find your Building Blocks

Open the workbook to pages 9-11. Building Blocks are things you already do at school, at home, in your community, or for fun. Each one teaches a skill.

  1. List at least three things you do.
  2. Beside each one, name a skill it builds.
  3. Share two with a partner.
Need Building Block ideas?

Open the Building Blocks word bank or open the bilingual word bank.

4. Add them to your profile

  1. Return to your H&L Climber Profile.
  2. Open Building Blocks.
  3. Add and save at least three real experiences.
  4. Find your recommended clusters and write the top three in your notebook.

5. Complete the exit ticket

Open the Day 3 exit ticket

You are done when

  • Your top two work values are in your notebook.
  • At least three Building Blocks are saved in H&L.
  • Your top three recommended clusters are recorded.
  • Your exit ticket is turned in.
Were you absent or is H&L unavailable?

Complete the workbook Building Blocks inventory and name the skill each experience teaches. Write your work-value prediction in your notebook. Tell your teacher which H&L activities you still need; complete them during Day 5 catch-up.
