H&L Setup and Discover Your Core

Core Day A - 50 minutes - TEKS d(1)(A), d(1)(B)

Presentation: Download the Day 2 slides. Speaker notes include timing, sources, the model, the pivot, and recovery steps. This guide contains the full lesson.
Protect this day. Students need this result for Day 3, the mid-year career-plan check, and the final capstone. If class is interrupted, move the complete lesson to the next clean period.

Open the student guide

  • Open the Day 2 presentation and Student Guide. The presentation includes the required Learning Your Core Personality Types frames; do not open a second Climber Notes deck.
  • Open Hats & Ladders and use the exact campus-issued sign-in method. The current page offers Continue with School, Google, or an issued username/password. Do not assume the school route means Clever or ClassLink.
  • Confirm the teacher account reaches the Coach Dashboard, the class roster exists, and students know which sign-in method they were assigned. Do not create a fake student for testing.
  • Advance once through the deck so the warm-up, six-type frames, notebook model, and close all display correctly.
  • Set one Find Your Future workbook per student to page 21. Page 22 is Thursday's reflection and stays closed today.
  • Have the private CCE Work page or physical equivalent ready.
  • Keep this guide available for recovery details; project the matching notebook model from the deck.
Roster or login not ready by the bell? Use the complete six-type chart route today. Students choose a provisional match, cite one phrase, and complete the same private notebook interpretation. Record the affected names and protect Friday catch-up for each student's own app activity. Never use another student's account.

What students produce

  • Completed Discover Your Core activity in Hats & Ladders
  • Private notebook interpretation with the result, one key phrase, one real question, and one career curiosity

Practice only: review the notebook response for evidence and explanation. Do not grade which personality type a student receives or create a second submission.

50-minute lesson flow

1. Warm-up - 5 minutes

Students predict one type: Doer, Analyzer, Creator, Helper, Persuader, or Organizer. Say: “This is a guess, not a grade. We will compare it with your result later.”

2. CTE and future planning - 5 minutes

Use Find Your Future page 21. Define CCMR and Program of Study. Ask: “Why does it help to explore careers before high school?” Keep this to five minutes so the assessment has enough time.

3. H&L profile setup - 10 minutes

Model the one campus-issued login route. Students confirm the account belongs to them, locate the profile area shown in the deck, and open Discover Your Core. If the displayed name is wrong, stop and record the access problem instead of editing or using the account.

4. Six types and Discover Your Core - 23 minutes

Use the Climber Notes frames embedded in the Day 2 presentation. Spend no more than five minutes on the six-type chart. Then direct students to Profile > Discover Your Core.

Before students begin, say: “Read every question. Honest answers are more useful than fast answers. No type is better than another.”

  1. First lap: confirm every student is in the correct activity.
  2. Second lap: stop students who are clicking without reading.
  3. Third lap: check that the notebook response selects one phrase, asks one real question, and applies the result to one career curiosity.

5. Interpretation and save check - 7 minutes

Complete model: “My result is Organizer. The phrase likes clear systems fits because I keep group projects on track. I wonder whether Organizers can also enjoy art. I am curious about event planning because it mixes systems with creative choices.” Students save this one entry, close H&L, return FYF to the class-set location, and show only the entry's location -- not private content -- during the final check.

Six-type reference and examples
Language and reading supports
  • Read assessment items aloud or use a fluent-reading peer when the student requests support.
  • Keep the six-type chart open while students write.
  • Offer: “My result fits me because...” and “I was surprised because...”
  • Accept an oral response before the student writes it.
If H&L or SSO is unavailable

Students use the six-type chart to choose a provisional match and complete the notebook response with evidence. Record affected students. They complete their own H&L activity during Day 5 catch-up or the first clean period of Week 1. Never use another student's account for an individual result.

Early finisher

Students explore two or three Hats connected to their top type and record one career that interests them and why.
