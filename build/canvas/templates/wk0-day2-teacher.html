50-minute lesson flow

1. Finish Monday's goal page - 8 minutes

Students open the same page they began Monday and finish only the blank fields: goal, why, action, checkpoint, confidence, and support. Students who are already finished begin the six-word prediction. Do not distribute a second page or add a separate notebook verification step.

2. CCMR purpose bridge + Core-type prediction - 4 minutes

Project FYF p. 21 and name the source: “This page is from Find Your Future, not H&L.” Read only the yellow CCMR definition aloud. Ask: Why is it helpful to think about future goals before high school? Take one turn-and-talk response, then say: “H&L will give us one clue about the kinds of work that may interest us.”

Explain that the six Core types are H&L interest patterns, not grades or permanent labels. Use the official chart in the presentation to introduce three types at a time. Students then record one prediction and one real-life clue on the class's private Day 2 response page. This is a guess, not a grade.

3. Google sign-in and Jumpstart Your Profile - 10 minutes

Students select Sign in with Google and use their IISD account. They confirm the account belongs to them, open the yellow Profile Climbs card, and complete Jumpstart Your Profile. If the displayed name is wrong, stop and record the access problem.

4. Discover Your Core - 18 minutes

From Profile Climbs, students open Discover Your Core. Use the current H&L screens in the presentation. If students need a reminder, name each type in one sentence instead of reteaching the full chart.

Before students begin, say: “The six types are H&L interest patterns, not grades or permanent labels. Read every question. Honest answers are more useful than fast answers.”

  1. First lap: confirm every student is in the correct activity.
  2. Second lap: stop students who are clicking without reading.
  3. Third lap: check that students can locate the private response route chosen for the class and have selected one interpretation route.

5. Private interpretation - 8 minutes

Complete model: “My H&L result is Organizer. I chose the phrase route: likes clear systems fits because I keep group projects on track.” Students record the result and choose one interpretation route on the class's private Day 2 response page.

6. DOL and device return - 2 minutes

Students identify their accurate stopping point, show that the private Day 2 page is located, close H&L, and return their assigned device using the class check-in routine. Do not require private content to be projected or read aloud.

Six-type reference and examples
Language and reading supports
  • Read assessment items aloud or use a fluent-reading peer when the student requests support.
  • Keep the six-type chart open while students write.
  • Offer: “My result fits me because...” and “I was surprised because...”
  • Accept an oral response before the student writes it.
If H&L or SSO is unavailable

Students use the six-type chart to choose a provisional match and complete the notebook response with evidence. Record affected students. They complete their own H&L activity during Day 5 catch-up or the first clean period of Week 1. Never use another student's account for an individual result.

Early finisher

Students explore two or three Hats connected to their top type and record one career that interests them and why.
