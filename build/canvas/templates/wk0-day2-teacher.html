Teacher Facilitator Guide - 1SW Wk0 Day 2

H&L Setup and Discover Your Core

Core Day A - 50 minutes - TEKS d(1)(A), d(1)(B)

Protect this day. Students need this result for Day 3, the mid-year career-plan check, and the final capstone. If class is interrupted, move the complete lesson to the next clean period.

Open the student guide

Before students arrive

  • Open the student guide and the Climber Notes deck, Learning Your Core Personality Types.
  • Confirm Hats & Ladders opens through the campus SSO route.
  • Write the six-type warm-up where students can see it.
  • Have engineering notebooks and Find Your Future page 21 ready.
  • Print or prepare the Day 2 exit ticket.

What students produce

  • Completed Discover Your Core activity in Hats & Ladders
  • Notebook entry with the top type, evidence from the description, and one possible career
  • Completed exit ticket

Minor-grade option: score the notebook response and exit ticket for evidence and explanation. Do not grade which personality type a student receives.

50-minute lesson flow

1. Warm-up - 5 minutes

Students predict one type: Doer, Analyzer, Creator, Helper, Persuader, or Organizer. Say: “This is a guess, not a grade. We will compare it with your result later.”

2. CTE and future planning - 5 minutes

Use Find Your Future page 21. Define CCMR and Program of Study. Ask: “Why does it help to explore careers before high school?” Keep this to five minutes so the assessment has enough time.

3. H&L profile setup - 10 minutes

Model the campus login route. Students locate Profile, confirm their name and grade, and notice which parts of the profile are still empty. Explain that this profile will collect evidence about their interests, values, and experiences all year.

4. Six types and Discover Your Core - 25 minutes

Project Climber Notes slides 2-4. Spend no more than five minutes on the six-type chart. Then direct students to Profile > Discover Your Core.

Before students begin, say: “Read every question. Honest answers are more useful than fast answers. No type is better than another.”

  1. First lap: confirm every student is in the correct activity.
  2. Second lap: stop students who are clicking without reading.
  3. Third lap: check that the notebook response uses evidence from the result description.

5. Exit ticket - 5 minutes

Correct answer: B. Sam should read the Organizer description and look for evidence from real life. A changes answers to force a result; C treats one result as permanent; D incorrectly says people can have only one type.

Six-type reference and examples
Language and reading supports
  • Read assessment items aloud or use a fluent-reading peer when the student requests support.
  • Keep the six-type chart open while students write.
  • Offer: “My result fits me because...” and “I was surprised because...”
  • Accept an oral response before the student writes it.
If H&L or SSO is unavailable

Students use the six-type chart to choose two possible matches, select the stronger match, and complete the notebook response with evidence. Record affected students. They complete the H&L activity during Day 5 catch-up or the first clean period of Week 1. Do not let one login issue stop the room.

Early finisher

Students explore two or three Hats connected to their top type and record one career that interests them and why.
