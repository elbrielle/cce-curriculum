Week 1 - Day 3

Design, build, and test a bike rack

Make the engineering choices a welder makes before a product reaches the factory floor.

Hot-glue safety: Glue guns stay on heat-safe mats. Wear craft gloves. Never touch fresh glue. At the five-minute warning, unplug the gun and leave it to cool.

1. Sketch the design

Your rack must hold several bikes, survive outdoor weather, and fit beside a sidewalk. Draw a top view and a side view.

2. Choose material and welds

Compare aluminum, stainless steel, and carbon steel. Choose one and explain the trade-off. Then select welding methods and label every planned joint on your sketch.

3. Build one prototype per pair

  1. Compare both sketches and choose the clearer design.
  2. Dry-fit sticks and straws before gluing.
  3. Build for seven minutes. Watch every joint.
  4. Press gently to test balance and strength.
  5. Identify one weak spot and the first improvement.
No glue station available?

Dry-fit the prototype and use masking tape at each joint. Run the same balance test and label the weld that would replace each taped connection.

4. Exit ticket

Open the Day 3 exit ticket

You are done when

  • Your sketch has top and side views with labeled welds.
  • Your material choice has a reason.
  • Your pair tested one prototype and named an improvement.
  • Your exit ticket is turned in.
