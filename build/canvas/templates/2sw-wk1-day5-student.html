2nd Six Weeks · Week 1 · Day 5

Finish the legal-career evidence

Revise the position paper, save one life experience in Xello, and make a career connection.

1. Revise and submit

Use the Legal Policy Position Paper Rubric. Check the position, two evidence details, legal-career connection, and completed Entrepreneur Card.

2. Complete the required Xello task

Open the Xello Life Experience Connection.

  1. Sign in through district SSO and open Xello.
  2. Open About Me, then Experiences.
  3. Add at least one life experience. It may come from home, school, a club, a team, a hobby, or volunteering.
  4. Save it and check that the entry appears.
  5. Connect the experience to one career from this week.

3. Record the current career choice

Name one legal career you want to investigate—or state that none is a current fit—and cite one job detail. H&L may be used if available, but a platform favorite is not required evidence for this lesson.

You are done when: the final evidence packet is submitted, at least one Xello life experience is saved, and the connection names a real experience and a specific career task.
Xello or absence route

Complete the paper connection now and show the teacher. The required Xello entry moves to the next supervised catch-up block. Do not create a second account or include private details you do not want to share.
