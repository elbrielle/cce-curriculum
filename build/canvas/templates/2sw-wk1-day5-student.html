  3. Explicit connection to a legal career pathway.

2. Complete the Assigned Life Experiences Task

Open the Xello Life Experience Connection:

  1. Sign into Xello via district Single Sign-On.
  2. Navigate to About Me → Experiences → Life Experiences.
  3. Click + Add Experience. Enter an authentic experience from home, school, sports, club, church, hobby, or community volunteering (do not share sensitive private information).
  4. Write a brief summary of what you did and learned, then click Save.

3. Connect Life Experience to a Career Path

On your connection sheet, record how the skills from your life experience (such as organizing, mediating conflict, following complex rules, or communicating clearly) connect to a specific legal career task.

Términos clave:

  • Transferable Skill (Habilidad transferible): Habilidad adquirida en la vida diaria o escolar que se puede aplicar en un trabajo profesional (ej. comunicación, puntualidad, mediación).
  • Authentic Experience (Experiencia auténtica): Actividad real en la que participaste y aprendiste algo valioso.

Connection Frame:

“My life experience in [Activity/Responsibility] taught me how to [Skill/Habit]. This connects directly to the work of a [Legal Career] when they must [Specific Daily Job Task].”
