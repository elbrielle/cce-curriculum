2. Learn what the six Core types mean

The six Core types are Hats & Ladders interest patterns. They describe kinds of activities that may feel interesting or natural. They are not grades, permanent labels, or workbook pages. Most people match more than one.

Follow along while your teacher uses the same chart shown below. Read the “In Your Life Right Now” examples before you make your prediction.
