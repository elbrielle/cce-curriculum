Week 0 - Day 2

Who are you at work?

Learn the six core personality types. Then use Hats & Ladders to find your starting type.

Today you will

  • predict one personality type that may fit you;
  • complete Discover Your Core in Hats & Ladders;
  • write your result and one reflection in your engineering notebook; and
  • complete the exit ticket.

Get ready

  • Chromebook
  • Engineering notebook
  • Find Your Future workbook, page 21
  • Hats & Ladders login

1. Make a prediction

In your notebook, write the one word that sounds most like you right now:

Doer - Analyzer - Creator - Helper - Persuader - Organizer

Do not look up the words yet. This is only your prediction.

2. Connect today to your future

Open your workbook to page 21. Read the short sections about CCMR and Programs of Study.

Think: Why does it help to explore careers before high school?

3. Learn the six types

Follow along while your teacher reviews the six types. No type is better than another. Most people match more than one.

Need help remembering the six types?

Use this chart to compare what each type likes to do.

4. Discover Your Core

  1. Open Hats & Ladders.
  2. Select Profile.
  3. Open Discover Your Core.
  4. Read every question and answer honestly.
  5. When your result appears, leave it open.

5. Record your result

Start a new notebook entry. Label it Wk0 Core Day A. Write:

  1. My top core personality type is __________.
  2. One line from the description that fits me is __________.
  3. One career that might fit this type is __________.
Sentence starters

My result fits me because...

I was surprised because...

This type may connect to the career _____ because...

6. Complete the exit ticket

Open the Day 2 exit ticket

Your teacher will tell you whether to complete it on paper or submit it digitally.

You are done when

  • Discover Your Core is complete.
  • Your result and reflection are in your notebook.
  • Your exit ticket is turned in.
