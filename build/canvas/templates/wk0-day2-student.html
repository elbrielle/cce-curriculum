Week 0 - Day 2

Who are you at work?

Learn the six core personality types. Then use Hats & Ladders to find your starting type.

Today you will

  • predict one personality type that may fit you;
  • complete Discover Your Core in Hats & Ladders;
  • interpret your result in your private CCE notebook.

Get ready

  • Chromebook
  • Private OneNote CCE Work page or the physical equivalent
  • Find Your Future workbook, page 21
  • Hats & Ladders login

1. Make a prediction

In your notebook, write the one word that sounds most like you right now:

Doer - Analyzer - Creator - Helper - Persuader - Organizer

Do not look up the words yet. This is only your prediction.

2. Connect today to your future

Open your workbook to page 21. Read the short sections about CCMR and Programs of Study.

Think: Why does it help to explore careers before high school?

3. Learn the six types

Follow along while your teacher reviews the six types. No type is better than another. Most people match more than one.

Need help remembering the six types?

Use this chart to compare what each type likes to do.

4. Discover Your Core

  1. Open Hats & Ladders.
  2. Select Profile.
  3. Open Discover Your Core.
  4. Read every question and answer honestly.
  5. When your result appears, leave it open.

5. Record your result

Open the private page Core Personality - Day 2. Add one short interpretation of the H&L result. This is not a separate notes packet.

  1. Result: My top core personality type is __________.
  2. Evidence: One phrase from the description that fits or surprises me is __________.
  3. Question: One real question I have is __________.
  4. Career connection: One career I am now curious about is __________ because __________.
Sentence starters

My result fits me because...

I was surprised because...

This type may connect to the career _____ because...

6. Save and locate your one entry

Save the page. Then show your teacher where the page lives without showing private details to the class. You do not need to copy this work into a second form.

You are done when

  • Discover Your Core is complete.
  • Your result, key phrase, question, and career curiosity are in one private notebook entry.
  • You can reopen the same entry tomorrow.
Were you absent or is Hats & Ladders not working?

Read the six-type chart above. Choose one provisional match and complete the private notebook entry with evidence. Tell your teacher that you still need your own Hats & Ladders activity during the next catch-up block.
