Topic: Nursing Career Evidence

I can: Compare three nursing careers and support one current fit with responsibility, preparation, work-condition, and pay evidence.

Show my learning: Nursing Career Evidence Map with one next fact to verify.

Today you will:

  • Compare three careers in the fixed Nursing Career Guide.
  • Keep the source labels attached to every pay fact.
  • Support one current fit and name one next research question.

1. Audit Three Careers

  1. Open the fixed Nursing Career Guide.
  2. Choose at least three nursing-related careers.
  3. Record one responsibility and one work condition for each career.
  4. Record the common preparation path.
  5. Copy one pay fact with its year, geography, and measure.
Optional Workbook Health Science App Tour

2. Compare and Decide

Open the Nursing Career Evidence Map:

  • List all three careers you compared today.
  • Choose the career that currently fits best and explain the evidence connection.
  • Write one concrete question you still need to research (e.g., high school endorsements, college prerequisite courses, clinical licensing).
Vocabulario y ayuda en español (Spanish Support & Sentence Frames)

Términos de exploración:

  • Responsibility (Responsabilidad): Una tarea que el trabajador debe completar.
  • Preparation (Preparación): Educación, formación o credencial común para una carrera.

Reflection Frame:

“[Career] is my strongest current fit because the guide shows [responsibility or preparation fact]. Before deciding, I still need to verify [specific fact].”

You are done when:
  • Three careers have the same evidence fields completed.
  • The Nursing Career Evidence Map includes a supported fit and research question.
