Save Three Careers

Keep careers for comparison. You are not committing to them.

Required Xello task: save at least three careers. Use the full 30-minute work block.
Optional workbook connection

1. Complete Xello Save careers

  1. Open Xello through ClassLink.
  2. Use Explore Careers or a profile recommendation.
  3. Open a career and review tasks, preparation, and work conditions.
  4. Save the career.
  5. Repeat until three careers appear in your saved list.

2. Compare your three careers

Open the Save Three Careers Reflection. Name one career that fits, one profile result or building block, and one fact to research next.

You are done when: three careers appear in Xello and the comparison is complete.
