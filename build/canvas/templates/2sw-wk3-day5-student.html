Save Three Careers

Keep careers for comparison. You are not committing to them.

Topic: Saved Career Evidence

I can: identify at least three career opportunities in Xello and explain one current fit with career or profile evidence.

Show my learning: save at least three careers in Xello and complete the Save Three Careers Reflection.

Today you will

  • save at least three careers in Xello;
  • compare the saved careers without treating them as commitments; and
  • complete the current-fit reflection with career or profile evidence.
Required Xello task: save at least three careers. Use the full 30-minute work block.
Optional workbook connection

1. Complete Xello Save careers

  1. Open Xello through ClassLink.
  2. Use Explore Careers or a profile recommendation.
  3. Open a career and review tasks, preparation, and work conditions.
  4. Save the career.
  5. Repeat until three careers appear in your saved list.

2. Compare your three careers

Open the Save Three Careers Reflection. Name one career that fits, one profile result or building block, and one fact to research next.

Use while you compare: save/guardar · preparation/preparación · work condition/condición de trabajo · evidence/evidencia · verify/verificar

Complete thought: _____ currently fits me because Xello shows _____ and my profile or Week 0 evidence shows _____. I still need to verify _____.
