Today you will

  • review a policy through an assigned perspective;
  • use controlled evidence to argue and judge; and
  • make your own decision and connect the work to a career.
Keep this at the system level. Discuss the hypothetical tool and policy—not a real person, case, or family. You may pass on speaking and submit the same written evidence.

1. Learn the legal-review routine

The workbook uses a city-park policy to teach the routine. Today's hypothetical court-tool policy uses the same support, oppose, judge, and revise structure.

2. Prepare and judge

Open the Legal Review Argument and Evidence Sheet. Use only its policy and evidence bank. Prepare your assigned side, listen or read the other side, then rule: approve, revise, or reject.

3. Connect the work to a career

Complete the Legal Entrepreneur and Association Card. Page 2 contains the fixed career and association bank, so no open-web search is required. Your final position can differ from the side you were assigned.

You are done when: the argument uses three evidence notes, the ruling explains its strongest evidence, the personal position uses at least two details, and the career card names a source and date.
