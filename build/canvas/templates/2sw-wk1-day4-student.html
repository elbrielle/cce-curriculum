1. Learn the Legal Policy Review Routine

In legal and policy work, attorneys and policy analysts must understand and anticipate the strongest arguments on both sides of an issue before making a recommendation.

2. Prepare Arguments and Issue a Ruling

Open the Legal Review Argument and Evidence Sheet. Use only the supplied policy and evidence bank:

  • Assigned Perspective: Draft at least 3 evidence-grounded points supporting or opposing the policy.
  • Judicial Ruling: Listen to or read the opposing argument, then write a formal ruling: Approve, Revise, or Reject with specific reasons.
  • Independent Position: State your own final stance (which may differ from your assigned side).

Vocabulario y ayuda en español (Spanish Support & Sentence Frames)

Términos legales y de asociaciones:

  • Professional Association (Asociación profesional): Organización que establece estándares éticos, ofrece credenciales y apoya el desarrollo profesional en una carrera.
  • Entrepreneurship (Emprendimiento): Capacidad de iniciar y administrar un negocio propio ofreciendo servicios especializados.
  • Judicial Ruling (Fallo o dictamen judicial): Decisión formal de un juez basada en la evidencia y en la ley aplicable.

Argument & Ruling Frames:

  • “The strongest evidence supporting/opposing this policy is [Fact/Evidence] because [Reasoning].”
  • “As a reviewer, I rule to [Approve / Revise / Reject] this policy because [Specific Safeguard/Risk].”
