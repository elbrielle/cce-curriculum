Week 5 · Day 1

Build a realistic route into cybersecurity

Start with a real cybersecurity task, then explore the people who do this work.

Write in: your Cybersecurity Career Route Guide.

Today you will

  • spot one warning clue on a practice message;
  • review Irving ISD cybersecurity options;
  • use one teacher-modeled career snapshot; and
  • sketch a possible preparation path.

1. Start with the Workbook

Open your Find Your Future workbook to p. 24. Point to one clue that would make a cybersecurity analyst pause. You will investigate the seven emails on p. 25 tomorrow, so leave that page blank today.

2. Find Irving ISD Options

Open your Find Your Future workbook to pp. 36–37. Find Irving ISD's Cybersecurity program of study and one school option you might investigate.

3. Use the Dated Career Guide

Open the Cybersecurity Career Route Guide

  1. Compare duties across support, networking, and information security roles.
  2. Use the printed analyst snapshot. Your teacher will model what median pay and projected growth mean.
  3. Explain the growth comparison and circle one limit of the data.
  4. Sketch your own now, high-school, and after-high-school options.
