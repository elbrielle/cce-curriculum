1. Choose the Scenario and Ten Items

Make your copy: Emergency Essentials Kit Design

Select one disaster scenario: Earthquake, Fire Evacuation, or Flood.

From the workbook list, select exactly ten items. For each item, write the specific problem it solves during the first 72 hours of the emergency.

2. Design and Defend

Sketch or label your 10-item kit layout so another responder could unpack it immediately in the dark or under pressure. In the defense section, explain why your top three items are essential for your specific scenario.

After reviewing with a peer or doing a self-audit, record one revision you would make to improve the kit.

3. Connect the Task to a Career

Complete the career reflection on your plan: “Firefighters, paramedics, and emergency management directors plan before an emergency by [Planning Task] so that [Intended Outcome].”

Vocabulario y ayuda en español (Spanish Support & Sentence Frames)

Términos de gestión de emergencias:

  • Emergency Management (Gestión de emergencias): Planificación y organización de recursos para responder a desastres y salvar vidas.
  • Triage / Prioritization (Priorización de recursos): Decidir qué suministros o acciones son más urgentes según la situación.
  • Constraint (Restricción o límite): Límite de peso, espacio o recursos (por ejemplo, tener espacio para exactamente 10 artículos).

Sentence frames for defense & revision:

  • “I selected [Item Name] because in a [Scenario], the immediate threat is [Danger] and this item provides [Function/Protection].”
  • “After evaluating my kit, I replaced [Old Item] with [New Item] because [Reason].”
You are done when:
  • One scenario is clearly selected.
  • Exactly ten items are selected and clearly labeled.
  • Your top three items have written justifications tied to the chosen scenario.
  • One clear revision is documented.
  • The career connection sentence explains pre-emergency planning.
