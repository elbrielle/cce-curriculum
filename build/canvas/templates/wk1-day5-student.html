Week 1 · Day 5

Use Matchmaker to study your interests

Answer honestly, inspect one result, and explain how an interest changed a match.

Response Home: Xello Matchmaker holds the assessment. For today’s writing, use the one place your teacher assigns: CCE Work OneNote → Day 5: Xello Matchmaker & Reflection, or the private Canvas Matchmaker Reflection.

Today you will

  • complete the first 39 Matchmaker questions in Xello;
  • review your career matches;
  • open Find out why for at least one career; and
  • submit your private three-part reflection in Canvas.
This is not a test with right answers. Use the full scale from Like Very Much to Dislike Very Much. Matchmaker suggests possibilities; it does not decide your future.

1. Open Matchmaker

  1. Open ClassLink > Xello.
  2. Go to About Me > Matchmaker.
  3. If Matchmaker starts with a goal question, answer it and continue. It is part of Matchmaker, not a separate assignment.

2. Complete Phase 1

  1. Answer the first 39 questions.
  2. Read each task before choosing a response.
  3. Use the full response scale.
  4. When your matches appear, open at least one career.
  5. Select Find out why and notice which interests raised or lowered the match.

3. Submit Your Private Reflection

Make your copy: Xello Matchmaker and Career-Match Reflection

Open the response home your teacher assigned. Use CCE Work OneNote → Day 5: Xello Matchmaker & Reflection, or open the private Canvas Matchmaker Reflection. Complete all three prompts in one place only.

  1. What result surprised you, and why?
  2. What did Find out why show about one career match?
  3. Give one example of how an interest raised or lowered a career match.
Need sentence frames or Spanish vocabulary?

Spanish terms: Matchmaker = Evaluador de intereses · Match score = Nivel de compatibilidad · Find out why = Descubre el porqué · Goal = Meta.

“I was surprised that [Career] appeared because [reason].”
“Find out why showed that [Career] matched because I like [interest/activity].”
“My interest in [activity] raised/lowered the match for [Career] because [reason].”

You are done when

  • the first 39 questions are complete in Xello;
  • you opened one career and selected Find out why; and
  • all three reflection prompts are complete in your assigned response home.
