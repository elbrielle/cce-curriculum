Week 1 - Day 5

Use Matchmaker to study your interests

Answer honestly, inspect one result, and explain how an interest changed a match.

Today you will

  • complete the first 39 Matchmaker questions;
  • review your career matches;
  • open Find out why for at least one career; and
  • submit a private three-part reflection.
This is not a test with right answers. Use the full scale from Like Very Much to Dislike Very Much. Matchmaker suggests possibilities; it does not decide your future.

1. Open Matchmaker

  1. Open ClassLink > Xello.
  2. Go to About Me > Matchmaker.
  3. If Xello asks for your after-high-school goal first, stop and tell your teacher. Do not guess or skip the prerequisite.

2. Complete Phase 1

  1. Answer the first 39 questions.
  2. Read each task before choosing a response.
  3. Use the full response scale.
  4. When your matches appear, open at least one career.
  5. Select Find out why and notice which interests raised or lowered the match.

3. Submit your private reflection

Open the private Matchmaker Reflection

  1. What result surprised you, and why?
  2. What did Find out why show about one career match?
  3. Give one example of how an interest raised or lowered a career match.
Sentence frames

I was surprised that _____ because _____.
Find out why showed that _____ matched because _____.
My interest in _____ raised/lowered the match for _____ because _____.

You are done when

  • The first 39 questions are complete.
  • You opened one career and Find out why.
  • All three reflection jobs are submitted privately.
  • Your teacher can verify Matchmaker in the Completion Standards report.
A platform is unavailable or you were absent

A paper interest sort or teacher sample can help you practice the reflection, but it does not count as Matchmaker completion. Complete Xello and the private reflection during supervised catch-up.
