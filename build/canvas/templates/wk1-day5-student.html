Week 1 - Day 5

Test, solve, and present

Run the factory-floor program, finish both fault plans, and connect the week to a Manufacturing career.

Major team evidence: your two-minute Robots for Crayons presentation is scored with the 20-point rubric.

1. Refine and run the robot

  1. Run the program twice.
  2. Check duration, heading, obstacles, and overshoot.
  3. Change one block at a time.
  4. Complete one official run on the floor or in the simulator.

2. Brainstorm both factory fixes

Write at least 15 ideas across Color Confusion and Slowpoke Robot. One idea per sticky note. Do not judge ideas yet.

3. Complete the action plan

Pick the strongest idea for each problem. A plan is ready only when your team can name the steps, tools, time, and production improvement.

4. Present for two minutes

  • Name and explain both problems.
  • Give the chosen solution for each and why it won.
  • Explain repair steps, tools, estimated time, and production impact.
  • Name one Manufacturing career your team would pursue.
