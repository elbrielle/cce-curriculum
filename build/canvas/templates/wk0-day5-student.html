Career Perks, Neutrals & Quirks

The same career detail can feel different to different people.

1. Learn the three marks

Open your Find Your Future workbook to p. 4.

Mark Meaning Visual
PerkA detail that fits what you value or prefer.👍
NeutralA detail that neither attracts nor discourages you.➖
QuirkA demanding or unusual detail that may not fit you.👎
Model

Detail: A veterinary technician may work evening or weekend emergency shifts.
Mark: Quirk
Why: I prefer a predictable daytime schedule, so changing hours would be difficult for me.
Source: Hats & Ladders career profile.

2. Complete your career table

Make your copy: Career Perks, Neutrals, and Quirks

  1. Choose one career from an FYF profile, a Hats & Ladders recommendation, or the profile your teacher provides.
  2. Write at least four specific details about duties, schedule, work setting, training, or tools.
  3. Mark each detail as a Perk, Neutral, or Quirk for you.
  4. Explain each mark with a work value or preference.
  5. Name the source you used.

Word bank: perk · neutral · quirk · duty · schedule · work setting · training · work value · preference

Optional sentence stem: “I marked ___ as a ___ because I value/prefer ___.”

You are done when

  • you have at least four specific career details;
  • every detail has a P/N/Q mark and a personal reason; and
  • you named the source you used.
Remember: A quirk is not automatically “bad.” It is a tradeoff to understand before you decide whether a career fits you.
