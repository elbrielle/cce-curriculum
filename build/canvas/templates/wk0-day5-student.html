  4. Show the finished work to your teacher.

Path B: Perks, quirks, and two Hats

A perk is a part of a career you may enjoy. A quirk is unusual or challenging. The same detail can feel different to different people.

  1. Open the H&L Hat Finder.
  2. Choose two careers: one familiar and one new to you.
  3. Use H&L and the BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook.
  4. On workbook page 5, record pay, training, powerskills, other skills, work environment, and one additional fact.
  5. Mark each detail as a perk, neutral, or a quirk for you.
