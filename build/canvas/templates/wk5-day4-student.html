Week 5 - Day 4

Choose a favorite career cluster in Xello

Protect the full 40 minutes, save at least one cluster, and explain one career connection.

Required Grade 8 Xello task: Favorite clusters. H&L and live CyberSeek exploration are optional.

Today's learning

Topic: Favorite Clusters

I can: identify a career opportunity within a cluster and explain why it connects to my interests.

Show your learning: save at least one Favorite cluster in Xello and record one cluster-career-interest connection.

Today you will

  • Open Xello Favorite clusters.
  • Explore cluster descriptions and careers.
  • Save at least one cluster and record one career-interest connection.

1. Sign in and open Favorite clusters

  1. Use district SSO to open Xello.
  2. Open About Me.
  3. Open Favorite clusters.
  4. Read cluster descriptions and example careers.
  5. Save at least one favorite cluster.

2. Explore before you decide

You may also open Explore careers, filter by cluster, and inspect careers inside more than one cluster. A career can connect to more than one cluster.

3. Record the connection

Open the Favorite Cluster Connection

You are done when

  • At least one cluster is saved in Xello.
  • You named one career inside a cluster.
  • You connected the career to an interest or reason.
  • Your access issue is recorded if Xello was unavailable.
Xello unavailable or absent?

Tell your teacher about the access issue and complete the paper connection with a cluster you already know. The paper is temporary evidence; complete the required Xello save during the next catch-up block.
