Week 5 · Day 4

Which cybersecurity work fits me?

Compare the work, then name one interest and one next question.

Write in: the “Jot down your thoughts on this cluster” space in your Find Your Future workbook, p. 38.

Today you will

  • compare three kinds of IT work;
  • find one Irving ISD option that could help you explore; and
  • explain one interest or question on workbook p. 38.

1. Match the work

Which task would you try: help someone fix a computer problem, maintain a network, or investigate a suspicious message? Tell a partner one reason. You can say none of these if you explain why.

2. Find a school connection

Open your Find Your Future workbook to pp. 36–37. Find the Cybersecurity program of study and one school option you might investigate. Your plan can change as you learn more.

3. Compare roles

Review the three short role descriptions on your Day 1 Cybersecurity Career Route Guide. Match each role to the task it does. Which sounds most or least like you? Give a reason from the work.

4. Explore and write once

Open your workbook to p. 38. If your class uses Hats & Ladders, open ClassLink → Hats & Ladders → Clusters → Information Technology and follow the workbook's exploration steps.

In the “Jot down your thoughts” space on p. 38, name one role you might try or reject, one reason, and one question or school action. You can add a small drawing or emoji.

Need a word bank or sentence frame?

Words: help · maintain · protect · investigate.

“I might try ___ because ___. I still want to know ___.”

You are done when

  • you matched the work to a role; and
  • your p. 38 thought has a reason and a next question or action.
