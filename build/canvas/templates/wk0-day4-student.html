Week 0 - Day 4

Tell your career story so far

Use your Week 0 results to complete the My Career Journey reflection.

This is the Week 0 major grade. Use your open Climber Profile instead of guessing from memory.

Today you will

  • review your H&L results;
  • complete all eight reflection sections;
  • name three people in your career community; and
  • rank your recommended clusters.

Get ready

  • Chromebook with your H&L Climber Profile open
  • Engineering notebook
  • My Career Journey handout
  • Find Your Future workbook, page 22

1. Gather your Week 0 evidence

Before writing, locate these five things in your profile and notebook:

  1. top core personality type;
  2. top two work values;
  3. top three Building Blocks;
  4. top three recommended clusters; and
  5. your original personality prediction.

2. Open the reflection

Open My Career Journey

Complete all eight sections. Item 6 asks for a specific career, not a cluster. Explain your interest in two sentences.

Need sentence starters or bilingual support?

Open the sentence-stem version or open the bilingual-support version.

3. Build your career community

Read workbook page 22. Name three real people who can encourage you, answer questions, or help you stay on track. A family member, teacher, coach, counselor, friend, or future mentor can belong in your career community.

4. Check before turning it in

  • All eight sections are complete.
  • Items 1-5 match your profile.
  • Item 6 names a career and includes two sentences.
  • Item 8 names three people.

5. Complete the exit ticket

Open the Day 4 exit ticket

You are done when

  • Your complete My Career Journey handout is turned in.
  • Your cluster ranking exit ticket is turned in.
