2nd Six Weeks · Week 2 · Day 3

Run a safe trail-response simulation

Practice communication, consent, observation, and documentation—not medical treatment.

Simulation only. Never practice on an injury. Do not straighten, pull, reduce, or force a body part. Choose a model/mannequin, consenting uninjured partner, or observer/documenter route. Stop immediately for pain, tingling, numbness, color change, distress, or a request to stop.

Open the Injured on the Trail Simulation Record.

1. Meet the career

2. Learn what the materials represent

3. Observe, explain, improve

Run two short rounds. Narrate what each material represents, keep it loose, check comfort, and record one improvement. The observer/documenter records the same evidence and earns the same credit.

You are done when: the research section and both round columns are complete, and one safety or communication improvement is explained.
