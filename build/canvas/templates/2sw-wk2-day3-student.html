Safety Boundary — Simulation Only:

Never practice on a real injury. Do not pull, straighten, or apply tight pressure. Choose a mannequin, consenting uninjured partner, or the observer/documenter route. Stop immediately if anyone expresses discomfort.

3. Observe, Communicate, and Improve

Complete two short simulation rounds with your partner or role card:

  • Consent & Communication: Introduce yourself, state your role, and ask for permission before acting.
  • Observation & Action: Narrate each step calmly so the patient understands what you are doing.
  • Post-Round Documentation: Record what went well and identify at least one safety or communication improvement.
Vocabulario y ayuda en español (Spanish Support & Sentence Frames)

Términos de respuesta y primeros auxilios:

  • Consent (Consentimiento): Permiso verbal explícito que el paciente da antes de recibir asistencia.
  • Immobilization (Inmovilización): Mantener una extremidad quieta para evitar más dolor o daño durante el traslado.
  • Observation vs. Treatment (Observación vs. Tratamiento médico): Registrar el estado visible del paciente sin intentar procedimientos médicos no autorizados.

Simulation Communication Frames:

  • “Hello, my name is [Name]. I am trained in basic first response. May I examine your wrist/ankle to help support it?”
  • “In Round 1, I learned to improve communication by [Explaining each movement / Checking comfort level].”
