3. Apply the Safe Response Protocol

Pause → Check in the official app or with a contact you already know → Report it → Delete it. Do not use a link or phone number inside a suspicious message.

In the margin of p. 25, finish this sentence: A cybersecurity worker shows integrity by ___ because ___.

You are done when

  • you can explain the hardest choice using a clue you saw; and
  • your margin sentence explains an honest, safe response.

Practice-message safety rules

Use fictional people and an example.com address. Do not use real names, credentials, IDs, live links, QR codes, attachments, or Irving ISD impersonation. Keep the draft on paper or in the assigned document. Never send or post it.
