Get ready

Open the City Council Town and Ordinance Plan.

1. Learn the three tests

A strong ordinance is clear, fair, and realistic to enforce.

2. Build from town to law

  1. Name the town, climate, transportation, special feature, and jobs.
  2. Write one possible problem in each of four areas.
  3. Draft two ordinances. Each needs the rule, why it is needed, and a realistic consequence.
  4. Use feedback or the self-check to revise one ordinance.

3. Name the worker

Name one worker who could create, explain, review, or enforce one ordinance. State the exact task that worker would do.

You are done when: the town has enough detail to create problems, all four problem rows are filled, both ordinances answer all three questions, one revision is explained, and one worker is connected to a specific task.
Need a sentence frame?

“In the town of ___, people must/may not ___ because ___. A person who breaks the ordinance will ___.” A consequence must be possible for the town to enforce.
