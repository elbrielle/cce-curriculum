1. Learn the Three Tests for an Ordinance

A strong municipal ordinance must pass three essential tests:

  1. Clear: Citizens know exactly what is allowed and what is prohibited.
  2. Fair: The rule applies equally to all community members without bias.
  3. Realistic to Enforce: City workers have the actual tools, personnel, and authority to enforce it.

Vocabulario y ayuda en español (Spanish Support & Sentence Frames)

Términos clave:

  • City Council (Concejo municipal): Grupo de funcionarios elegidos que aprueban leyes y ordenanzas locales para una ciudad.
  • City Attorney (Abogado/a municipal): Asesor legal que revisa que las ordenanzas cumplan con las leyes estatales y constitucionales.
  • Code Enforcement Officer (Oficial de cumplimiento de códigos): Inspector que verifica que los negocios y residentes sigan las normas de la ciudad.

Ordinance drafting frame:

“In the town of [Town Name], all residents/visitors must/may not [Specific Action] because [Reason/Public Safety Need]. Any person who violates this ordinance will receive [Enforceable Consequence, e.g., a $50 fine or mandatory community service].”
