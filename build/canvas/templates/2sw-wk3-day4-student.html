2nd Six Weeks · Week 3 · Day 4

Write a Careful Handoff

Use fictional data. Record what is known without diagnosing.

Privacy boundary: use only the fictional cards. Do not share personal health information or take measurements from classmates.

Open the Fictional Patient Handoff Cards and open the individual handoff record.

1. Use five parts

  1. Situation: where and what needs attention?
  2. Observations: values and exact reported symptoms.
  3. Comparison: change, no change, or unreliable data.
  4. Handoff: which supervised role receives the report?
  5. Reasoning: which two facts support the action?
See the workbook report structure

2. Revise with the rubric

Open the 16-point rubric. Remove any diagnosis from the observation section.

3. Check the misconceptions

Take the ungraded Vital Signs and Handoff Check. You may retry it. Your teacher may use the paper fallback.

You are done when: the handoff uses two case facts, reports the person's words, and names a supervised next step without diagnosing.
