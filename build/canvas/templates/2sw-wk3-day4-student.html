1. Structure Your 5-Part Handoff

Open the Fictional Patient Cards and the Individual Handoff Record.

  1. Situation: Location, room number, and immediate context requiring attention.
  2. Observations: Numerical readings (heart rate, O2, BP) and exact patient words in quotes.
  3. Comparison / Trend: Worsening, improving, or unchanged compared to baseline.
  4. Supervised Handoff: The specific licensed role (RN, Charge Nurse, Physician) receiving your report.
  5. Reasoning: Two specific case facts explaining why this handoff is urgently needed.

Vocabulario y ayuda en español (Spanish Support & Sentence Frames)

Términos clínicos y de comunicación:

  • Objective Observation (Observación objetiva): Signos visibles y datos medibles sin emitir juicio o diagnóstico personal.
  • Subjective Report (Reporte subjetivo): Las palabras exactas que el paciente usa para describir su dolor o molestia.
  • Supervised Handoff (Entrega supervisada de información): Transferencia verbal y escrita de datos al enfermero/a supervisor/a para coordinar la atención médica inmediata.

Clinical Handoff Sentence Frame:

“The monitor shows [Vital Sign Reading] and the patient reports “[Exact Patient Words]”. This represents a [Trend: Change / Decline] from baseline. I am reporting this immediately to the [Supervising RN / Physician] because [Two Evidence Facts].”

You are done when:
  • All 5 handoff parts are filled with case facts and exact patient statements.
  • No medical diagnosis is asserted in the observation field.
  • The supervising nursing role is specified.
