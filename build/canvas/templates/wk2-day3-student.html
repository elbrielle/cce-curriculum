Week 2 · Day 3

How do we recover and read career data?

Repair a failed test, then compare three careers using one dated table.

Write in: Find Your Future p. 27 and page 4 of your five-page IT Salary Comparison packet.

Today you will

  • plan four fixes for a cybersecurity team's failed test;
  • learn what a national median and a job-growth estimate mean; and
  • compare three careers using one dated table.

1. Flip the Failure

Open your Find Your Future workbook to pp. 26–27. Read the four failed test events on p. 26. On p. 27, write one fix and one way to help the team stay focused for each failure.

2. Read One BLS Row With Your Teacher

Open your five-page salary packet

Turn to p. 4. BLS is the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Your teacher will show you how to read the job title, U.S. median yearly pay, and projected job growth. The three rows you need are already on this page.

Median means the middle wage. Projected means estimated future change. Neither number promises a starting wage or a job.

3. Compare the Three Careers

Answer the four questions on packet p. 4. Use the numbers from the same table. Keep your three app records on pp. 1–3 as they are.

You are done when

  • FYF p. 27 has a fix and team tip for each failure;
  • packet p. 4 compares the three dated salaries and growth estimates; and
  • your career choice uses two facts and names one data limit.
