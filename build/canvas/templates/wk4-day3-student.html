Week 4 · Day 3

Build a help desk troubleshooting sequence

Use MakeCode or the equal paper logic route to make Button A advance steps and Button B show FIXED.

Response Home: Help Desk Program Evidence Sheet (Minor 3 Part A) & Day 3 Branching Exit Ticket.
Minor 3 Evidence Begins Today: Troubleshooting logic, test records, and durable backup form the technical portion of the 16-point Minor 3 rubric. (Hardware is not part of the score.)

Today you will

  • choose a help desk scenario and assign team roles (Driver, Navigator, Tester, Recorder);
  • build or trace a 3-step troubleshooting sequence in MakeCode;
  • program Button A to loop through steps and Button B to confirm resolution; and
  • save durable program evidence and complete the Day 3 exit check.

1. Get a Scenario, Roles, and Project Name

Open the scenario cards. Name your project Period - Team - Help Desk.

  • Driver: controls the one team device.
  • Navigator: reads block instructions one at a time.
  • Tester: predicts and runs the A/A/A/A/B button test sequence.
  • Evidence Recorder: writes down observations and results.

2. Build or Trace the Logic

Supplied Printer Model: Step 1: Check power cable. Step 2: Check paper tray. Step 3: Restart the printer.

Dry Trace Logic: On start, step = 1. Pressing A advances 1 → 2 → 3 → 1 (loops). Pressing B displays "FIXED."

Open the block-order guide · Open the step-sort cards · English/Spanish step cards

3. Test, Save, and Clean Up

Open the Help Desk Program Evidence

  1. Stop building when your teacher calls time.
  2. Run A/A/A/A/B and record the output on your evidence sheet.
  3. Use your class backup method (screenshot, share link, downloaded .hex, or signed paper trace).

4. Exit Check

Open the Day 3 branching exit ticket

You are done when

  • your three support steps are in a safe, logical order;
  • the Button A trace advances through all three steps and loops correctly;
  • Button B displays "FIXED"; and
  • your evidence sheet, durable backup, and branching exit check are complete.
