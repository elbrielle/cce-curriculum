Week 3 · Day 3

Turn an app idea into four testable screens

Plan for a real user, draw the flow, test it, and revise it.

Response Home: Find Your Future pp. 30–33 / Wireframe Template (Major 1 Part A).
Major-grade evidence begins today: Your app plan, four screens, and two feedback-based improvements form the design portion of the 16-point Major 1 rubric.

Today you will

  • choose an app brief and plan for a specific target user;
  • draw a four-screen mobile flow (Home, Main Menu, Action, Success);
  • conduct a partner walkthrough test; and
  • make and star at least two visible revisions based on user feedback.

1. Choose and Plan

On FYF p. 30, choose: Food Connection, Stress-Less, or Passion Project. Name your app, identify a specific target user, and list two or three core features.

2. Draw the Four-Screen Flow

  1. Screen 1 (Home): Introduce the app and show the primary entry action.
  2. Screen 2 (Main Menu): Present clear, labeled navigation choices.
  3. Screen 3 (Action): Show what happens during user interaction.
  4. Screen 4 (Success / Confirmation): Clearly show what finished or updated.

Open the wireframe template · Open the bilingual template

Need wireframe symbols and Spanish terms?

Spanish terms: Wireframe = Esquema o boceto de pantalla · Flow = Flujo de navegación · Target User = Usuario objetivo · Feedback = Retroalimentación.

3. Test and Improve

Have your testing partner point to where they would tap next on each screen. Record any confusion they experience. Make at least two visible changes on your wireframe and star (★) both revisions.

Open the 16-point Major 1 rubric

You are done when

  • your plan on FYF p. 30 names a specific user and useful features;
  • all four screens show a clear next action;
  • you recorded where your tester tapped and one point of confusion; and
  • two revisions are visible and starred on your wireframes.
Absent or no partner?

Use a teacher, classmate in catch-up, or self-recorded walkthrough. The reviewer must say where they would tap and name one confusion. Write the feedback before making your two starred changes.
