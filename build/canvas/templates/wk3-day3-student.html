Week 3 - Day 3

Turn an app idea into four testable screens

Plan for a real user, draw the flow, test it, and revise it.

Major-grade evidence begins today: your app plan, four screens, and two feedback-based improvements become three parts of the 16-point rubric.

Today's learning

Topic: App Wireframes

I can: identify app design as an IT career by planning, testing, and revising a four-screen flow for a specific user.

Show your learning: turn in an app plan, Home/Main Menu/Action/Success screens with clear next actions, written walkthrough feedback, and two starred changes tied to that feedback.

Today you will

  • Choose an app brief and plan for one user.
  • Draw a four-screen flow.
  • Test the flow and make two visible improvements.

1. Choose and plan

Choose Food Connection, Stress-Less, or Passion Project. Name the app, identify a specific target user, and list two or three useful features.

2. Draw the four-screen flow

  1. Home: show the app and the first action.
  2. Main Menu: show the choices.
  3. Action: show what happens after one choice.
  4. Success: show what changed or finished.

Open the wireframe template · Open the bilingual template

3. Test and improve

Your partner points to where they would click next. Write the confusion they find. Make at least two changes and star both changes.

Open the 16-point major rubric

You are done when

  • Your plan names a specific user and useful features.
  • All four screens show a clear next action.
  • You recorded where the reviewer tapped and one confusion.
  • Two revisions are visible and starred.
Absent or no partner?

Use a teacher, classmate in catch-up, or recorded walkthrough. The reviewer must say where they would tap and name one confusion. Write the feedback before making your two starred changes.
