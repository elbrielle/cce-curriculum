Open the Fictional Patient Care Report and Safety Plan. Open the evidence rubric.

1. Complete the report and career connection

Write scene, observed condition, simulated action, reasoning, and handoff facts. Then name the EMT or Search and Rescue documentation responsibility the report represents.

2. Choose one complication

Thunder: move toward an appropriate enclosed building or hard-topped vehicle. Fast or unknown water: do not enter it. Anxiety: communicate calmly and honestly. Add a reassessment trigger and alternative.

3. Check, revise, and submit

Use all four rubric rows. Revise one sentence for accuracy or safety. Submit the completed written report and plan through private file upload or text entry, or turn in a labeled paper copy. Speaking is optional; a media recording alone is not the Major evidence.

You are done when: all five report sections, the career-role connection, and all four complication-plan prompts are complete.
