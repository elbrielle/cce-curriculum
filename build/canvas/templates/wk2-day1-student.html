On FYF p. 23, choose: shut the app down immediately or keep it running while the engineering team fixes the vulnerability. Explain one cost of your choice for users and one cost for the company.

Need decision frames and Spanish vocabulary?

“I would [shut down / keep open] because leaving the app open could [user/company risk], while shutting it down could [business/service consequence].”
