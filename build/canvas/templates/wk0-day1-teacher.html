Teacher Facilitator Guide - 1SW Wk0 Day 1

Lab Routines and Your Choice

Flex day - 50 minutes - routines, Xello access, or Classroom Career Hunt

Choose the path before class. The default is lab routines and the safety contract. Add Xello or the Career Hunt only if time and campus conditions allow. Protect Days 2-4.

Open the student guide

Before students arrive

  • Post Time, Voice, Body and the room’s Chromebook/equipment routines.
  • Prepare engineering notebooks and decide the Day 1 artifact.
  • Print the safety contract; keep the bilingual version available.
  • If using Xello, verify district SSO.
  • If using the Career Hunt, open workbook pages 2-3 and prepare the starter sheet.

Student evidence

Use the signed safety contract or teacher-selected routine artifact. If Xello runs, record login plus the after-high-school goal. If the Career Hunt runs, collect workbook page 3 or the Day 1 exit ticket.

Gradebook: this is a completion-based minor-grade option. Do not grade a student’s career certainty.

Default 50-minute flow

1. Notebook warm-up - 5 minutes

Students label the notebook and write or draw a possible future. Normalize “I do not know yet.”

2. Lab routines and safety - 35 minutes

Model Chromebook checkout/return, permitted sites, equipment respect, voice levels, movement, backpack location, clear walkways, food/drink rules, and damage reporting. Students add any room-specific rules and sign the contract.

3. Close and collect - 10 minutes

Practice the room reset exactly once. Collect the assigned artifact and explain how guardian signatures will be returned.

Compressed routines + Career Hunt flow

Warm-up 5; routines 20; workbook pages 2-3 and partner hunt 20; share/exit 5. Monitor for each pair naming the invisible career behind at least one object.

Xello route and access fallback

Allow 25 minutes for login and About Me > after-high-school goal. Do not add Matchmaker. Record blocked accounts and move students to the Career Hunt; do not stop the class to repair one roster.

Language and access supports
  • Use the bilingual safety contract for home access.
  • Pair students during the Career Hunt and offer the prefilled starter sheet.
  • Accept a drawn notebook response with an oral explanation.
