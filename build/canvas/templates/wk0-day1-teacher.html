Copy-ready OneNote page

In the Content Library, create one editable page named Notebook Setup + First-Week Goal. Copy this block into the page. Do not use a PDF printout as the digital response surface. In OneNote, place each writing job in one wide native 1×1 table cell so the response box expands as the student types; do not use underscore lines as the response control.

50-minute flow

1. As students enter and Do Now · 5 minutes

Students open the Day 1 Student Guide in Canvas. Project: You are starting a new class. What is one thing a student can do to make the first week go well? Hold silent think time, then offer partner sharing or a private response. Use the remaining time to model the daily routine: start in Canvas, open only the activity named in the directions, use the notebook when directed, and turn in only the named work. Connect one useful first-week habit to the specific goal students will write later.

2. Locate the course tools · 8 minutes

Students return to this Student Guide, point to FYF, and locate H&L and Xello without starting. By minute 13, at least 80% should return to the guide independently. If not, stop and model the route once more.

3. Set up the notebook · 12 minutes

Students enter the private notebook or label the three physical sections. Under CCE Work → Notebook Setup + First-Week Goal, they write: “The place I will use for CCE work is ____. If it does not open, I will use ____.” Record access problems, move students to Canvas/paper, and continue directly to the goal.

4. First-week CCE goal · 17 minutes

Students complete the six fields on the distributed OneNote page or one-page paper equivalent. This preserves the field order and response-space design from a teacher-created AVID first-week goal-setting sheet. Do not collect another class’s grades, count planners, or score supplies.

5. Partner or private check · 5 minutes

Students may share only the action and checkpoint, or complete the private self-check. They do not have to share the goal or reason.

6. Close and return devices · 3 minutes

Students point to their action and checkpoint fields and name tomorrow’s Canvas starting point. Then they follow the class device-return routine. Preview that H&L begins building the Climber Profile tomorrow. If most of the class stopped before the plan fields, record the last field reached and finish those fields in the first five minutes of Day 2 without distributing a second goal page.

Complete model and non-model

Model

Goal: By Friday, I will complete Discover Your Core carefully.
Why: I want the result to be based on my real answers.
Action: I will read every question and ask for help if a word is unclear.
Checkpoint: Tuesday during the 20-minute H&L block.
Confidence: 4
Support: Teacher check-in and the Canvas/paper fallback if my account does not open.

Non-model

Goal: Do better. Action: Try harder later. Confidence: 5.

Ask what is missing: a visible action, a real checkpoint, and a support/recovery route.

Monitoring, pivot, trim, recovery

  • Minute 25: every student has a digital page or named physical/Canvas route.
  • Minute 38: every goal has an action and checkpoint. If one third remain vague, revise one anonymous example together.
  • Protected trim: shorten the course-tool tour and partner check. Protect the route test, action/checkpoint, and fallback.
  • Missing page/device/account: use the Student Guide or paper now; do not require later recopying.
  • Absent/late: complete the test line and six goal fields; no setup scavenger hunt.
Collection and grade boundary

No separate Canvas submission. Review only the named private page or physical entry. If a completion record is needed, check the six fields. Never grade notebook type, supplies, handwriting, decoration, neatness, confidence level, career certainty, or private content.

Sources and technology

The goal sequence, silent Think, partner response, and specific-action model are adapted from teacher-created AVID materials. OneNote setup and recovery follow current Microsoft Class Notebook support guidance.
