1. Describe two requirements

Open your Find Your Future workbook to p. 36 and read how a graduation plan connects courses and graduation requirements. Check the Day 1 career guide for a cybersecurity career example.

In the margin beside p. 36, finish both thoughts:

  • To finish high school, my plan must include courses that meet graduation requirements, such as ___.
  • After high school, an information security analyst typically needs ___ and ___; other paths may differ.

You can use a different career from a reliable source if you know its preparation. This is an example to explain requirements, not a promise to follow one career.

2. Improve the flyer

Open your Canva draft from Day 3 and check the flyer scoring guide. Make one visible improvement to the safety advice, readability, event details, or privacy of the sign-up information.

3. Download and submit

In Canva, choose Share → Download, then download the final one-page flyer as PNG or PDF. Open the assignment below, upload that file, submit it, and check that Canvas shows your submission.
