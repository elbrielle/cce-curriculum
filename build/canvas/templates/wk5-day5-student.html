1st Six Weeks Capstone Submission: Today you turn in the complete 16-point Major 2 Capstone packet and log Entry 1 in your CCE Evidence Log.

Today you will

  • write a clear postsecondary goal statement;
  • design an original symbolic emblem representing your pathway goal;
  • complete your 1st Six Weeks Career Journey Update; and
  • assemble and submit the complete 4-part capstone portfolio.

1. Name Your Goal Before Designing

“My symbol represents [Symbol Concept] because after high school graduation I plan to [Postsecondary Goal: e.g., college, apprenticeship, military, career credential].”

2. Create One Original Symbol (15 Minutes)

Combine two simple geometric shapes or visual metaphors. Do not copy or trace commercial logos or school seals. Use paper, Canva, Adobe Express, SVG, or PNG.

3. Update My Career Journey (20 Minutes)

Open the 1st Six Weeks Update · Open bilingual language support

Place your Week 0 reflection beside your new update. Identify: (1) one concrete six-weeks project example, (2) one middle-to-high school transition step, and (3) one high school-to-postsecondary action.

4. Assemble and Submit the Capstone Packet (10 Minutes)

  • Part 1: Cybersecurity Bootcamp Plan
  • Part 2: Bootcamp Flyer Prototype
  • Part 3: Original Goal Symbol & Written Goal Sentence
  • Part 4: Career Journey Update with Two Transition Steps

Open the 16-point Capstone Rubric

Log Entry 1 (CCE Evidence Log): In your binder or digital folder, record:
  • Artifact: Career Journey Update
  • Transferable Skill: One skill demonstrated this six weeks
  • Evidence: Tangible action from your work
  • Revision / Growth: One fix or improvement made
  • Next Step: Immediate action item for 2nd Six Weeks

You are done when

  • all four capstone parts are attached and submitted;
  • your symbol is original and has a valid goal sentence;
  • the reflection cites a specific six-weeks learning experience;
  • both transition steps are defined; and
  • Entry 1 is completed in your CCE Evidence Log.
Absent or missing an earlier piece?

Complete the paper/digital version of the missing evidence. Use the recovery box if the Week 0 page is missing. No laser cut, live presentation, or gallery walk is required for the grade.

If your Evidence Log is missing, copy the five short phrases from the open Career Journey Update under Entry 1 hold in your CCE notebook or teacher-designated digital folder. Transfer the phrases later. Do not reconstruct or upload old work.
