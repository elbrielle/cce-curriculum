Week 5 - Day 5

Finish the 1st Six Weeks capstone

Create an original postsecondary symbol, update your career thinking, and submit one evidence packet.

Today's learning

Topic: Transition Planning

I can: describe actions that support the transition from middle school to high school and from high school to a postsecondary goal.

Show your learning: submit an original goal symbol, two transition steps, and a specific Career Journey update in the four-part capstone packet.

Today you will

  • Name the postsecondary goal your symbol represents.
  • Create one original goal symbol.
  • Update My Career Journey and submit the four-part packet.

1. Name the goal before designing

Finish this sentence first: My symbol represents _____ because after high school I want to _____.

2. Create one original symbol - 15 minutes

Combine two simple shapes or ideas. Do not trace a college, company, or military logo. Use paper, Canva, Adobe Express, SVG, or PNG. Submit the digital or paper design; a laser-cut copy is optional and does not affect the score.

3. Update My Career Journey - 20 minutes

Open the 1st Six Weeks Update Open bilingual language support

Put your Week 0 response beside it. Your interests may change. Use one specific example, one middle-to-high-school action, and one high-school-to-postsecondary action.

4. Check and submit the packet - 10 minutes

  • Bootcamp plan
  • Flyer prototype
  • Original symbol plus goal sentence
  • Career Journey update with two transition steps

Open the 16-point capstone rubric

Use 2 to 3 minutes for Entry 1. Keep your Career Journey Update open. In your CCE Six-Weeks Evidence Log, copy short phrases for:

  • Artifact or task: Career Journey Update
  • Transferable skill: one skill you used
  • Evidence: the visible action in your specific example
  • Revision or recovery: one change, fix, or catch-up move
  • Next step: the next action already on your update

Return the log to your CCE binder or teacher-designated digital folder. Do not submit the log or your old work again. It is not another grade.

You are done when

  • All four pieces are attached or turned in.
  • The symbol is original and has a goal sentence.
  • The reflection cites one specific six-weeks example.
  • The reflection includes both transition steps.
  • Entry 1 is stored in the Evidence Log, or the five short phrases are saved in an Entry 1 hold note.
Absent or missing an earlier piece?

Complete the paper/digital version of the missing evidence. Use the recovery box if the Week 0 page is missing. No laser cut, live presentation, or gallery walk is required for the grade.

If your Evidence Log is missing, copy the five short phrases from the open Career Journey Update under Entry 1 hold in your CCE notebook or teacher-designated digital folder. Transfer the phrases later. Do not reconstruct or upload old work.
