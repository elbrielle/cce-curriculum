Week 4 - Day 5

Show the logic, add a skill, and turn in the evidence

Give a one-minute lightning demo, complete Xello Add Skills, and finish the individual major-grade connection.

1. Give the one-minute lightning demo

  1. Name the scenario.
  2. Run or trace the three Button A steps and Button B FIXED.
  3. Explain why Step 1 belongs first.
  4. Name one support career and its common entry preparation.
  5. Name one transferable skill the team used.

The live demo is communication practice. The teacher scores the durable program evidence and each student's connection sheet.

2. Complete Xello Add Skills

  1. Open Xello through district SSO.
  2. Open About Me > Skills.
  3. Add or update at least one skill.
  4. Record one real example showing where you use it.

This is the required Grade 8 Xello task for Week 4. If your rostered account is missing, tell your teacher; do not create another account.

3. Finish the individual connection

Open the Xello Skill and Help Desk Connection

Open the 16-point major rubric

You are done when

  • Your team gave or scheduled the lightning demo.
  • Xello Add Skills is complete or your access issue is recorded.
  • Your team Program Evidence and your individual connection sheet are turned in.
Platform unavailable or absent?

Use the paper trace for program evidence and the written connection with a skill you already know. Xello completion moves to the next catch-up block. An absent student may give the one-minute explanation to the teacher or attach a written script.

Optional: H&L favorites

If all required work is complete, review or favorite IT support careers in H&L. This is not required evidence.
