1. Give the One-Minute Lightning Demo

  1. Name your scenario.
  2. Run or trace the three Button A steps and the Button B FIXED confirmation.
  3. Explain why Step 1 belongs first in the troubleshooting order.
  4. Name one IT support career and its common entry preparation.
  5. Name one transferable skill your team used.
Demo sentence model (45 seconds)
“Our scenario was [Scenario Name]. Button A advances through [steps] and Button B confirms FIXED. Step 1 came first because [reason]. A [Support Career] prepares through [certification/training]. We used [transferable skill] when we [team action].”

2. Complete Xello Add Skills

  1. Open Xello through district SSO.
  2. Go to About Me > Skills.
  3. Add or update at least one skill.
  4. Record one real example showing where you use that skill in life or school.

(This is the required Grade 7 Xello task for Week 4. If your rostered account is missing, tell your teacher.)
