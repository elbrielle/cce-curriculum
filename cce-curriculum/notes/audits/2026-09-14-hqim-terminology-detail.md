# HQIM Terminology Audit — CCE Grade 7 (Canvas + repo)

Scan date: 2026-09-14. Read-only audit. Pattern: `HQIM` / `H.Q.I.M` (case-insensitive).
Sources: Canvas dump `.tmp/canvas-dump-20260914/`, Google Docs `.tmp/docs-trusted-read/20260825-current/`,
repo `docs/`, `build/canvas/`, `build/worksheet_sources/`, `build/decks/`, `build/onenote/`, `build/google_docs/`,
`cce-curriculum/`, and PDFs under `docs/resources/`. Git worktrees under `.claude/worktrees/`, the built `site/`,
`public-site/dist/`, and dev scratch `tmp/canvas-previews/` were excluded as derived/duplicate copies.

## Headline finding: HQIM carries two different meanings

1. **1SW Wk2 (IT salary packet family)** — HQIM means *the localized salary figure from the app*. The worksheet
   field literally reads `Platform: H&L / Xello`, and the Wk2 Day 3 teacher guide says "H&L or Xello supplies the
   district HQIM localized figure." These are **class B**: the replacement is a phrasing decision
   ("Hats & Ladders or Xello", or simply "the app"), not a straight swap to H&L.
2. **Everywhere else in the curriculum (Wk1, Wk3, Wk4, Wk5-cyber, 2SW, 3SW, 4SW, the Safe-or-Spoofed deck)** —
   HQIM means **the printed *Find Your Future* (FYF) workbook**, always adjacent to an `FYF pp. NN` citation
   (e.g. `| District HQIM | FYF pp. 24-25 |`, "Required HQIM: one FYF workbook per student"). These are **class D**:
   replacing them with "Hats & Ladders" would be factually wrong; the correct student word is
   "your workbook" / "*Find Your Future*".

Only 7 occurrences are unambiguously **class A** (Hats & Ladders specifically).
