# Auditor findings (judgment, not regex)

Scope: 1SW Wk1 (teacher-facing "Week 2") and 1SW Wk2 ("Week 3"). Sources: `build/canvas/templates/wk1-*.html`, `wk2-*.html`, `build/canvas/build_wk1.py`, `build_wk2.py`. Report only; no edits made. Objective, TEKS, and DOL are injected on both pages by `lesson_contracts.py`, so that block is present and is not a finding.

## Fix first (both weeks)

1. **Every Student Guide ends with an outage-plus-absence block** ("A platform is unavailable or you were absent", "H&L unavailable or absent?", "Platform unavailable or absent?"). Ten pages. The outage half is teacher logic; the absence half mixes in catch-up scheduling ("during supervised catch-up", "paper does not replace the platform record", "Your teacher can verify Matchmaker in the Completion Standards report"). Rewrite each as "Absent? Start here" with two or three student actions, or delete and leave absence to the Facilitator Guide fallback section, which already exists on every teacher page.
2. **Gradebook language on student pages in Wk2 Day 2 and Day 5** ("submits all five pages as Minor 2", "Minor 2 evidence", "Finish the Minor 2 reflection", "through the Minor 2 route your teacher names"). Students need the packet name and the submit button, not the grade category. Twelve hits on Day 5 alone.
3. **Teacher decisions exposed as student choices in Wk2 Day 4** ("choose the available programming route", "Code.org route" vs "Supplied trace route", "CHECK ROUTE"). The teacher picks one before class. The student page should show one path; the alternate belongs in the Facilitator Guide, or the page gets two clearly labeled teacher-selected versions.
4. **Sign-in route drift**: Wk1 Day 1 and Day 5 student pages say "Open ClassLink > Xello". Week 0 moved H&L to "Sign in with Google" and the deck lint bans "classlink". Confirm the live Xello sign-in route [VERIFY] and make the student wording match whatever Week 0 students actually did.

## Student Guide load (Wk1)

- Day 1 step 4 packs five separate actions (Cluster Tour with two pauses, one Hat that fits and one that does not, Pathway Possibilities rating, rate three Hats, salary-recording rules). Split into WHAT YOU SEE / DO THIS / DONE WHEN steps with a screenshot each, or cut to the two that the DOL needs (two careers, two questions).
- Day 1 step 2 is two discussion prompts in one step, one of which ("rocket-platform decision") references FYF content the page never shows. Either embed the FYF page or move the discussion to the deck and Facilitator Guide.
- Day 5 "Today you will" has six bullets against the cap of four; the second paragraph ("This is not a test with right answers...") is good and should stay.
- Notebook use is appropriate on Day 1 (H&L Cluster Tour has nowhere to record notes) but the Facilitator Guide never says so. Add the one-line Notebook field: "Day 1 notes page: two career notes, two questions." Days 2 through 5 use worksheets or Xello; no notebook page needed, and none is asked for. Good.

Wk1 and Wk2 teacher templates both lack the Slides line in the header area (Wk1 has the links lower on the page; Wk2 has no deck link at all [VERIFY whether Wk2 decks exist]).
