## Rule for the semester and next-year audit

For each lesson, record the exact TEKS action, the FYF page or app task that teaches it, the existing place where a student shows learning, and the teacher move needed to make the connection explicit. Use the workbook activity and its response space when they serve the target. Add a new response artifact only when the target calls for evidence the workbook or app cannot hold. An alternate access format replaces the default surface; it never becomes a second submission.

Preserve crafted materials that do a distinct job. A packet may supply criteria, a model, an accessible planning route, or a short exit check without becoming another assignment. For Week 5, FYF owns the default plan, the existing packet makes the flyer criteria explicit, Canva is the design tool, and the one Canvas Major 2 assignment is the final submission. [Current §127.2(d)(3)(A) and (4)(F)](https://tea.texas.gov/laws-and-rules/sboe-rules-tac/sboe-tac-currently-effect/ch127a.pdf) require describing academic transition requirements and identifying workplace success traits, respectively; neither specifies a four-part portfolio. Compare that official wording with iCEV alignment and the actual FYF, H&L, and Xello activities before deciding that a larger portfolio is needed elsewhere. iCEV is an instructional source, not the legal text of the standards.

Before assigning labor-market research, teach the labels with one projected example: occupation title, geography, median versus starting pay, data year, projection years, and what the number cannot promise. Then provide a dated same-source comparison when the TEKS calls for comparing occupations. Optional live browsing should deepen a question after the core lesson; it should not be the entry barrier to completing the assignment.
