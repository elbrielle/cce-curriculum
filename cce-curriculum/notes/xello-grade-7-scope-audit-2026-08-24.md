# Xello Grade 7 Scope Audit — 2026-08-24

## Decision

This is a Grade 7 CCE course. A Xello task belongs in the required course spine only when at least one of these is true:

1. Bowie Middle School's live Xello Completion Standards table assigns the task to Grade 7; or
2. the original coordinator scope-and-sequence spreadsheet explicitly assigns the task.

A task outside those two sources may be used only as the smallest verified
prerequisite needed to launch one of the selected Grade 7/original tasks. It
must be labeled as prerequisite support, not as a Grade 7 completion standard.
The dependency must be checked in the student experience because a prerequisite
named in the educator resource panel may be an inline launch prompt rather than
a separate task.

The live Grade 8 reconciliation is not authority for this course. It documents how the drift happened.
