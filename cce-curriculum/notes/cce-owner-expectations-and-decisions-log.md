## VILS notebook-pilot feedback protocol

Use this after teachers have tried their notebook route at least once. Keep the conversation to five minutes and do not ask teachers to bring student work, names, grades, or access details.

1. **Route used:** OneNote / another digital notebook or folder / physical notebook or binder / mixed route.
2. **Layout used:** What sections or labels did students actually need?
3. **Retrieval test:** Could students find the page again the next class without rebuilding it or asking for a new link?
4. **Friction:** What took teacher time, failed to sync, required printing, or made students copy work twice?
5. **Next decision:** Keep the route, change one label or routine, or switch routes. Do not standardize the three-section pilot for everyone until the group has evidence from actual use.
