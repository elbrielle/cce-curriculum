# CFA Pilot Specification

The district has not yet established a confirmed CFA pattern for this course. Until the first aligned district assessment is available, CFA files in this repository are **pilot drafts only**. Do not invest in full Canvas production, secure-test packaging, answer-sheet design, or gradebook setup from this specification.

## Temporary Draft Pattern

Use this only when a pilot is needed for teacher review.

| Element | Temporary expectation |
|---|---|
| Time | About 20 minutes |
| Length | 8-12 items |
| Item mix | Mostly multiple choice, with one or two platform-supported STAAR-style item types and no more than one short constructed response |
| Evidence | Controlled stimulus included with the test; no required live platform login |
| Rigor | A purposeful mix of identification, data interpretation, comparison, and application |
| Student access | Plain language, visible directions, documented accommodations, and no scoring penalty for language mechanics unless meaning is unclear |
| Status | Unpublished pilot until compared with the district CFA and reviewed by teachers |

## Drafting Rules

- Start with the TEKS actually taught during the six weeks. Do not use the CFA to backfill an uncovered standard.
- Write a short assessment blueprint before writing items. Avoid repeating the same standard or skill under different wording.
- Keep salary type, geography, and data year consistent. Xello is the default licensed source for localized career data; BLS figures must be labeled as national medians when that is what they are.
- Put every fact needed to answer a question in the controlled stimulus or in instruction students have completed. Do not make H&L, Xello, or another live login a testing variable.
- Write plausible distractors tied to likely misconceptions. Avoid trick wording, double negatives, and answer choices that differ only in reading complexity.
- Keep TEKS codes, keys, scoring notes, and item rationales out of the student-facing test.
- Treat match tables, multi-select, inline choice, hot text, and other STAAR-style interactions as item formats, not automatic rigor. The thinking demand comes from the evidence and decision the student must make.
- Provide an equivalent accessible interaction when the assessment platform cannot make an item type usable with keyboard or assistive technology.

## Scoring and Review Gate

Do not assume a CFA is a major grade. The district grade structure remains 40% minor and 60% major with at least three minor and two major grades per six weeks, but the CFA's place in that structure is unresolved.

Before production:

1. Compare the pilot with the first district-provided CFA.
2. Confirm item types, tested TEKS, length, timing, security, accommodations, and reassessment rules.
3. Run teacher review for answer-key accuracy, ambiguity, and instructional alignment.
4. Pilot the assessment and conduct a basic item analysis before reusing it.
5. Build the Canvas assessment only after those decisions are stable.
