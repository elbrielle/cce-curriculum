Career Conversation Starters
These career conversation starters make it easy to bring meaningful, student-centered career
discussions into any classroom. Use them to spark ideas, build connections, and keep future-focused
thinking part of everyday learning.
High School Career Courses
(Help students start thinking ahead)

Building Blocks
(Connect what they already do to future careers)

What is a class you’ve heard about in high school
that sounds interesting to you?

What is something you do outside of school that
you’re really good at?

If you could take a “fun” class in high school, what
would it be and why?

What is an activity you enjoy so much that you lose
track of time?

Do you think you would rather take a hands-on
class or a more traditional class? Why?

What is something you’ve improved at over time?
How did you get better?

What is something you wish you could learn in
school that you don’t currently learn?

Think about a hobby you have—what skills do you
think it’s helping you build?

If you could design your perfect high school
schedule, what classes would be on it?

If someone watched you during your favorite
activity, what would they say you’re good at?

Work Style & Preferences
(Super important for long-term fit)

Real-World Connections
(Help them see careers in everyday life)

Do you like working alone or with a team? Why?

Think about your favorite app, what kind of jobs do
you think helped create it?

Do you prefer following directions or coming up
with your own ideas?

Who is someone you know that has a job you find
interesting? What do they do?

Would you rather have a job where every day is the
same or different?

What is a job that helps your community that
people might not always notice?

Do you like working with your hands, technology,
or ideas the most?

What kinds of jobs do you think are important in
your school?

What kind of environment helps you do your best
work?

What problem in the world would you like to help
solve someday?
