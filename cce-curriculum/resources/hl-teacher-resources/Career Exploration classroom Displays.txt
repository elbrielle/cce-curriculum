Career Exploration Classroom Displays
These classroom display ideas help students track career discoveries throughout the year in a
visible, motivating way. Use them to celebrate growth, spark reflection, and keep “what’s next”
conversations active all year long.

Display Option

Option 1: The Climbing
Wall

Key Details
Create a “Our Climbing Wall” bulletin board. Students write Hats sticky notes
and place them higher as they discover new careers. Color-coded prompts:
A new career I discovered,
A career I’d like to explore more,
A
career that surprised me.

🟣

🟡

🟢

Teacher tip: “The goal isn’t to reach the top first—it’s to keep climbing.”

Option 2: The Ladder of
Possibilities

Use a large ladder display with 6 rungs representing exploration stages:
Discover Yourself, Try on Hats, Explore Career Pathways, Build Powerskills,
Make High School Connections, Set Future Goals.
Focus: Add Hats (or pathways) to the rungs as units are completed.

Option 3: Career Hat
Rack

Option 4: Every
Climber’s Journey

Create a giant hat rack. Each time students discover a new career, they add
paper “hats” labeled with the career. By year’s end, the rack is full.
Celebrate: “Look at all the hats we’ve tried on this year!”

Give each student an individual mountain/ladder tracker. Students color in
steps as they complete: assessments, career clusters, pathways, Hats, and
reflection activities.
Focus: Celebrating progress, not competition.

Option 5: Summit Board

Create an “Our Journey to the Summit” bulletin board. Students post:
Favorite Hat of the Week, Something New I Learned, A Skill I Want to Develop,
A Hat I Want to Explore More.
Focus: Track discoveries, questions, and connections rather than grades.
