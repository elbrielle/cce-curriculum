Activities for Early Finishers

These early finisher activities give students meaningful ways to keep learning and exploring without
adding extra workload for teachers. With a mix of in-app and real-world options, students can extend
their thinking, make connections, and stay engaged in career exploration, even after they’ve completed
their main work.
