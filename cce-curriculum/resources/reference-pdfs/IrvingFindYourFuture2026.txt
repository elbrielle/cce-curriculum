Things like knowing how to calm a situation down, push through
something hard, or get a group of people on the same page. These

Powerskill: Design Thinking
Ever thought about coming up with the worst idea possible?
It might sound strange, but it can actually help you think of
great ideas! Design thinking is a process where you purposely
come up with the most ridiculous, silly, and impractical ideas
you can imagine. This technique helps you break free from
regular thinking and look at problems in a totally new way. By
focusing on the opposite of what you think might be a good
solution, you can discover ideas you never expected. This is
why some workplaces use this method: to find new ideas that
may have been hidden by “traditional” thinking.

a problem. The crazier, the better!
You’re not trying to be serious —
you’re trying to push the limits of
your imagination. Who knows?
One of your worst ideas might just
turn into something really cool.

Website Revamp
Have you ever used an app that was super confusing or a website
where you couldn’t find the right button? That is an example
of bad User Experience (UX)! UX is the overall feeling you
get when you interact with a website. Good UX means a
website is smooth, logical, and helpful, while bad UX leaves
you feeling frustrated, lost, or annoyed. A UX designer’s job
is to prevent that frustration by building websites that are
organized, clear, easy, and enjoyable to use.

ACTIVI

TY

Imagine you are a UX Designer, someone who makes websites easy and enjoyable
to use. A company has asked for your help because their website is confusing, and
customers are leaving before they can buy anything. When you visit the site, you quickly
see the problem…it is not organized and very difficult to use! Your job is to carefully explore the
site, identify what is not working, and redesign it so it is clear, simple, and helpful for users.

Step 1: Learn About UX
Bad UX:

Good UX:
• Easy Navigation: Menus are
clearly labeled (like “Home,”
“About,” or “Contact”) so you
always know where you are and
never get lost.
• Clear Buttons and Links:
Important buttons stand out,
and you know exactly what will
happen when you click them.
• Fast and Simple: You can
complete your goal (like buying
an item or finding a video) in
just a few easy steps.

• Confusing Navigation: Links are hidden,
menus don’t make sense, or there is no
clear way to get back to the home page.
• Cluttered Design: Too much text,
distracting colors, or annoying pop-ups
that distract you from what you are trying
to do.
• Unclear Instructions: Buttons with
confusing names or search bars that are
impossible to find.
• Broken or Frustrating Features: Links
that lead to error pages or pages that
take forever to load.

Work Ethic

Work ethic is about working hard and doing it well!
It means showing up on time, doing your best, being
responsible, and not giving up even when tough
situations come up. People with strong work ethic
take pride in what they do and try to do their best,
even when no one else is watching. No matter what
career you have, work ethic matters. Employers want
employees who care about their work and do it well.
Having a strong work ethic helps you reach your
goals, earn respect, and make a difference.

City council members are local leaders who help
make decisions for their community. They create
and vote on laws (called ordinances) that keep
towns safe, fair, and organized. They listen to

ACTIVI

A new town is being built, and as a city council member, you are responsible for helping
create the laws that will guide the community. Important decisions must be made about
safety, fairness, and how people will live and work together.

Step 1: Learn about the Role
City council members are elected leaders who help make important decisions for their town or city.
One of their main responsibilities is creating and voting on local laws, also called ordinances.
Here are some examples:
• Community Spaces: Can the new skatepark stay open until
10 PM, or does it have to close at sunset?
• Public Safety: What is the speed limit in front of the
middle school? Are electric scooters allowed on the
sidewalks?
• Business Rules: Where are food trucks allowed
to park, and what kind of permits do they need?
Council members don’t just sit in a room and guess
what people want. They hold public meetings to listen
to residents. Imagine a group of citizens complaining
about dangerous potholes, or students asking for a
new basketball court—the council has to listen, debate
the issues, and figure out how to solve them. When
creating laws, council members must think carefully.
A strong law should be:
• Clear so that people understand it
• Fair so that it applies equally to everyone
• Realistic so that can actually be enforced

Injured on the Trail

When someone is injured in the wilderness, help looks very different than it
does in a city. That’s where Search and Rescue (SAR) teams come in. SAR teams
respond to emergencies in remote places like mountains, forests, and deserts,
using skill, strategy, and teamwork to reach people in need. Within SAR
teams are specially trained medics called Wilderness First Responders
(WFRs). WFRs provide medical care in environments where hospitals are
far away. One of their main goals is to stabilize patients by keeping an
injury from getting worse and protecting the patient until they can reach
advanced care, such as treatment from doctors in a hospital.

Grow System Rescue

Hydroponic farms are changing how food is grown. Instead of soil,
plants grow in nutrient-rich water. This allows farms to grow fresh
vegetables year-round while using less water and space than traditional
farms. Hydroponics technicians help keep these farms running
smoothly. They check water systems, mix nutrients, adjust lights,
monitor plant health, and solve problems to help crops grow
strong and healthy.

Step 1: Learn about Hydroponics Systems
Hydroponic systems depend on a few key parts:
• Water Flow: Moves water and nutrients to plant roots
• Nutrients: Help plants grow strong and healthy
• Lights: Replace sunlight indoors
• Clean System: Prevents blockages and keeps plants safe
A good hydroponics system needs to:
• Move water evenly to all plants
• Deliver the right amount of nutrients
• Provide consistent light
• Keep plants growing strong and healthy

Name: Rowan Lindstrum, Engineer
Location: Green Valley Farms
Farm Overview:
• Crop: Tomatoes on a 50-acre farm.
• Problem: The farm is way too large for the farmer to check every plant by hand.
Pests are going unnoticed until the damage is already done.
• Weather: Sunny, warm, and windy.
Pest Challenges:
• Pests Found: Flea beetles, spider mites, and tomato hornworms.

the worst pest spots are.

problems, notice patterns, and think through different
solutions. Strong critical thinkers ask questions, look
closely at details, and use evidence to guide their choices.
This skill is important in almost every career.

Click Factor
Ads are everywhere, especially online. People are scrolling,
tapping, and deciding in seconds what to click. The best ads
don’t just grab attention, they get people to take the next step,
like clicking on a link or visiting a website. They do that by using
a Call to Action (CTA). A CTA is the part of an ad that tells the
audience what to do next. It could be short and punchy like
