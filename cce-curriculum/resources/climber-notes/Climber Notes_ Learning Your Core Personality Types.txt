## Slide 2
Have you ever wondered why some activities feel totally natural to you while others feel like a chore? Personality types can help explain that. Researchers have found that most people tend to fall into multiple personality types, and knowing yours can help you discover careers and activities that actually fit who you are.
You may already know more about yourself than you realize. Now you will discover how the six core personality types connect to your interests, strengths, and experiences.

## Slide 3
Review the Six Personality Types
Type
They Like To…
In Your Life Right Now
Doer
Work with their hands, tools, machines, or animals. Solve real, physical problems.
You're the one who actually builds the project instead of just planning it. You like P.E., shop class, or working with your hands outside of school.
Analyzer
Research, observe, and think through complex problems. Ask "why" and "how."
You find yourself digging deeper into topics that interest you. Science or math class clicks for you, or you love figuring out how things work.
Creator
Express ideas through art, music, writing, or performance. Work without a rigid structure.
You doodle in your notebook, write stories for fun, play an instrument, or feel most like yourself when you're making something.
Helper
Connect with, support, and teach other people. Make a difference in someone's day.
You're the friend people come to when something's wrong. You like group work, mentoring, or anything that lets you support someone else.
Persuader
Lead, influence, and motivate others. Take charge and make things happen.
You naturally step up in group projects. You like being in charge, starting things, or convincing people to get on board with your ideas.
Organizer
Work with data, numbers, and systems. Bring order and accuracy to everything they do.
Your notes are color-coded. You like knowing the rules, following a clear process, and getting things exactly right.
