## Slide 2  [1 image(s)]
Example 1: Prenatal Ultrasound (Fetal Development)
What sonographers look for:
Shape of the baby (head, body)
Position of fetus
Movement and growth
What to notice:
Rounded shapes
Brighter outlines (bones)
Darker areas (fluid)

## Slide 3  [2 image(s)]
Example 2: Abdominal Ultrasound (Kidneys, Liver, Gallbladder)
What sonographers look for:
Smooth vs. irregular shapes
Bright spots (may indicate stones
Size of organs
What to notice:
Organs have consistent shapes
Bright white spots can signal
problems
(like kidney stones)

## Slide 4  [1 image(s)]
Example 3: Vascular Ultrasound (Blood Vessels & Flow)
What sonographers look for:
Blockages in blood flow
Blood clots
Bulges in vessels (aneurysms)
What to notice:
Dark areas inside vessels (may be a clot)
Irregular walls or bright spots (could be plaque)
Widened vessels (possible aneurysm)
Color flow patterns smooth = normal, patchy = possible problem
