## Slide 2
Everyone has different things they need from a job to actually feel good about doing it. These aren't about being good at something or getting along with your coworkers. Those matter too, but they're pretty common to everyone. Work values are the things that are genuinely different from person to person. Figuring out what you need can help you spot careers that will fit your life and not just your skills.
Small choices can reveal big clues about what matters to you. See what your answers reveal about the kind of work environment where you would thrive.
