Emergency Plumbing Basics
Shut off the water to stop the flow
Block off the area to keep people safe
Coordinate with:
City officials
Police (for traffic and safety)
Utility teams
Make a plan before starting to everyone knows their job
Communicate clearly and calmly so the team can work quickly and safely
